Nationalist Academy is an independently operated educational and information initiative. It develops and shares learning resources, study tools, research-support information and public-interest educational content.

Nationalist Academy is operated as a sole proprietorship by Faiez Mehmood. It is not a separate incorporated legal person. The proprietor is responsible for the business and its activities.
