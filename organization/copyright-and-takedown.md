# Copyright, Intellectual Property and Takedown Policy

Nationalist Academy respects intellectual property rights and expects visitors and contributors to do the same.

## Our materials

Original articles, explanations, design assets, branding and software are protected by applicable intellectual property laws unless a different licence or rights notice is stated. Third-party material remains the property of its respective owner.

## Permitted references

You may link to public pages and quote limited portions for lawful purposes, subject to applicable law and proper attribution. Do not republish complete articles, paid materials, books, scans, question banks or substantial portions without permission.

## Report a concern

If you believe material on a Nationalist Academy-controlled page infringes your copyright, privacy or other rights, email **faiezbinhafeez@gmail.com** with:
1. Your name and a reliable reply address.
2. The exact URL of the material complained of.
3. Identification of the original work or right involved.
4. A concise explanation of the concern and your authority to make the request.
5. The action requested and any supporting evidence.

Do not include unnecessary identity documents or sensitive personal data.

## Review and action

We will review sufficiently detailed, good-faith notices and may temporarily restrict, correct or remove material where appropriate. We may request clarification or evidence. False or abusive notices may be disregarded. This process does not replace any statutory procedure or legal remedy.
