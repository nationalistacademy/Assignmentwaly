# Academic Integrity and Responsible Learning Policy

Nationalist Academy supports learning, understanding and independent academic work.

## Student responsibility

Students must:
- understand the question and prepare their own response;
- verify facts, calculations, quotations and references;
- cite sources honestly and follow their institution's rules;
- disclose assistance or AI use where required;
- avoid plagiarism, impersonation, fabricated data, fabricated citations and contract cheating.

## Tools are learning aids

Templates, editors, quizzes, outlines, examples and research-support tools help organise learning. They do not replace the student's own analysis, evidence or responsibility. A tool output should never be treated as automatically accurate or ready for submission.

## Research ethics

Do not invent participants, survey results, experiments, interviews, references or findings. Obtain required consent and institutional approval before collecting or publishing research data involving people.
