# Complaints, Corrections and Grievance Policy

We welcome good-faith reports about factual errors, accessibility barriers, privacy concerns, misuse, copyright issues and other problems involving Nationalist Academy-controlled content.

## How to contact us

Email **faiezbinhafeez@gmail.com**. Include:
- the page URL;
- a clear description of the issue;
- relevant evidence or source links;
- the correction or action you are requesting.

Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## Review

The proprietor or an authorised representative will review the report, may request clarification and will take proportionate action where appropriate. Depending on the issue, this may include correcting a statement, adding context, restricting content or removing material.

## Corrections

Material factual corrections should be made transparently where practical. We may retain a brief correction note so readers can understand what changed.

## Limitations

This channel is not an emergency service and does not replace statutory complaint bodies, courts, regulators or platform reporting tools.
