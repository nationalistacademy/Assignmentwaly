📖 How to Submit B.Ed Assignments (Step by Step)

  • Online Submission: Login to Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk) → Go to your course → Click "Add Submission" → Upload PDF → Save.
  • Offline Submission: Write neatly on assignment sheets → Attach assignment form → Submit at regional office or tutor's address.
  • Always keep a copy and take a screenshot as proof of submission.
  • Deadline tip: Submit at least 3 days before the due date to avoid last‑minute server or courier issues.

🎯 Exam Strategy for B.Ed Students (From a Tutor)

  • ✅ Start preparing at least 2 months before exams – revise one course per week.
  • ✅ Focus on past papers – AIOU repeats 50‑60% of questions.
  • ✅ For theory subjects (8601, 8602, 8605), write answers with headings, bullet points, and real classroom examples.
  • ✅ For teaching methods courses (6400, 6403, 6406), include lesson planning, learning objectives, and assessment techniques.
  • ✅ Never submit copied assignments – write in your own words to avoid plagiarism and secure higher marks.
  • ✅ Practice time management – allocate 10 minutes for MCQs, 30 minutes for short questions, and 80 minutes for long questions.
