How to select the correct revision material

  1. Open your CMS/enrolment record and copy the course code exactly.
  2. Check your programme and semester from the official record.
  3. Search the course code in AIOU's official soft-book portal.
  4. Compare any revision file with the book's units and learning outcomes.
  5. Use past papers to understand question styles, not to predict the next paper.

Frequently asked questions

Are all 2026 programme guess papers available here?

No. This directory does not claim complete coverage. Use a course-specific resource only when its course code, programme and semester are clearly identified.

Why were generic download buttons removed?

A generic link that points to an unrelated or placeholder page can mislead students. A download should be shown only when the actual file or course page has been verified.

Can I rely only on expected questions?

No. Study the complete syllabus and use expected questions as optional practice. An exam may assess any relevant learning outcome.

Where can I confirm my current programme courses?

Check your official CMS enrolment record and the current AIOU prospectus.

Study smart: A course-code match and a correct textbook are more valuable than a generic “all programmes” download link. Keep your preparation grounded in the material assigned to your own semester.
