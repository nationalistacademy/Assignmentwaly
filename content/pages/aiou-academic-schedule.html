Who sees Assignmentwaly?

Assignmentwaly publishes independent study guidance for Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU) students in Pakistan. Our readers are mainly distance-learning students preparing for assignments, guess papers, exams and admissions. Because of this focused audience, we prefer advertising that genuinely helps students rather than generic offers.

Important: Assignmentwaly is an independent website. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Allama Iqbal Open University. Advertisers must never imply an official AIOU partnership, endorsement or accreditation.

Suitable advertising topics

We are most likely to consider offers that are useful and appropriate for students and educators, such as:

  • Educational books, stationery and learning materials.
  • Online courses, tutoring and academic skills resources.
  • Student productivity, accessibility and study tools.
  • Scholarship and career-information services, where claims can be verified.

Offers outside education may still be considered, but only if they are clearly relevant to students and do not conflict with our editorial standards.

How to send an enquiry

Please follow these steps so your proposal can be reviewed properly:

  1. Email your organisation name, website and contact details.
  2. Describe the product or service and explain why it is relevant to AIOU learners.
  3. State whether you are asking about a banner, sponsored article or another collaboration.
  4. Include any supporting information needed to review your claims and proposal.
  5. Wait for a written response confirming whether the proposal can be considered.
Helpful tip: Proposals that clearly state the audience, placement type, duration and budget are reviewed faster than general "please advertise with you" messages.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do you have fixed advertising packages?

A: No fixed rates or packages are published here. Any offer would be discussed individually and confirmed in writing.

Q: Can you guarantee impressions, clicks or sales?

A: No. Traffic and campaign outcomes vary, and Assignmentwaly does not guarantee impressions, clicks, leads or sales.

Q: Can I submit a sponsored article?

A: You may send an enquiry with the proposed topic, draft or outline and supporting sources. Publication is subject to editorial review and disclosure requirements.

Q: Does advertising mean an official AIOU endorsement?

A: No. Assignmentwaly is an independent website and does not imply endorsement by AIOU.

Q: What information should I include in my first email?

A: Include your organisation name, website, contact details, the product or service, why it is relevant to AIOU learners, the placement type you want, and any supporting documents. This helps us review your proposal quickly.

Q: How long does a review take?

A: Review time depends on the completeness of your proposal and current site capacity. We reply in writing once a decision is reached. No timeline is guaranteed.

Thank you: We appreciate your interest in working with Assignmentwaly. Our goal is to keep the site useful, trustworthy and student-focused. Proposals that respect these principles are always welcome.
