📝 Assignment Submission Guidelines

  • Assignments must be handwritten (except for computer-related courses where typed is allowed).
  • Submit assignments to your respective tutor or regional office before the deadline.
  • Late submissions are accepted with a fine but may affect result timing.
  • Keep a copy of submitted assignments for reference.

📌 How to Check Your Personalized Schedule?

  1. Visit LMS Portal (lms.aiou.edu.pk) and login with your registration number.
  2. Go to "My Courses" to see assignment submission deadlines for each subject.
  3. Check "Exam Schedule" section for date sheet and center details.
  4. Contact your regional office for any confusion.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ When will AIOU announce the next admission schedule?

Spring admissions are announced in January, Autumn in July. Keep checking the official AIOU website.

❓ Can I submit assignments after the deadline?

Yes, but with a late fee. However, it may delay your result and exam eligibility.

❓ Where can I download my AIOU date sheet?

Date sheets are available on aiou.edu.pk under "Examinations" section and on the LMS portal.

❓ How many assignments per subject in a semester?

Two assignments per course (Assignment 1 and Assignment 2) for most programs.

❓ What happens if I miss the exam form submission?

You cannot appear in exams for that semester. You will have to apply for the next semester.
