AIOU Academic Schedule 2026: Key Dates

Quick answer: AIOU follows Spring and Autumn semesters, but the academic schedule differs by programme and study mode. Use the university's current calendar for the normal semester pattern, then confirm exact deadlines from the latest dated notice for your programme before taking action.

Schedule reminder: The dates below are the general schedule published by AIOU, not a guarantee that every programme follows identical dates in every semester. Special programmes, revised notices, extensions and Phase-II admissions can have separate timelines.
Definition — AIOU academic schedule: The academic schedule is the university's calendar of semester activities, including admissions, mailing or book dispatch, study period, examinations and result announcements. ODL and face-to-face (F2F) programmes may follow different activity windows.

How the AIOU academic schedule works

Allama Iqbal Open University generally organises its academic year around two semesters: Spring and Autumn. Students use the semester calendar to plan admission, course registration, assignment preparation, workshops, examinations and result follow-up.

The calendar is a planning guide. It should not replace a programme-specific admission advertisement, date sheet, assignment schedule or official notification. If a new AIOU notice changes a date, follow the latest notice rather than an older blog post or a screenshot shared in a WhatsApp group.

How to check your programme's AIOU schedule

  1. Identify your programme. Confirm whether you are enrolled in Matric, FA, Associate Degree, BS, B.Ed, postgraduate or a research programme.
  2. Confirm the study mode. Check whether your programme is ODL or face-to-face. Do not use an ODL date for a F2F programme without confirmation.
  3. Open the official calendar. Compare the semester activity with the current AIOU academic calendar and admission advertisement.
  4. Check your student portal. Use your official CMS or the relevant AIOU service to confirm personal enrolment, course and examination information.
  5. Save the latest notice. Keep a copy or screenshot showing the notice title and date so you can distinguish it from older announcements.
  6. Recheck before payment or travel. Verify the latest deadline, fee challan, examination centre or workshop details before acting.

Common mistakes when reading the AIOU academic schedule

  • Mixing ODL and F2F dates: These programme modes can have different activity windows.
  • Treating a general calendar as a personal date sheet: Examination dates and centres must be checked through the official examination service.
  • Using an old semester notice: A Spring notice cannot automatically confirm an Autumn deadline.
  • Assuming every programme is open: Admission availability depends on the programme advertisement and eligibility rules.
  • Relying on forwarded screenshots: Open the original AIOU notice and check its date before making a decision.
  • Ignoring extensions or revised notices: A later official announcement may replace an earlier deadline.

Frequently asked questions about the AIOU academic schedule

How many semesters does AIOU offer in a year?

AIOU generally follows two semesters: Spring and Autumn. The exact programmes offered in each semester depend on the current university prospectus and admission advertisement.

Are AIOU ODL and face-to-face dates the same?

No. The university publishes separate ODL and face-to-face activity patterns. Always select the calendar that matches your programme's study mode.

Does the academic calendar show my assignment last date?

Not necessarily. The general academic calendar gives semester activity windows. Assignment due dates and submission methods should be confirmed from the current programme instructions, course information or official LMS.

Where can I check the AIOU examination date sheet?

Use the official AIOU Examination Department and the university's student services. Check your own programme and examination session rather than relying on a general semester range.

Can AIOU change a published semester date?

Yes. The university may issue revised schedules, extensions or programme-specific notices. Follow the latest official announcement that applies to your programme.

Is Assignmentwaly an official AIOU website?

No. Assignmentwaly is an independent educational resource. Official AIOU notices, portals and university staff are the authority for admissions, fees, examination arrangements and student records.
