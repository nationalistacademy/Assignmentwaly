📝 How to Apply Online – Step by Step

  1. Visit the official AIOU admission portal: admissions.aiou.edu.pk
  2. Create an account using your CNIC/B-Form number and email.
  3. Select your program (FA, I.Com, BA, BS, etc.) and fill the online form.
  4. Upload scanned documents (photograph, CNIC, previous degrees).
  5. Pay admission fee via 1Bill, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank challan.
  6. Submit the form and print the admission slip for future reference.

💰 Fee Structure & Payment Methods

AIOU fees vary by program. For Intermediate (FA/I.Com) the approximate fee per semester is PKR 4,500–6,000. You can pay via ATM, mobile banking, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or any HBL/UBL branch. After payment, upload the proof on the admission portal.

⚠️ Note: Late fee submission results in a fine of PKR 500–1000. Always keep the payment receipt safe.

🎓 Required Documents for Admission

  • CNIC / B-Form (for minors) – copy
  • Recent passport-size photograph (with blue background)
  • Previous educational certificates (Matric for intermediate, Intermediate for bachelor)
  • Domicile certificate (optional but recommended)
  • Admission processing fee challan receipt

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ When will AIOU announce admission for 2026? Spring admissions usually start in January, Autumn in July. Check the official website or this blog for updates.
❓ Can I apply online without a computer? Yes, you can apply using your smartphone via the AIOU admission portal – it is mobile responsive.
❓ How to get my admission confirmation? After fee verification, AIOU sends an SMS and admission slip on the portal. You can also check via the "Track Admission" option.
❓ What is the AIOU helpdesk number for assignments? For assignment/tutor issues, call 051-111-112-468 and select the relevant department, or email helpdesk@aiou.edu.pk.
