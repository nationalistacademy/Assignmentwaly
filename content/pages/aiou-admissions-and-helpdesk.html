How to apply for AIOU admission online

  1. Open the official portal. Visit admissions.aiou.edu.pk. Check that the address is on the official AIOU domain.
  2. Read the prospectus first. Confirm that you meet the age, education, subject and other eligibility conditions for your selected programme.
  3. Create or access your account. Enter your own details carefully. Use an active mobile number and email address that you can access.
  4. Select the correct programme and mode. Check the programme name, code, study mode and semester before completing the form.
  5. Complete the application profile. Enter your name, CNIC/B-Form and educational information exactly as required by the portal and supporting documents.
  6. Upload the required documents. Use clear, readable scans or photographs in the file format and size accepted by the portal.
  7. Review the fee challan. Confirm the amount and payment instructions generated for your own application. Do not use an old challan or a payment number shared by an unknown person.
  8. Submit and save proof. Keep the application number, submitted form and payment receipt. Recheck the portal for objections or status updates.
Payment safety: Only pay through the payment methods and account details shown by AIOU's official admission system or current prospectus. Never send money to a personal Easypaisa/JazzCash number or an unofficial agent claiming to guarantee admission.

Documents to prepare before applying

The exact document checklist varies by programme. Depending on your application, you may be asked for:

  • CNIC or B-Form details and supporting copy
  • Recent photograph, if requested by the portal
  • Previous educational certificates and marks sheets
  • Equivalence or other supporting documents, where applicable
  • Programme-specific documents mentioned in the prospectus or admission notice

Do not assume that every applicant needs a domicile certificate or the same photograph background. Follow the instructions for your selected programme and the upload fields shown in the official portal.

AIOU admission fee and payment guidance

There is no single fee that applies to all AIOU students. The payable amount depends on the programme, course load, study mode and applicable semester charges. Use the Autumn 2026 prospectus and the fee challan generated for your application to confirm the current amount.

Before paying, check these details:

  • Applicant name and application or challan reference
  • Programme and semester shown on the challan
  • Total payable amount and due date
  • Payment channel and instructions currently provided by AIOU

Keep the original payment receipt or a clear digital copy. If a payment is deducted but the portal does not update, use the official Helpdesk and attach the transaction evidence. Do not pay again until the payment status has been checked.

Frequently asked questions about AIOU admission

Q: Is AIOU Autumn 2026 admission still open?

A: The standard admission windows in AIOU's published calendar have passed by 4 November 2026. However, AIOU lists programme-specific notices and Phase-II information separately. Check the current admission notice and portal for your exact programme before assuming it is closed or open.

Q: Where can I download the AIOU Autumn 2026 prospectus?

A: Open the official AIOU Prospectus page and select the document for your programme group.

Q: How can I apply for a fresh AIOU admission?

A: Use the official Online Admission System. Read the prospectus, check eligibility, complete the form and pay only through the instructions generated by AIOU.

Q: How do I contact AIOU about an admission objection?

A: Use the official Helpdesk Portal at support.aiou.edu.pk, or email the admission department at adms@aiou.edu.pk. Include your application reference and a clear explanation; avoid sharing passwords or one-time codes.

Q: Does AIOU have one fixed fee for all programmes?

A: No. Fees vary by programme and semester. Confirm the amount in the relevant prospectus and the challan generated for your application.

Q: Where can I find AIOU assignment and workshop information?

A: Use AIOU's official Guidance & Help page for assignment schedules, tutor information, workshops and student guidance.
