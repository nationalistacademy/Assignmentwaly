Advertise With Assignmentwaly

Quick overview: Assignmentwaly may consider relevant advertising, educational partnerships and sponsored-content enquiries. All proposals are reviewed first; no audience size, impression volume, placement date or campaign result is guaranteed.

Availability: This page does not publish fixed advertising rates or promise specific placements. Any proposal depends on current site capacity, content relevance and written agreement.

Suitable advertising topics

We are most likely to consider offers that are useful and appropriate for students and educators, such as:

  • Educational books, stationery and learning materials.
  • Online courses, tutoring and academic skills resources.
  • Student productivity, accessibility and study tools.
  • Scholarship and career-information services, where claims can be verified.

Content and advertising standards

  • Sponsored material must be clearly identified as sponsored.
  • Claims about fees, outcomes, accreditation or results must be accurate and supported.
  • We do not accept misleading offers, copied material or prohibited content.
  • Links and advertisements must not impersonate AIOU or suggest an official university partnership.
  • Acceptance of an enquiry does not guarantee publication or a particular result.

How to send an enquiry

  1. Email your organisation name, website and contact details.
  2. Describe the product or service and explain why it is relevant to AIOU learners.
  3. State whether you are asking about a banner, sponsored article or another collaboration.
  4. Include any supporting information needed to review your claims and proposal.
  5. Wait for a written response confirming whether the proposal can be considered.
