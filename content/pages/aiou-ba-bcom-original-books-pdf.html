📖 How to Download and Use These AIOU Original Books

  • Click the “PDF” link next to your course code. The file will open in a new tab or download directly.
  • Save the file to your phone, tablet, or computer. Create a folder named “AIOU BA BCom Books”.
  • Use a good PDF reader (Adobe Acrobat, Xodo, or Google Drive) for highlighting and bookmarking.
  • Follow the chapter sequence – do not skip any unit, as exams cover the full syllabus.
  • Solve all exercise questions – they are often repeated in assignments and final exams.

Are these the same books that AIOU sends by courier?

What is the difference between BA and B.Com?

BA (Bachelor of Arts) focuses on humanities subjects like Economics, Sociology, History, Psychology, etc. B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce) focuses on accounting, business math, banking, and management. Both are 2‑year or 4‑year programs depending on the scheme.

Can I print these PDFs?
