AIOU Assignments 2026: Questions & Submission

Quick answer: AIOU students should download the assignment questions for their own semester and course code, read the instructions attached to those questions, and submit work using the method assigned to their programme. Some programmes submit through AAGHI LMS, while SSC/HSSC students submit to their assigned tutors; never assume one method applies to every student.

Academic integrity: Use solved examples and study notes to understand a topic, plan your answer and check your concepts. Do not submit copied answers, another student's work or ghostwritten material as your own. AIOU assignment instructions require students to write in their own words and avoid reproducing source material.
Definition — AIOU assignment: An assignment is a course assessment task that asks a student to answer set questions during the semester. Assignment marks may contribute to the course result and, depending on programme rules, may be part of the continuous assessment required for examination eligibility.

Where to download AIOU assignment question papers

The safest starting point is AIOU's official Assignments / Question Papers page. It lists assignment papers by semester and programme group. Select the correct semester first, then choose the relevant programme category and confirm the course code printed on your question paper.

How to identify the correct assignment for your course

  1. Check your student record. Open your official CMS or enrolment information and note your programme, semester and registered course codes.
  2. Choose the same semester. Do not use a Spring paper for an Autumn course unless AIOU specifically instructs you to do so.
  3. Match the complete course code. Compare the code on the official question paper with your enrolled course. Similar course titles can have different codes.
  4. Read the attached instructions. Check assignment number, total marks, question count, submission format and due date.
  5. Save the official paper. Keep the downloaded question paper so you can refer to its exact wording while preparing your answer.

AIOU assignment submission: LMS or tutor?

Submission method depends on the programme category and the current instructions. AIOU's FAQ states that students enrolled in BS, B.Ed, MA/MSc ODL and equivalent programmes submit assignments through AAGHI LMS, while SSC/HSSC students submit assignments to their tutors. The university may publish programme-specific instructions or exceptions, so check the latest notice for your own course.

How to prepare an original AIOU assignment

A good assignment answers the question directly, explains the key ideas and shows that you understand the course material. It should not be a collection of copied paragraphs. Use the official book and relevant readings to learn the topic, then organise the explanation in your own words.

  1. Understand the command word. “Define” needs a clear meaning; “compare” needs similarities and differences; “discuss” needs a reasoned explanation; “evaluate” needs evidence and a judgement.
  2. Break the question into parts. Underline each instruction so you do not miss a second or third part of the question.
  3. Consult the course book. Read the relevant unit and note the concepts, examples and terms that directly answer the question.
  4. Make a short outline. Arrange your answer into an introduction, relevant headings, explanation, examples and a brief conclusion where suitable.
  5. Write in your own words. Explain what you understood. If you use an outside source, record its details and cite it in the required format.
  6. Review before submission. Check every part of the question, spelling, readability, page order and file format.

Formatting and file preparation

For programmes that require digital upload, prepare a readable document and follow the format stated in the assignment instructions. AIOU's published guidance for many ODL degree programmes specifies typed Word or PDF submissions. Some assignment instructions include a specific exception for Urdu or Arabic answers, including a scanned handwritten PDF with a file-size limit. That exception should only be used when it appears in the instructions for your course and semester.

  • Use a clear, readable font and consistent headings.
  • Keep question numbers in the same order as the official paper.
  • Check that diagrams, tables and Urdu text remain readable after PDF export.
  • Open the final PDF before uploading; confirm that pages are not blank or rotated.
  • Use a sensible file name with your course code and assignment number, without exposing unnecessary personal data.

Assignment deadlines and late submission

There is no single assignment deadline that applies to every AIOU course. The due date may depend on semester, programme, course and the assignment schedule. Check the official assignment schedule and the due date shown with your course instructions. For LMS submissions, do not wait until the final minutes: sign in early, upload the correct file and confirm that the portal records the submission.

Keep proof: Save the LMS submission confirmation or, where a tutor-directed postal submission is required, keep the courier or registry receipt. If marks are missing later, AIOU's support guidance may ask for proof of timely submission.

How to check whether an assignment was uploaded

  1. Sign in to the official AAGHI LMS if your programme uses online submission.
  2. Open the correct course and assignment activity.
  3. Check the submission status and the file name shown on screen.
  4. Save the confirmation or take a private record for your own reference.
  5. If the assignment is not visible or cannot be uploaded, contact the relevant AIOU regional campus or support channel before the deadline where possible.

What to do if assignment marks are missing

First check the course result and confirm that the assignment was submitted for the correct course and semester. If you submitted on time and have proof, contact your concerned regional office and follow AIOU's process for the inclusion of assignment marks. Keep your course code, semester, submission date and proof ready. Do not post your roll number, registration details or private documents publicly in comments.

Official support references: AIOU Helpdesk and its guidance on inclusion of assignment marks.

Frequently asked questions about AIOU assignments

Where can I download AIOU assignment papers for Autumn 2026?

Open the official AIOU Assignments (QP) page, select Autumn 2026 and choose the correct programme group. Confirm the course code before using the paper.

Do all AIOU students upload assignments on AAGHI LMS?

No. AIOU's FAQ says SSC/HSSC assignments are submitted to assigned tutors, while BS/B.Ed/MA/MSc ODL and equivalent programmes use AAGHI LMS. Follow your programme's current instructions.

Can I upload handwritten assignments as a PDF?

Many ODL degree instructions require typed Word or PDF files and do not accept scanned handwritten work. Some course instructions provide a limited Urdu/Arabic exception. Check the exact instructions for your course and semester.

What should I do if my course is not visible in AAGHI LMS?

AIOU says courses are updated after admission confirmation. Check your admission status and contact the concerned regional campus or official helpdesk if the course remains unavailable.

Can I copy a solved assignment from a website?

No. Use examples for learning and prepare your own response. AIOU instructions emphasise original analysis and warn against reproducing material or submitting another person's work.

What if I submitted an assignment but its marks are missing?

Keep proof of submission and contact your concerned regional office. Follow AIOU's official procedure for checking or requesting inclusion of missing assignment marks.

Final reminder: The correct course code, semester, due date and submission route matter more than downloading a file quickly. Check AIOU's official instructions, prepare your own work and keep proof of submission.
