Ways to collaborate with Assignmentwaly

Assignmentwaly publishes study guidance and educational resources for AIOU students. We may consider relevant contributions such as:

  • Clear explanations of AIOU course topics and study processes.
  • Corrections to outdated dates, course information or broken resource links.
  • Original study notes and learning resources with appropriate references.
  • Suggestions that improve accessibility, readability and student support.

Editorial contribution standards

  • Submit original work and identify any sources used.
  • Do not copy copyrighted books, paid notes or another website's content.
  • Verify course codes and semester information against official AIOU material.
  • Do not promise grades, pass percentages or guaranteed exam results.

How to contact us

  1. Write a short email explaining your area of interest.
  2. Include relevant writing samples or a brief description of your experience, if available.
  3. State whether you are suggesting a correction, offering content or asking about a future opening.
  4. Wait for a reply; response times may vary and sending an email does not guarantee selection.
