About Assignmentwaly

Quick overview: Assignmentwaly is an independent educational website for students looking for AIOU study guidance, assignment resources, original-book links, guess papers and academic information.

Independent resource: Assignmentwaly is not an official website of Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU). Official university notices, prospectuses and student records take priority over information published here.

What Assignmentwaly publishes

The website is organised around common questions students face during distance learning. Depending on the programme and semester, pages may include:

  • Assignment guidance and course-code reference material.
  • Links to AIOU original books and study resources.
  • Guess-paper and past-paper revision resources.
  • Admission, academic calendar and assignment-deadline guidance.
  • Explanations of student processes such as checking notices and preparing submissions.

How to use the resources

  1. Confirm your programme, semester and course code from your AIOU CMS or official student record.
  2. Open the relevant Assignmentwaly page and check its update date and archive notice.
  3. Compare dates, eligibility and submission instructions with the latest official AIOU notice.
  4. Use examples to understand the topic, then prepare your own work and cite sources appropriately.

Our approach to accuracy

Educational schedules and course requirements can change. We aim to make information easier to understand, but a page may need updating after a new AIOU notification. If you find a broken link, incorrect course code or outdated detail, please send the page URL and a short explanation so it can be reviewed.
