📝 Step-by-Step Guide to Recover Your LMS ID

Neeche diye gaye asaan steps ko follow karein:

  • AIOU LMS Portal par jayen (lms.aiou.edu.pk).
  • "Forgot Password" ke option par click karen.
  • Apni Registration ID (Jo aapki admission slip par hoti hai) enter karein.
  • Apni Registered Email ya Contact Number select karein.
  • Aapke Email ya Mobile Number par ek OTP (One Time Password) aayega.
  • Wo OTP wahan enter karein.
  • Mubarak ho! Aapko aapki LMS ID aur Naya Password mil jayega.
Pro Tip: Apni nayi LMS ID aur Password ko kisi safe jagah par likh lein. Agar OTP email par na aaye, to apna "Spam/Junk" folder zaroor check karein. Agar phir bhi masla ho, to apne nearest AIOU Regional Office se rabta karein.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

🔹 Agar mujhe apni Registration ID yaad nahi hai to kya karun?
Aap apni AIOU admission fee ki challan slip, books ke parcel, ya kisi bhi official result card par apni Registration ID dekh sakte hain. Yeh 9 ya 10 digits ki alphanumeric code hoti hai (jaise 21PGA12345).
🔹 OTP kahan aayega?
OTP aapke us email address ya mobile number par aayega jo aapne AIOU admission ke waqt diya tha. Agar aapne apna number/email change kiya hai, to aapko pehle CMS portal se wo update karwana hoga.
🔹 Kya old students online assignment upload kar sakte hain?
Haan, BA/B.Com ke tamam naye aur purane students ab assignments sirf AAGHI LMS portal par hi upload kar sakte hain. Physical ya postal assignments accept nahi honge.
