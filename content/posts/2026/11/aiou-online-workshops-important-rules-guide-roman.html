AIOU Online Workshops ke Ahem Usool (BS, B.Ed, BBA, ADP)

Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU) ke BS, B.Ed, BBA, BS 2.5 (MA), PGD aur ADP ke tamam semesters ke students mutawajjah hon! Workshops (Online Classes) ke chand usool achi tarah samajh lein.
  • Har kitab ki sirf 6 din online workshop hoti hai (rozana 2 ghante aur time din 1 baje se raat 9 ke darmiyan kabhi bhi).
  • Farz karein agar kisi student ki class 1 baje shuru hogi toh 3 baje automatic khatam aur agar 3 baje shuru hogi toh 5 baje automatic khatam ho jayegi.
  • Workshops ke taqreeban 10 se 15 groups (batches) hote hain, kuch students ki pehle groups mein hoti hain aur kuch ki baad mein.
  • Har student ka workshop ka schedule mukhtalif hota hai, yeh kabhi mat kahein ke falan student ki workshop ho rahi hain meri kyun nahi ho rahi, har student ne apna schedule check karna hota hai.
  • Har class ka link mukhtalif hota hai, har class LMS Portal par ja kar alag se join karni hoti hai.
  • Jab workshop join karte hain toh hazri automatic lag jati hai, attendance bolni nahi hoti.
  • Poore 2 ghante class join rakhna zaroori nahi, agar aap ke paas time nahi toh aadhe ghante ya 1 ghante baad class chor sakte hain.
  • Workshop parhai ke liye bohat zaroori hain is liye kabhi bhi workshop mat chorein.
  • Workshop ke doran teacher add nahi karte, yeh mukammal auto system hota hai aur teacher hazri bhi nahi lagate.
  • Workshop ke teachers aarzi (temporary) hote hain, inka aap ki assignments, quiz ya paper ke sath koi taluq nahi, na hi woh aap ki koi bhi cheez check karenge.
  • Jis hafte jis bhi subject ki workshop hogi usi hafte ke aakhir par Jumma, Hafta, Itwar ko usi subject ka 30 Number ka Online Quiz hoga.
⚠️ Ahem Note: Quiz mein shirkat na karne ki soorat mein aap ke 30 numbers zaya ho sakte hain, is liye workshop ke sath sath quiz ka schedule bhi lazmi check karein.

Kya aap ko Assignments ya Quiz ki tayari mein madad chahiye?

Hum AIOU ki tamam solved assignments, guess papers aur quiz ki tayari mein mukammal rehnumai faraham karte hain.
