📌 What is Wrong Dispatch of Books? (AIOU System Glitch)

Wrong Dispatch ka matlab hai ke mailing post office ya central packaging cell ki ghalti ki wajah se aapko ghalat course materials allot ho jayein. Iski basic examples darj-zail hain:

  • ✅ Incorrect Course Codes: Enrolled course codes ki jagah dusre codes ki guide books receive hona.
  • ✅ Semester Discrepancy: Semester 1 ke dauran semester 2 ya 3 ki books parcel me aana.
  • ✅ Medium Mismatch: Enrolled English medium subject ki books Urdu language version me dispatch hona.
  • ✅ Duplicate Copies: Aik hi subject ki do/teen copies parcel me hona aor dusri essential books ka missing hona.
  • ✅ Program Shift Issue: Program switch (e.g. BA to BS 4-Year) karne par purane system ki books parcel dispatch hona.
Application ki Aakhri Tareeq: Ghalat parcel receive hone ke 30 din ke andar replacement application process rawana karein. Late requests par process charge lagta hai ya direct reject ho sakti hain.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Guide to Replace Wrong Books

  1. Download Application Form: Upar diye gaye central green button par click kar ke wrong dispatch form download karein aor print out lein.
  2. Enter Student Credentials:
    • Apna full name, CNIC, Mobile Number, aor Student Registration ID/Enrollment Number enter karein.
    • Academic Year, program detail aur current Semester mention karein.
    • Ghalat kitabon ki tafseel: Receive ki gayi kitabon ke codes aor titles likhein.
    • Sahi kitabon ki tafseel: Jo kitabein aapko milni chahiye thin (LMS status se dekh kar course code aor titles likhein).
  3. Staple Required Evidence Documents:
    • Attested copy of CNIC or B-Form.
    • Copy of current admission fee paid Challan slip.
    • LMS Profile Details Print: Enrollment details aur subjects verification ka screen capture.
    • Receive ki gayi ghalat kitabon ke cover pages ki photos.
  4. Fee Structure (Fault Assessment):
    • AIOU/Courier fault: Bilkul Muft (Rs. 0).
    • Student fault (Change of program delay ya incorrect address shift): processing fee bank me pay karni ho gi (Details niche tabular fee block me dekhein).
  5. Dispatch to Mailing Section:
    • Apni complete raw file envelop me seal kar ke official postal dispatch address par TCS/Pakistan Post Registry rawana karein: Mailing Section, Directorate of Admissions and Mailing, Block No. 28, AIOU Main Campus, H-8, Islamabad – 44000.
Pro Student Tip (Return Policy): Ghalat dispatched kitabon par pencil ya pen se kuch mat likhein aor na hi binding kharab karein. AIOU policy ke mutabiq aapko wo kitabein original condition me return karni par sakti hain taake new copies receive ho sakein!
