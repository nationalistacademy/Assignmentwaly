Many students download solved assignments and copy them word-for-word, which often leads to poor marks or rejection due to plagiarism. Here are the professional rules you must follow for the Spring 2026 semester:

  • Mandatory Typing Rule: Unlike Matric and FA programs, B.Ed assignments cannot be handwritten. All B.Ed (1.5, 2.5, 4 years) assignments must be typed in MS Word (using English, or Urdu InPage for elective languages) and exported to PDF format.

📖 Complete Guide to Submitting Solved Assignments on Aaghi LMS

Preparing the assignments is only half the battle. You must submit them correctly to avoid missing marks. Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Access Portal: Visit the official portal at aaghi.aiou.edu.pk.
  2. User Authentication: Enter your unique username and password provided by AIOU via SMS.
  3. Select Course: Navigate to your dashboard, select the relevant course code, and click on "Assignment 1" or "Assignment 2".
  4. Upload Document: Click on "Add Submission". Drag and drop your typed PDF file (ensure the file is under 5MB).
  5. Save Changes: Click "Save Changes". Ensure the submission status changes to "Submitted for grading" in green.
