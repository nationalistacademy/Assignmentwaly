📌 When is Picture Change Required?

  • ✅ Your face is not clearly visible in the existing photograph.
  • ✅ The existing photo is too old (more than 5 years).
  • ✅ You have legally changed your appearance (e.g., beard, hijab, surgery).
  • ✅ You are applying for a degree, transcript, or renewed ID card.
  • ✅ The university has requested a fresh photograph.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure to Change Picture at AIOU

  1. Download the Application Form: Click the green button above to download the “PictureChange.jpg” form.
  2. Fill the Form:
    • Write your full name, enrollment number, program, and semester.
    • Clearly mention “Request for Change of Photograph”.
    • Attach one new passport‑size photograph (with white/blue background).
    • Also attach one old photograph for reference (if available).
  3. Attach Supporting Documents:
    • Copy of your CNIC/B‑Form.
    • Copy of existing AIOU ID card (if any).
    • Affidavit on stamp paper (if major appearance change).
  4. Pay the Processing Fee: Rs 500 (subject to change). Deposit in any AIOU regional office or via online challan.
  5. Submit the Application: Send by courier or visit your nearest AIOU regional office. Keep a stamped copy as acknowledgment.
  6. Follow Up: After 15–20 working days, contact the examination department to confirm the update.
Pro Tip: Always keep a photocopy of your submitted application along with the bank fee receipt. The process usually takes 2–4 weeks.

📋 Required Documents Checklist

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Change of Picture

🔹 Can I change my picture online?
No, AIOU currently requires a physical application with attested photographs. You must submit the form by hand or courier.
🔹 What size photograph is required?
Passport‑size (1.5 x 2 inches) with white or light blue background. Do not wear glasses or cap.
🔹 How will I know the picture has been updated?
You will receive an updated ID card or the change will reflect on your LMS profile. Contact the examination department for confirmation.
🔹 Is there a last date for picture change?
No fixed deadline, but it is best to apply at least 2 months before degree issuance or examination.
