📌 Who is Eligible for Online Refund?

  • ✅ Students who paid admission/registration fees but did NOT complete the admission process.
  • ✅ Students who withdrew from a program within the stipulated withdrawal period.
  • ✅ Duplicate fee payment made by mistake (e.g., paid twice for same course).
  • ✅ Cancellation of admission by the university due to eligibility issues.
  • ✅ Overpayment of fees beyond the required amount.
Time Limit: Refund applications must be submitted within 60 days from the fee deposit date. Late applications are generally rejected.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure for Online Refund Application

  1. Download the Procedure PDF: Click the green button above to read the official step‑by‑step guide from AIOU.
  2. Prepare Required Documents:
    • Original fee deposit challan / bank receipt.
    • Attested copy of CNIC.
    • AIOU enrollment number (if assigned).
    • Admission form copy (if available).
    • Bank account details (for online refund transfer).
  3. Access the Online Refund Portal: Visit the official AIOU website → “Students” → “Online Refund Application” or use the direct link provided in the PDF.
  4. Fill the Online Form:
    • Enter personal details (name, CNIC, mobile number).
    • Enter enrollment number (if any).
    • Select the semester and program.
    • Upload scanned copies of challan and CNIC.
    • Provide bank account information (IBAN, account title, bank name).
  5. Submit Application: After reviewing, click “Submit”. You will receive an application tracking number via SMS/email.
  6. Track Status: Use the tracking number on the same portal to check processing status.
  7. Refund Processing Time: Normally 30–60 days after verification. Refund is transferred directly to your bank account.
Pro Tip: Keep printed copies of the submitted application and tracking number. If no update after 60 days, contact the AIOU accounts department or regional office.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Online Refund Application

🔹 Can I apply for a refund if I have not started classes?
Yes, if the semester has not yet started and you withdraw officially, you may be eligible for a partial refund.
🔹 How long does it take to get the refund?
Normally 30–60 days after successful verification. Delays can occur due to incomplete documentation.
🔹 Is the refund amount sent via bank transfer or cheque?
AIOU now prefers direct bank transfer (IBAN). You must provide correct bank details in the online form.
🔹 What if I paid by EasyPaisa or JazzCash?
Refund will still be processed to your bank account. You must provide bank account details on the portal.
