📌 What is RUMC and When to file an Appeal? (Shikayat e Izala)

Regional University Management Committee (RUMC) local regional offices par banayi gayi aik special administrative authority hai jo students ke admission cancellation, fee refund applications rejection, change of subjects, aur copy checking disciplinary disputes (UMC cases) par final verification decisions deti hai. Agar local RUMC ka decision aapke khilaf aata hai aur aap samajhte hain ke aap ke sath zyadati hui hai, to aap Registrar Office Main Campus H-8 Islamabad me Central Appeals Board ke paas review ke liye form bhej sakte hain.

Aapko darj-zail cases me is appeal cell se contact karna chahiye:

  • ✅ Unfair Refund Rejections: Fee refund request bina kisi legal rule ke cancel kar di gayi ho.
  • ✅ Admission Revocation: Document verification delay ki wajah se regular admission cancel ho chuka ho.
  • ✅ Disciplinary/UMC Unfair Verdicts: Paper copy cheating case (UMC) me bina waja saza sunayi gayi ho.
  • ✅ Fresh Evidence Availability: Aapke paas naye verified certificates ya proof hon jo pehle committee ke samne pesh na kiye ja sake hon.
Appeal Karne Ki Aakhri Tareeq: RUMC decision letter receive hone ke 30 din ke andar appeal central department ko rawana karein. 30 din ke baad bheji gayi appeals legal framework ke mutabiq directly reject kar di jati hain.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure to File RUMC Appeal

  1. Download Official Form: Upar diye gaye central green button par click kar ke standard Word (.DOC) form download karein aur print lein.
  2. Draft Statement of Facts:
    • Apna mukammal personal data (Name, CNIC, Phone Number, and Student ID/Enrollment Number) fill karein.
    • RUMC Case Reference: RUMC letter ka tracking/reference number aur issue date darj karein.
    • Grounds of Appeal (Ahem Nuqat): Clear headings ke sath likhein ke RUMC ka faisla kis waja se ghalat hai. (Agar space kam ho to sath extra paper block attach karein).
  3. Attach Legally Certified Evidence:
    • Attested copy of CNIC / Student Portal Profile Card.
    • RUMC original Decision Letter jo regional center se mila ho.
    • Pehle se regional desk ko submit ki gayi original application ki photo copy.
    • Fresh Annexures (Naye Saboot): Medical certificates, verified transcripts, fee slips ya stamp papers (Affidavit) jo aapke claim ko solid sabit karein.
  4. Appeal Processing Fee:
    • Major cases (disciplinary reviews) ke liye Rs. 500 se Rs. 1,000/- processing fee bank challan ke zariye pay karni hogi (Fee details niche tabular grid me check karein).
  5. Send to Registrar’s Office:
    • Apne complete application bundle ko envelope me seal kar ke by registry/courier direct rawana karein: Registrar Office, Central Appeal Committee Section, Block No. 1 (Admin Block), Ground Floor, AIOU Sector H-8, Islamabad – 44000.
Pro Tip (Legal Representation): Disciplinary UMC aur serious cheating charges ke cases me form ke sath Stamp Paper (Affidavit) zaroor bhein jis me likha ho ke aap dobara aisi kisi activity me shamil nahi honge. Is se committee sympathetic grounds par saza maafi (leniency) ka faisla jald karti hai!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – RUMC Appeal Support Desk

🔹 Appeal process hone me kitna time lagta hai?
Normal course me Central Appeal Board 30 se 60 working days leta hai. Complex legal cases me 90 din tak ka time lag sakta hai.
🔹 Kya mujhe appeal case ki hearing ke liye Islamabad campus jana parega?
Zaroori nahi hai. Board zyadatar cases documents aur evidence ki base par khud hi resolve karta hai. Lekin UMC/cheating cases me students ko personal hearing ka call letter bheja ja sakta hai.
🔹 Agar Central Appeal bhi reject ho jaye to aakhri option kya hai?
Agar Central Appeal Committee faisla khilaf deti hai, to aap University Ombudsman (محتسب) ke paas shikaayat darj kar sakte hain ya directly Vice Chancellor ko Review petition file kar sakte hain.
🔹 Kya appeal form online portals par submit ho sakta hai?
Nahi. RUMC decisions ke khilaf reviews administrative legal records hote hain, is liye paid bank challan ke sath physical application courier ke zariye central campus bhejna laazmi hai.
