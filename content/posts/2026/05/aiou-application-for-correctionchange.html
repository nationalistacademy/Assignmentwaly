📌 When is Address Change/Correction Required? (Tabdeeli e Pata)

Aapko darj-zail situations me address update application zaroor bhejna chahiye:

  • ✅ City Shift: Aap permanently kisi naye shehar ya province me shift ho gaye hon.
  • ✅ Spelling Ghalti: Mohalla, street, ghar number, ya pin code kisi bhi jagah ghalat darj ho.
  • ✅ Incomplete Address: Old address me ghar number ya sector missing ho jis ki wajah se courier delivery fail ho rahi ho.
  • ✅ CNIC Address Match: CNIC pe darj naya address AIOU record se match na karta ho.
  • ✅ Books/Degree Delivery Fail: University ke books ya degree package delivery attempts fail ho chuki hon.
Ahem Mashwara (Best Time to Apply): Degree printing, transcript dispatch, ya roll number slip release se kam az kam 2 se 3 mahine pehle address update application submit karein. Dispatch ho jane ke baad records change nahi ho sakte!

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure for Address Change

  1. Download & Print: Upar diye gaye green button par click kar ke official PDF download karein aur A4 print lein.
  2. Provide Accurate Information:
    • Apna mukammal naam, enrollment number, program aur semester darj karein.
    • Purana Pata (Old Address): Jo address AIOU records me ghalat ya outdated show ho raha hai bilkul waisa hi likhein.
    • Naya Pata (New Address): Ghar/flat number, mohalla/street, sector, shehar, district, aur postal pin code mukammal likhein.
    • Active mobile number aur email address bhi confirm karein.
  3. Gather Proof Documents:
    • Attested copy of CNIC (naye address wala agar update ho chuka ho).
    • Proof of new address: Electricity/Gas/Water bill (naye address par), Rent agreement (lease), ya local domicile certificate.
    • Province shift cases: Rs. 50/100 stamp paper par signed affidavit ke aap permanently shift ho gaye hain.
  4. Pay Processing Fee (If Required):
    • Active students: Rs. 0/- (Bilkul Free).
    • Post-degree updates: Rs. 200/- to Rs. 500/- token fee (fee grid niche hai).
  5. Submit Application Package:
    • Courier: Director of Examinations, Correction Cell, AIOU, Sector H-8, Islamabad – 44000.
    • In Person: Examination Department, Ground Floor, Admin Block, AIOU Main Campus, H-8 Islamabad.
    • Regional Office: Nearest AIOU regional center (forward karein ge).
  6. Get Stamped Acknowledgment: Return copy par receive stamp zaroor lein taakay proof record rahe.
  7. Follow Up: 15 se 30 working days baad LMS profile check karein ya examination department ko call karein.
Pro Tip (Double Confirmation): Physical form ke sath-sath LMS portal login karein aur "My Profile → Edit Address" section me bhi naya address update karein. Double update se delivery system dono databases me sync ho jata hai!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Address Update Support

🔹 Kya main sirf LMS portal se address update kar sakta hon?
LMS me profile update karna sirf contact record ke liye hota hai. Degree printing aur books dispatch ke liye official physical form submit karna compulsory hai, warna parcel dispatch nahi hoga.
🔹 Meri purani degree par naya address print ho ga?
Nahi. Jo degree ya transcript already print ho chuki ho, uska address change nahi hota. Sirf future documents naye address par issue honge. Agar existing degree ko update karwana ho, to Duplicate Degree form alag se bharna hoga.
🔹 Agar utility bill naye address par nahi hai to address proof kaise dein?
Agar bill nahi hai, to local rent agreement (baqa'ida sign shuda) ya Rs. 100 stamp paper par affidavit de sakte hain. Gazetted officer ki attestation bhi acceptable hai.
🔹 Address change request ka last date kya hai?
Koi fixed deadline nahi hai, lekin degree printing ya roll number slips dispatch se kam az kam 2 mahine pehle apply zaroor karein warna update dispatch cycle me apply nahi hoga.
