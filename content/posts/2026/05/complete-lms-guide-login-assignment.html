📌 What is AIOU LMS / Aaghi Portal?

The AIOU LMS (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk) is an online platform launched to digitize distance education. It allows students to:

  • 📚 View digital course books and study material
  • 📝 Submit assignments online (typed or scanned handwritten)
  • 💻 Attend online workshops and webinars
  • 📊 Check assignment marks and exam results
  • 💬 Communicate with tutors via messaging
  • 📅 Access semester schedules and deadlines

All AIOU programs – from Matric to PhD – use the same LMS platform.

🔑 How to Login to AIOU LMS (Aaghi Portal) – Step by Step

Step 1: Go to the Official Website

Open your browser and visit https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk (or https://lms.aiou.edu.pk).

Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

Username: Your AIOU enrollment number (e.g., 0000-1234567).
Password: Default is your CNIC number (without dashes) or the password set during admission. If you changed it, use the new one.

Step 3: Click “Login”

After entering, click the Login button. If successful, you will see your dashboard with enrolled courses.

Step 4: Forgot Password?

Click “Forgot Password” link. Enter your enrollment number. A reset link will be sent to your registered email or phone. Follow the instructions to create a new password.

📝 How to Submit Assignments on AIOU LMS (Spring 2026)

  1. Login to Aaghi LMS.
  2. From the dashboard, click on your course (e.g., “417 – Pakistan Studies”).
  3. Click on the “Assignments” tab.
  4. You will see Assignment 1, Assignment 2, etc. Click “Add Submission” next to the assignment you want to submit.
  5. Upload your file: Only PDF format is accepted (max 10MB). If your assignment is handwritten, scan it into a single PDF using a mobile scanner app (CamScanner, Microsoft Lens).
  6. Click “Save Changes” or “Upload”.
  7. Verify submission: After upload, refresh the page. The status should change from “Not submitted” to “Submitted” or “Pending grading.” Download the file to ensure it’s readable.
⚠️ Important Deadlines – Spring 2026

Late submissions are NOT allowed – LMS closes automatically after the deadline.

💻 How to Attend Online Workshops via LMS

AIOU conducts mandatory online workshops for many programs (especially B.Ed, BS, MA). Steps:

  • Login to Aaghi LMS.
  • Look for “Workshop” or “Live Sessions” in your course menu.
  • Click on the scheduled workshop link.
  • You may need to install Zoom or join via browser.
  • Attendance is automatically recorded by the system.

Workshop Schedule (Spring 2026): Phase 1: June 2–4, 2026 | Phase 2: June 12–14, 2026 (after Eid holidays). Check your LMS for exact dates.

📊 How to Check Assignment Marks / Exam Results on LMS

  • Login to Aaghi LMS.
  • Go to your course → “Grades” or “Results” section.
  • You will see marks for assignments and final exams (once announced).
  • You can also download result cards from the main dashboard under “My Results”.

AIOU LMS is mobile‑responsive. You can access it via smartphone browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari). There is no official app, but the website works well on mobile. For better experience, use desktop mode on larger screens when submitting assignments.

📚 AIOU Assignment Submission Guidelines (Spring 2026)

To avoid getting your assignments rejected by your tutor, you must carefully follow the submission rules set by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU):

  • Matric, FA, BA/B.Com: Must be written by hand on blank single-sided pages. Staple three copies of the official Assignment Pertal (پرتال) form and send them via Pakistan Post or TCS directly to your tutor's address before the deadline.
  • BS, B.Ed, MA, MSc: Type digitally in MS Word, convert your file into PDF format, and upload it directly to the official Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk).
