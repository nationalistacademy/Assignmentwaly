📌 When is Correction Required?

  • ✅ Spelling mistake in your name, father’s name, or surname.
  • ✅ Difference between AIOU records and CNIC/B‑Form.
  • ✅ Incorrect date of birth (DOB) entered during admission.
  • ✅ Legal name change (after marriage, court order, etc.).
  • ✅ Father’s name correction due to spelling or documentation error.

📝 Step‑by‑Step Procedure for Correction

  1. Download the Form: Click the green button above to get the “Name_FName_DoBcorrection letter.pdf”.
  2. Fill the Form Carefully:
    • Write your full enrollment number, program, and semester.
    • Clearly mention the existing incorrect entry and the desired correct entry.
    • Attach a recent passport‑size photograph (attested).
  3. Attach Supporting Documents:
    • Attested copy of CNIC (for name/DOB correction).
    • Attested copy of B‑Form (for students under 18).
    • Matric / Intermediate certificate (if name/DOB is correct there).
    • Affidavit on stamp paper (for major name changes or legal name change).
    • Marriage certificate / court order (for name change after marriage).
  4. Pay the Processing Fee: Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 (depending on type of correction). Deposit challan at any AIOU regional office or bank branch.
  5. Submit the Application: Send by registered courier or visit the AIOU Examination Department, Ground Floor, Main Campus, Islamabad.
  6. Follow Up: After 30–45 working days, check your LMS profile or contact the examination department for confirmation.
Pro Tip: Always keep a copy of the submitted application, fee receipt, and all supporting documents. The process may take 1–2 months.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Correction in Name, Father Name & DOB

🔹 Can I apply for correction without visiting the university?
Yes, you can send the entire application with supporting documents via registered courier to the Examination Department, Islamabad.
🔹 What if my name is different on CNIC and Matric certificate?
You must provide both documents and an affidavit explaining the discrepancy. The decision rests with the university committee.
🔹 Can the date of birth be changed after degree issuance?
No, DOB correction is usually allowed only before the degree is printed. After degree issuance, it is very difficult.
🔹 How will I know the correction has been approved?
You will receive an updated ID card, and the corrected details will appear in your LMS profile and future transcripts.
