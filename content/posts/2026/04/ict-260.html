📖 Course Outline – ICT 260

ICT 260 introduces fundamental concepts of information technology, computer hardware/software, internet services, and the role of ICT in education. The official AIOU handbook covers the following eight units:

  • Unit 1: Introduction to IT – definitions, history, and applications.
  • Unit 2: Computer Hardware – CPU, memory, input/output devices.
  • Unit 3: Computer Software – system software, application software, OS.
  • Unit 4: Computer Networks – LAN, WAN, internet, intranet.
  • Unit 5: Internet Services – email, web browsing, e‑commerce, social media.
  • Unit 6: ICT in Education – LMS, digital classrooms, online assessment.
  • Unit 7: Security & Privacy – viruses, firewalls, encryption, ethics.
  • Unit 8: Emerging Technologies – cloud computing, AI, IoT in education.

✍️ Assignment Writing (90%+ Marks)

Based on feedback from AIOU tutors, follow this structure to maximise your marks:

  • Cover page: Course code (260), title, student name, roll number, tutor name, semester.
  • Restate each question before answering.
  • Use headings, bullet points, and short paragraphs – avoid long blocks.
  • Include diagrams (CPU block, network topologies) – hand‑drawn or digital.
  • Cite references (see reference section below). At least 2–3 sources per assignment.
  • PDF naming: 260_YourRollNo_Assignment1.pdf (no spaces).
