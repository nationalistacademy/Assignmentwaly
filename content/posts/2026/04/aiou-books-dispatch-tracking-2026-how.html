📦 AIOU Books Dispatch Tracking 2026 – Complete Guide

✍️ By Assignmentwaly 📅 Updated: April 13, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read 📚 For AIOU Students

You've confirmed your admission. You're excited to start studying. But now you're waiting... and waiting... and waiting for your books to arrive. Sound familiar?

I've been there. Every semester, students ask me: "Where are my books? How do I track them? When will they come?" The anxiety is real — especially when assignments start coming and you don't have your study material yet.

The good news? AIOU now has a books dispatch tracking system. You can check exactly where your books are and when they'll arrive. In this guide, I'll show you all the methods to track your study material — SMS, online portal, and courier tracking.

📌 Before You Start: Keep your Roll Number and CNIC handy. You'll need them for all tracking methods. Books are usually dispatched 2-4 weeks after admission confirmation.

📱 Method 1: Track Books via SMS (Fastest)

AIOU offers a dedicated SMS service for book dispatch tracking. This is the quickest way to get an update — no internet needed!

1 Open your phone's messaging app.

2 Type the following format:

BOOKS [space] Your Roll Number

Example: If your roll number is 123456789, type:

BOOKS 123456789

3 Send to 8008.

4 You'll receive an SMS reply with one of these:

  • ✅ "Your books have been dispatched on [Date] via [Courier Name]. Tracking ID: XXXXX"
  • ⏳ "Your books are being processed. Please wait for dispatch."
  • ❌ "No dispatch record found. Please contact your regional office."
✅ Pro Tip: If you get a tracking ID, save it immediately. You'll use it to track the courier online (see Method 3 below).

💻 Method 2: Track Books via AIOU Online Portal

Prefer using a computer? The online portal gives you more detailed information.

1 Visit the official AIOU website: www.aiou.edu.pk

2 Navigate to "Student Services" → "Books Dispatch Status" or "Study Material Tracking"

3 Enter your Roll Number and CNIC (without dashes)

4 Complete the captcha verification

5 Click "Submit" or "Check Status"

6 Your dispatch status will appear showing:

  • Dispatch date
  • Courier service name (TCS, Pakistan Post, Leopards, or Call Courier)
  • Tracking number
  • Expected delivery date
  • List of books dispatched
⚠️ Note: The online portal can be slow during peak hours (10 AM - 4 PM). Try checking early morning (6-8 AM) or late night for faster loading.

🚚 Method 3: Track via Courier Website (After Dispatch)

Once you have your tracking ID from SMS or portal, you can track your books directly on the courier's website.

📮 TCS (Most Common)

AIOU mostly uses TCS for book dispatch. Tracking is very easy.

Steps:

  • Visit: www.tcs.com.pk/tracking
  • Enter your TCS Tracking Number (e.g., 12345678901)
  • Click "Track" to see current location and estimated delivery

📮 Pakistan Post

For remote areas, books may be sent via Pakistan Post.

Steps:

  • Visit: www.pakpost.gov.pk/tracking
  • Enter your tracking number
  • Click "Track" (note: Pakistan Post updates can be slower)

📮 Leopards Courier

Sometimes used for major cities.

Steps:

  • Visit: www.leopardscourier.com/tracking
  • Enter your tracking number
  • Click "Track Shipment"

📮 Call Courier

Another courier service used by AIOU.

Steps:

  • Visit: www.callcourier.com.pk/tracking
  • Enter your tracking number

📅 Books Dispatch Timeline – What to Expect

📌 Typical Dispatch Schedule

  • Admission Confirmation: Week 1-4 after application
  • Books Processing: 1-2 weeks after confirmation
  • Books Dispatched: 2-4 weeks after confirmation
  • Delivery Time: 3-10 days after dispatch (depends on location)
  • Total Time: Usually 4-8 weeks from admission to books in hand
📌 Real talk: During peak admission seasons (Jan-Feb and July-Aug), dispatch can take 6-10 weeks. Don't panic if your books take longer than expected — thousands of students are waiting at the same time.

📞 Method 4: Contact AIOU Regional Office

If online methods aren't working or you need immediate help, visit or call your nearest regional office.

What to bring:

  • Your Roll Number
  • CNIC copy
  • Admission confirmation (if available)

What they can do: The staff can check the dispatch status in their internal system and give you a direct update. In some cases, they can even arrange for books to be issued from the regional office if dispatch is delayed.

❌ Common Problems & Solutions

❌ Problem: "No dispatch record found" error

Solution: Your books may not have been dispatched yet. Wait 1-2 weeks and check again. If it's been more than 6 weeks since admission confirmation, contact your regional office.

❌ Problem: SMS to 8008 not working

Solution: Check your balance (need at least Rs. 5). Ensure correct format: BOOKS [space] Roll Number. Try sending from a different mobile network.

❌ Problem: Tracking shows "Delivered" but books not received

Solution: Check with neighbors or family members. Sometimes couriers leave books with a security guard or neighbor. If still not found, contact the courier company immediately with your tracking ID.

❌ Problem: Books are damaged or missing pages

Solution: Don't accept delivery if the package is visibly damaged. If already accepted, contact your regional office within 7 days with photos of the damage. They will arrange for replacement.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does it take for AIOU books to arrive after dispatch?

Usually 3-10 working days depending on your location. Major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad) take 3-5 days. Remote areas may take 7-14 days.

Q2: Can I get my books from the regional office instead of courier?

In some cases, yes. If you urgently need books and they haven't been dispatched, visit your regional office. They may issue books directly if available in stock.

Q3: What if I moved to a new address after admission?

Update your address with your regional office immediately. If books are already dispatched, you may need to contact the courier company to redirect the package (additional charges may apply).

Q4: Do I have to pay anything when receiving my books?

No! AIOU books are free for enrolled students. The courier company cannot ask for any payment at delivery. If someone asks for money, do NOT pay and report to AIOU immediately.

Q5: Can I track books for previous semesters?

The tracking system usually only shows current semester dispatch. For previous semester books, contact your regional office directly.

Q6: What if I receive incomplete books (some courses missing)?

Contact your regional office immediately. Sometimes books are dispatched in multiple packages. Ask them to check if all books were sent or if there's a delay in printing for specific courses.

Q7: Can I access digital books while waiting for physical copies?

Yes! Log in to the Aaghi LMS portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk) using your roll number. Many courses have digital versions of textbooks available for download. This is a lifesaver if physical books are delayed.

🎓 Final Advice: Waiting for books can be frustrating, but try to be patient. The dispatch system handles thousands of students every semester. Use the tracking methods above regularly, and don't hesitate to contact your regional office if it's been more than 8 weeks since admission confirmation.

And remember — digital books on the LMS portal are available immediately. Start there while you wait for your physical copies!
