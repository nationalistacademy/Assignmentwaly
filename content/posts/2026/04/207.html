❓ Question 1: What is a sentence? Explain its kinds with examples.

Answer:

🔹 Definition of a Sentence:

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought. It begins with a capital letter and ends with a punctuation mark (., ?, !).

🔹 Kinds of Sentences:

TypeDefinitionExample
Declarative (Statement)Makes a statement or expresses an opinion.She is a good student.
Interrogative (Question)Asks a question.What is your name?
Imperative (Command/Request)Gives a command or makes a request.Close the door. Please help me.
Exclamatory (Exclamation)Expresses strong emotion.What a beautiful day!

❓ Question 4: Fill in the blanks with correct form of the verb (Tenses).

Answer:

  1. She _______ (go) to school every day. → goes
  2. They _______ (play) football now. → are playing
  3. I _______ (finish) my homework yesterday. → finished
  4. He _______ (live) in Lahore since 2010. → has lived
  5. We _______ (visit) the museum tomorrow. → will visit
💡 Tense Rules Applied: Present Simple for habits, Present Continuous for ongoing actions, Past Simple for completed past, Present Perfect for duration, Future Simple for future plans.

❓ Question 8: Write a story with the moral "Honesty is the Best Policy". Use the outline: (woodcutter - axe falls into river - sad - angel appears - brings golden axe - woodcutter refuses - then silver axe - refuses - then his own axe - rewards him for honesty)

Answer:

The Honest Woodcutter

Once upon a time, there lived a poor woodcutter. He used to cut wood from the forest and sell it to earn his living. One day, while he was cutting wood near a river, his axe slipped from his hand and fell into the deep water. He became very sad because he had no money to buy a new axe.

Suddenly, an angel appeared from the river. She asked, "Why are you crying?" The woodcutter told her the whole story. The angel felt pity for him. She dived into the water and brought a golden axe. She asked, "Is this your axe?" The woodcutter replied, "No, this is not mine."

The angel dived again and brought a silver axe. The woodcutter again refused, saying, "That is not mine either." Finally, the angel brought his own iron axe. The woodcutter happily said, "Yes, this is my axe!"

The angel was very pleased with his honesty. She gave him the golden and silver axes as a reward. The woodcutter became rich and lived happily ever after.

Moral: Honesty is the Best Policy.
