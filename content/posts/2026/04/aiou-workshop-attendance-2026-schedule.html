AIOU Workshop Attendance 2026 – Schedule & Important Dates

Complete AIOU workshop schedule for the 2026 semester. Mandatory attendance rules, datesheet, and online workshop guide for all programs.

What Are AIOU Workshops?

Workshops are mandatory group sessions where tutors:

  • Teach difficult topics.
  • Solve student questions.
  • Check attendance (required for exams).
  • Collect assignments.

How to Check Your Workshop Schedule

  • Method 1: Visit tutor.aiou.edu.pk → Enter roll number → View workshop dates.
  • Method 2: SMS "WORKSHOP [Roll Number]" to 8008.
  • Method 3: Check the notice board at your regional office.

Attendance Rules (Very Important!)

  • Minimum 70% attendance is required.
  • Missing 2 workshops = exam entry blocked.
  • Sign the attendance register at every workshop.
  • Late comers (after 30 mins) marked absent.
  • Medical emergency = submit doctor certificate.

What Happens at Workshops

Day Activities
Day 1 Introduction + Course overview + Assignment guidelines
Day 2-3 Chapter-wise teaching + Question answers
Day 4 Past paper discussion + Exam tips
Day 5 Assignment checking + Final Q&A

Online Workshops (For Distance Students)

Some programs offer online workshops via Zoom. Requirements:

  • Working laptop/phone with a camera.
  • Stable internet connection.
  • Join 10 minutes before start time.
  • Keep microphone muted until asked.

📚 AIOU Assignment Submission Guidelines (Spring 2026)

To avoid getting your assignments rejected by your tutor, you must carefully follow the submission rules set by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU):

  • Matric, FA, BA/B.Com: Must be written by hand on blank single-sided pages. Staple three copies of the official Assignment Pertal (پرتال) form and send them via Pakistan Post or TCS directly to your tutor's address before the deadline.
  • BS, B.Ed, MA, MSc: Type digitally in MS Word, convert your file into PDF format, and upload it directly to the official Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Is workshop attendance mandatory for exams?
YES. Without 70% attendance, you cannot appear in final exams. No exceptions.
❓ What if I miss a workshop due to an emergency?
Submit a medical certificate or proof of emergency to the regional office within 7 days.
