AIOU MA Program Guide 2026 – Admission, Fee, Subjects, Career

Complete guide to AIOU MA (Master of Arts) 2-year program. Admission process, fee, available subjects, eligibility, and career opportunities after MA.

What is AIOU MA Program?

MA is a 2-year (4 semesters) postgraduate degree program for students who have completed BA or equivalent.

MA Programs Offered by AIOU 2026

  • MA English – Teaching, content writing, journalism
  • MA Urdu – Teaching, poetry, literature
  • MA Islamic Studies – Religious education, research
  • MA History – Teaching, research, archives
  • MA Political Science – Civil services, politics
  • MA Economics – Banking, finance, planning
  • MA Sociology – Social work, development
  • MA Psychology – Counseling, HR, clinical
  • MA Education – Teaching, school administration
  • MA Pakistan Studies – Research, teaching

Eligibility Criteria for MA Program

- BA/BSc/BCom from recognized university
- Minimum 45% marks in BA/BSc
- No third division in graduation
- Relevant subjects preferred (BA English for MA English, etc.)
- Age limit: No maximum age limit

MA Fee Structure 2026

Admission Fee (one time): Rs. 4,000
Tuition Fee per semester: Rs. 5,500
Total 4 semesters: Rs. 22,000
Degree Fee (at end): Rs. 2,000
Total Program Cost: Rs. 28,000

MA Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Visit admission.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Click "MA Program Admission 2026"
Step 3: Fill online form with your BA details
Step 4: Upload BA degree and marksheet
Step 5: Pay admission fee (Rs. 4,000)
Step 6: Submit and save application number

MA Program Structure

Semester 1: Compulsory courses + 2 core subjects
Semester 2: Core subjects + 1 elective
Semester 3: Advanced core subjects + research methods
Semester 4: Specialization + Thesis/Research Project

Career Opportunities After MA

Teaching: School Teacher (Rs. 40,000-80,000), Lecturer (Rs. 60,000-120,000 after MPhil)
Content Writing: Rs. 50,000-150,000/month
Civil Services: CSS/PMS officer (Rs. 80,000-200,000)
Banking: Officer (Rs. 60,000-120,000)
NGO Sector: Program Manager (Rs. 70,000-150,000)

Further Studies After MA

✅ MPhil/MS in same subject (2 years) – Required for lecturer jobs
✅ PhD (3-5 years) – For professor and research careers
✅ B.Ed (1 year) – For teaching in schools
✅ Professional diplomas in your field

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do MA without BA?

No. BA/BSc is mandatory for MA admission. You cannot skip graduation.

Is AIOU MA degree valid for PhD?

Yes, after completing MA with minimum 55% marks, you can apply for MPhil/PhD in any HEC recognized university.
