Synonym of "Firm" is:

B) Persistent

📝 Part 2: Important Grammar Questions

1
What is a preposition? Give examples.

Answer: A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. It indicates position, direction, time, or manner.

Examples: in, on, at, under, over, between, among, during, before, after, with, without, for, since, etc.

Usage Examples:

  • The book is on the table.
  • She arrived after the meeting.
  • He walked toward the door.
2
Change the voice: "She will write a letter."
Answer: A letter will be written by her.
Rule: Active to Passive: Object + will be + past participle + by + subject.
3
Change the narration: "He said, 'I am happy.'"
Answer: He said that he was happy.
Rule: Direct to Indirect: Remove commas and quotation marks. Change pronouns and tense accordingly (present to past).
4
Identify the underlined word: "Pakistan is very hot in summer." (Adjective/Adverb/Noun)
Answer: Adjective
Explanation: "Hot" describes the noun "Pakistan", so it is an adjective.
5
Identify the sentence type: "If we wait here, we shall be late."
Answer: Conditional Sentence (First Conditional)
Explanation: First conditional sentences express real and possible situations. Structure: If + present simple, will + infinitive.

📝 Part 4: Important Essay Topics (150-200 words)

1
My Aim in Life / My Ambition

Outline:

  • Introduction – Every person should have a goal in life
  • My aim – To become a teacher
  • Reasons – Teaching is a noble profession, helps build the nation
  • Preparation – I study hard and learn teaching skills
  • Conclusion – With determination and hard work, I will achieve my aim
2
Importance of Education

Outline:

  • Introduction – Education is the key to success
  • Benefits – Removes ignorance, builds character, provides opportunities
  • Education for all – Men and women both need education
  • Role of education in national development
  • Conclusion – Education is essential for a prosperous society
3
Role of Media in Society

Outline:

  • Introduction – Media is the fourth pillar of the state
  • Types of media – Print, electronic, social media
  • Positive role – Informs, educates, entertains, exposes corruption
  • Negative aspects – Fake news, propaganda, time-wasting
  • Conclusion – Media should be responsible and balanced
4
Advantages and Disadvantages of Social Media

Outline:

  • Introduction – Social media has changed modern communication
  • Advantages – Connectivity, information sharing, business opportunities
  • Disadvantages – Addiction, privacy issues, spread of fake news
  • Conclusion – Wise and limited use is beneficial
5
A Visit to a Historical Place

Outline:

  • Introduction – Historical places remind us of our heritage
  • Name of the place – Badshahi Mosque, Minar-e-Pakistan, Mohenjo-Daro, etc.
  • Description – Location, architecture, historical significance
  • Experience – What I saw and learned
  • Conclusion – Such visits are educational and inspiring
