🎓 AIOU Scholarship Application Process 2026 – Complete Guide

📅 Updated: April 12, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 9 min read 📚 Category: AIOU Resources ⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

Education is a powerful tool, but financial constraints should never be a barrier. Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU) offers various scholarship programs for deserving, needy, and meritorious students. Whether you're pursuing Matric, FA, BA, BSc, MA, MSc, B.Ed, or M.Ed, there are scholarships available to support your educational journey.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain everything you need to know about AIOU scholarships — from types and eligibility to application process, required documents, deadlines, and tips to increase your chances of selection.

📌 Did You Know? AIOU allocates millions of rupees annually for student scholarships. Thousands of students benefit each year. Don't miss out — apply if you meet the criteria!
🏆 Types of Scholarships Available at AIOU
🎓 Need-Based Scholarship (Financial Assistance) Most Common

For students from low-income families who cannot afford tuition fees. Priority is given to orphans, children of widows, and students with disabilities.

Award Amount: 50% to 100% tuition fee waiver

Eligibility: Family income below PKR 25,000/month

⭐ Merit-Based Scholarship High Achievers

For students with excellent academic performance in previous exams. Encourages high achievers to continue their education.

Award Amount: 25% to 75% tuition fee waiver

Eligibility: Minimum 70% marks in last exam / 3.0 CGPA

👩‍🎓 Women Scholarship Program Empowering Women

Special scholarships for female students to promote female education, especially in rural and remote areas.

Award Amount: 50% tuition fee waiver

Eligibility: Female students enrolled in any AIOU program

🌄 Remote Area Scholarship Rural Support

For students belonging to remote, underdeveloped, or disaster-affected areas (Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, Tharparkar, etc.).

Award Amount: 50% to 100% tuition fee waiver

Eligibility: Domicile of eligible remote area

♿ Special Person / Disability Scholarship Inclusive Education

For students with physical or mental disabilities. Special quota reserved for differently-abled students.

Award Amount: 100% tuition fee waiver + book allowance

Eligibility: Valid disability certificate from recognized medical board

🏅 HEC Need-Based Scholarship (Through AIOU) Government Funded

Funded by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) for deserving students across Pakistan. AIOU acts as the implementing partner.

Award Amount: Up to PKR 120,000 per year

Eligibility: Family income below PKR 45,000/month + academic merit

📚 Hafiz-e-Quran Scholarship Religious Recognition

For students who have completed Hifz (memorization of the Holy Quran). Recognizes religious dedication.

Award Amount: 25% tuition fee waiver

Eligibility: Verified Hafiz-e-Quran certificate

📋 General Eligibility Criteria for AIOU Scholarships
  • ✅ Must be a registered student of AIOU (valid roll number)
  • ✅ Must have minimum 40% marks in last semester/annual exams (for continuing scholarships)
  • ✅ Must not be availing any other scholarship simultaneously
  • ✅ Family income must be below prescribed limit (for need-based)
  • ✅ Must submit complete application before deadline
  • ✅ Must maintain good attendance in workshops (if applicable)
📝 Step-by-Step AIOU Scholarship Application Process
Step 1: Check Scholarship Announcement

Scholarships are announced at the beginning of each semester (Spring: January-February, Autumn: July-August). Check:

  • 📢 AIOU Official Website: www.aiou.edu.pk (News & Announcements section)
  • 📢 Your Regional Office: Notice boards at regional campuses
  • 📢 Aaghi Portal: Student dashboard notifications
  • 📢 Newspapers: Dawn, Jang, The News (national dailies)
Step 2: Download Application Form
  • Visit the AIOU website → "Student Services" → "Scholarships"
  • Download the Scholarship Application Form (PDF format)
  • Alternatively, collect a physical form from your regional office
Step 3: Gather Required Documents

📄 Essential Documents Checklist:

  • ✅ Duly filled Scholarship Application Form (signed)
  • ✅ Attested copy of CNIC (student and father/guardian)
  • ✅ Attested copy of B-Form (for under 18 students)
  • ✅ Attested copy of last exam result/DMC (previous semester/annual)
  • ✅ AIOU Roll Number Slip (current semester)
  • ✅ Income Certificate from Union Council or employer (for need-based)
  • ✅ Electricity/Gas/Sui Gas Bill (proof of residence)
  • ✅ Father/Guardian's CNIC copy (attested)
  • ✅ Passport-size photographs (2-4 copies)
  • ✅ Domicile Certificate (for remote area scholarship)
  • ✅ Disability Certificate (for special person scholarship)
  • ✅ Hafiz-e-Quran Certificate (for Hafiz scholarship)
  • ✅ Death Certificate of father/mother (if applicable, for orphan scholarship)
Step 4: Submit Application
  • Online Submission (if available): Upload scanned documents to the scholarship portal
  • Physical Submission: Submit the complete application package at your nearest AIOU Regional Office or Main Campus (Islamabad)
  • By Post/Courier: Send to "Directorate of Student Financial Aid, Allama Iqbal Open University, Sector H-8, Islamabad"
  • Get Acknowledgment: Always obtain a receipt or acknowledgment slip with submission date
Step 5: Selection Process

Applications are reviewed by the AIOU Scholarship Committee. Selection is based on:

  • Financial need (income level)
  • Academic performance (previous marks)
  • Category/Quota (women, disabled, remote area, etc.)
  • Completeness and accuracy of application
Step 6: Interview (If Required)

Some scholarships require a personal interview or home visit for verification. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted via SMS or phone call.

Step 7: Result Announcement & Award

Selected candidates are notified via:

  • SMS to registered mobile number
  • Notice at regional office
  • Online portal (if applicable)

The scholarship amount is adjusted against tuition fees or disbursed via bank account.

💡 Tips to Increase Your Chances of Getting a Scholarship
  • ✅ Apply early: Don't wait until the last day. Early applications get more attention.
  • ✅ Complete all fields: Incomplete applications are rejected immediately.
  • ✅ Attest documents properly: Get copies attested by a gazetted officer or your regional office.
  • ✅ Write a compelling statement: If required, explain your financial situation clearly and honestly.
  • ✅ Maintain good grades: Scholarships often require renewal each semester based on performance.
  • ✅ Apply for multiple scholarships: Don't limit yourself to one. Apply for all you're eligible for.
  • ✅ Keep copies of everything: Maintain a file of all submitted documents and receipts.
  • ✅ Follow up: Politely check the status of your application after 3-4 weeks.
❌ Common Reasons for Scholarship Rejection
  • ❌ Incomplete application (missing documents or signatures)
  • ❌ Late submission after deadline
  • ❌ Incorrect or falsified information
  • ❌ Income exceeds eligible limit
  • ❌ Low academic performance (below 40% marks)
  • ❌ Already availing another scholarship
  • ❌ Unattested document copies
  • ❌ Not meeting specific category requirements

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: Can I apply for a scholarship if I'm a new student?

Yes! Newly admitted students can apply based on their last academic qualification (Matric, FA, BA, etc.) results. You must have a valid AIOU roll number.

📌 Q2: How much scholarship amount can I get?

It varies by scholarship type. Need-based scholarships offer 50-100% tuition waiver. HEC scholarships offer up to PKR 120,000 per year. Merit scholarships offer 25-75% waiver.

📌 Q3: Is there a separate application for each scholarship?

Yes. Each scholarship has its own application form and requirements. Read the announcement carefully. Some scholarships use a common application form.

📌 Q4: Can I apply for a scholarship if I already have a degree?

Scholarships are for currently enrolled students. If you have completed your degree, you cannot apply. Apply at the beginning of your program.

📌 Q5: How will I know if I'm selected?

Selected candidates receive an SMS on their registered mobile number. Names are also displayed on regional office notice boards and the AIOU website.

📌 Q6: Can I reapply if rejected?

Yes, you can apply again in the next semester. Work on improving your application and gather all required documents properly.

📌 Q7: Do I need to repay the scholarship?

No, scholarships are gifts — they don't need to be repaid. However, some scholarships may require a service commitment (e.g., teaching in a remote area for 2 years).

📌 Q8: Can international students apply for AIOU scholarships?

AIOU scholarships are primarily for Pakistani nationals. International students should check with their home country's scholarship programs.
