AIOU Diploma Programs 2026 – Complete Guide for Short Courses

Complete guide to AIOU diploma and certificate programs 2026. Short courses duration 6 months to 1 year. Fast track your career skills without long degrees.

Why Choose Diploma Programs at AIOU?

Diploma programs are shorter than degrees (6-12 months). They focus on practical job skills. Perfect for working professionals and students who want quick qualifications.

Eligibility Criteria for Diploma Programs

Certificate Courses (6 months):
- Matric pass (minimum 33% marks)
- No age limit

Diploma Programs (1 year):
- FA/FSc/ICS/ICom pass (minimum 40% marks)
- Or equivalent qualification

Post-Graduate Diplomas (1.5 years):
- BA/BSc/BS pass (minimum 45% marks)
- Or MA/MSc (for advanced diplomas)

How to Apply for Diploma Programs

Step 1: Visit admission.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Click "Diploma & Certificate Programs 2026"
Step 3: Select your desired program
Step 4: Fill application form
Step 5: Upload Matric/FA certificate
Step 6: Pay fee online or via challan
Step 7: Submit and save application number

Can You Upgrade Diploma to Degree?

Yes! Diploma credits can be transferred to degree programs:
- DIT diploma → BS Computer Science (1 year exemption)
- DBA diploma → BBA or BS Economics (1 year exemption)
- English certificate → BA English (exemption in 2 courses)

Frequently Asked Questions

Are AIOU diplomas recognized for jobs?

Yes, AIOU diplomas are HEC recognized and accepted for private sector jobs and government posts that require diplomas.

Can I do diploma without Matric?

No. Matric is minimum requirement for certificate and diploma programs.
