📚 AIOU Assignment Submission Guidelines (Spring 2026)

To avoid getting your assignments rejected by your tutor, you must carefully follow the submission rules set by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU):

  • Matric, FA, BA/B.Com: Must be written by hand on blank single-sided pages. Staple three copies of the official Assignment Pertal (پرتال) form and send them via Pakistan Post or TCS directly to your tutor's address before the deadline.
  • BS, B.Ed, MA, MSc: Type digitally in MS Word, convert your file into PDF format, and upload it directly to the official Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk).
