Q1: Define Business and explain its main objectives.

Answer: Business ka matlab hai koi bhi aisi legal activity jo profit kamanay ke liye ki jaye.

Objectives of Business:

  • Profit Making: Business ka sab se bara maqsad munafa kamana hota hai taake wo survive kar sakay.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Ache products de kar logon ko mutmain karna.
  • Innovation: Market mein roz naye aur behtar products mutarif karwana.
  • Social Service: Mulk ki economy ko behtar banana aur logon ko jobs dena.

Q2: What is Partnership? Discuss its Advantages and Disadvantages.

Answer: Jab do ya do se zyada log mil kar karobar karte hain aur munafa ya nuqsan baant-te hain, use Partnership kehte hain.

Advantages Disadvantages
More Capital (Zyada Paisa) Unlimited Liability
Shared Risk Chances of Conflict (Larayi ka darr)
Easy to Start Lack of Continuity

Q3: Explain the Importance of Marketing in Modern Business.

Answer: Aaj ke dour mein marketing ke baghair koi bhi business kamyab nahi ho sakta. Is ki ahem wajah ye hai:

  1. Awareness: Logon ko product ke bare mein pata chalta hai.
  2. Sales Increase: Marketing se sales barhti hai aur munafa zyada hota hai.
  3. Competition: Ye market mein doosri companies se muqabla karne mein madad deti hai.
  4. Brand Building: Is se brand ki pehchan banti hai.

Q4: Define "Capital" and its types.

Answer: Wo paisa ya asasa (assets) jo karobar shuru karne ke liye lagaya jaye, Capital kehlata hai.

  • Fixed Capital: Jo lambay arsay ke liye ho (Land, Building, Machinery).
  • Working Capital: Jo roz mara ke kharchon ke liye ho (Cash, Raw material).

Q5: Role of Information Technology (IT) in Business.

Answer: IT ne business karne ka tariqa badal diya hai. Computer aur internet ki wajah se ab "E-commerce" aam ho gaya hai, inventory manage karna asaan hai, aur communication fast ho gayi hai.
