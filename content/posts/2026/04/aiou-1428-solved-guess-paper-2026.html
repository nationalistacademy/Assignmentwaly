Q1: Define Commercial Geography and explain its relationship with other sciences.

Answer: Commercial Geography un awamil (factors) ka mutalia karti hai jo dunya mein tijarat (trade), paidawar (production), aur distribution ko mutasir karte hain.

Relationship with other sciences:

  • Economics: Dono ka maqsad profit aur resources ki management hai. Economics batati hai "Kitna" aur Geography batati hai "Kahan".
  • Agriculture: Zameen ki noiyat aur mausam ka faslon par asar Commercial Geography ka hissa hai.
  • History: Purani trade routes aur unki tabdeeli ka mutalia history ke zariye hota hai.

Q2: What is the significance of the Fishing Industry in the economy of Pakistan?

Answer: Mahi-giri (Fishing) Pakistan ki economy mein r रीड ki haddi (backbone) ki hesiyat rakhti hai, khas tor par coastal areas (Karachi, Gawadar) ke liye.

Economic Importance:

  • Employment: Lakhon log direct aur indirect taur par is se wabasta hain.
  • Foreign Exchange: Pakistan prawns, shrimps aur fish export kar ke karoron dollars kamata hai.
  • Food Security: Ye protein ka ek sasta aur behtareen zariya hai.
  • By-products: Machli ke tail (fish oil) aur fertilizer ki manufacturing mein madad milti hai.

Q3: Discuss the types and importance of Natural Resources.

Answer: Natural Resources wo khasayen hain jo qudrat ne insan ko muft faraham ki hain.

Main Types:

  • Renewable Resources: Wo jo kabhi khatam nahi hote (Sunlight, Wind, Water).
  • Non-Renewable Resources: Wo jo istemal ke baad khatam ho jate hain (Coal, Gas, Oil).

Importance: Ye resources kisi bhi mulk ki sanati (industrial) taraqqi ke liye base faraham karte hain. In ke baghair manufacturing mumkin nahi.

Q4: Explain the factors affecting the location of an Industry.

Answer: Kisi bhi jagah factory lagane se pehle darj-e-zail points dekhe jate hain:

  1. Raw Material: Factory kacha maal (raw material) ke qareeb honi chahiye taake transport cost kam ho.
  2. Energy Sources: Bijli aur gas ki musalsal farahmi zaroori hai.
  3. Labor: Sasti aur mahir labour ki dastiyabi.
  4. Market: Market qareeb honi chahiye taake tayyar maal fori bik sakay.
  5. Transport: Roads aur railways ka network hona chahiye.

Q5: Write a note on the Cotton Crop in Pakistan.

Answer: Kapas (Cotton) Pakistan ki "Silver Fiber" kehlati hai aur ye sab se bari cash crop hai.

  • Areas: Punjab (Multan, Bahawalpur) aur Sindh (Hyderabad, Sukkur) iske baray markaz hain.
  • Importance: Pakistan ki textile industry 100% isi par depend karti hai.
  • Exports: Raw cotton aur cotton yarn Pakistan ki export ka sab se bara hissa hain.
