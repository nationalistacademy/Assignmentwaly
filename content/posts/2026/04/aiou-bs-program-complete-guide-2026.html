AIOU BS Program Complete Guide 2026 – Admission, Fee, Subjects

Complete guide to AIOU BS (Bachelor of Science) 4-year program. Admission process, fee structure, available majors, eligibility, and career opportunities.

What is AIOU BS Program?

BS is a 4-year (8 semesters) undergraduate degree program. It offers in-depth knowledge in specific subjects with research component in final year.

BS Programs Offered by AIOU 2026

  • BS Computer Science – Best for IT careers
  • BS Economics – For banking and finance jobs
  • BS Psychology – For counseling and HR careers
  • BS Sociology – For social work and development
  • BS English – For teaching and content writing
  • BS Islamic Studies – For religious education
  • BS Pakistan Studies – For research and teaching
  • BS Business Administration – For management careers
  • BS Mass Communication – For media and journalism
  • BS Political Science – For civil services and politics

Eligibility Criteria for BS Program

For BS (4-Year):
- FA/FSc/ICS/ICom with minimum 45% marks
- Or equivalent qualification from recognized board
- No third division in any exam
- Age limit: No maximum age limit for AIOU

For BS (After Associate Degree):
- 2-year BA/BSc with 45% marks
- Can get admission directly in 3rd year (2 years remaining)

BS Fee Structure 2026

Admission Fee (one time): Rs. 4,000
Tuition Fee per semester: Rs. 6,000
Total 8 semesters: Rs. 48,000
Degree Fee (at end): Rs. 2,000
Total Program Cost: Rs. 54,000

BS Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Visit admission.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Click "BS Program Admission 2026"
Step 3: Fill online application form
Step 4: Upload documents (matric, FA/FSc certificates)
Step 5: Pay admission fee (Rs. 4,000)
Step 6: Submit and save application number

BS Program Structure

Year 1 (Semester 1-2): Compulsory courses (English, Islamiat, Pak Studies, Basics)
Year 2 (Semester 3-4): Core subjects of your major
Year 3 (Semester 5-6): Advanced major subjects + electives
Year 4 (Semester 7-8): Specialization + Research Project/Thesis

Career Opportunities After BS

BS Computer Science: Software Developer (Rs. 50,000-150,000/month)
BS Economics: Bank Officer (Rs. 60,000-120,000/month)
BS Psychology: Counselor, HR (Rs. 40,000-100,000/month)
BS Business Administration: Manager (Rs. 50,000-200,000/month)
BS Mass Communication: Journalist, Content Creator (Rs. 40,000-150,000/month)

Further Studies After BS

✅ MS/MPhil in same subject (2 years)
✅ MBA (1.5-2 years)
✅ Professional certifications (PMP, CFA, ACCA)
✅ PhD (3-5 years) for teaching careers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AIOU BS degree accepted for government jobs?

Yes, AIOU BS degree is HEC recognized and accepted for all government jobs in Pakistan including CSS, PMS, and banking.

Can I do job while studying BS in AIOU?

Yes, AIOU is distance learning. You can work full-time and study evenings/weekends.
