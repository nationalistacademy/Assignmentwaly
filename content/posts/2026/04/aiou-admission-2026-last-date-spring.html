Late Admission Rules 2026

  • 15 days late: Rs. 500 fine – Application accepted
  • 30 days late: Rs. 1,000 fine – Application accepted
  • 45 days late: Rs. 2,000 fine – Approval required
  • 60+ days late: Admission closed for semester

How to Apply Before Deadline

Online Application (Recommended):
- Apply anytime 24/7 from home
- Instant submission
- No courier delays
- Payment online or bank challan

Offline Application:
- Submit at regional office during working hours
- Submit at least 7 days before deadline (postal delay risk)

What Happens After Last Date?

If you missed Spring 2026 deadline:
- Apply for Autumn 2026 (starts July)
- Or pay late fee (up to 30 days after deadline)

If you missed both semesters:
- Wait for next year's admissions
- Some diplomas have rolling admission (apply any time)

📚 AIOU Assignment Submission Guidelines (Spring 2026)

To avoid getting your assignments rejected by your tutor, you must carefully follow the submission rules set by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU):

  • Matric, FA, BA/B.Com: Must be written by hand on blank single-sided pages. Staple three copies of the official Assignment Pertal (پرتال) form and send them via Pakistan Post or TCS directly to your tutor's address before the deadline.
  • BS, B.Ed, MA, MSc: Type digitally in MS Word, convert your file into PDF format, and upload it directly to the official Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk).
