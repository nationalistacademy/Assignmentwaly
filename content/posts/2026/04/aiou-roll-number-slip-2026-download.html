AIOU Roll Number Slip 2026 – Download Before Exams

Download AIOU roll number slip 2026 for Spring and Autumn exams. No entry without roll slip. Get your roll slip online or via SMS before exam day.

What is Roll Number Slip?

Roll number slip is your exam entry pass. It contains:
- Your name and photo
- Roll number
- Exam center address
- Subject codes and dates
- Timing of exams

How to Download Roll Number Slip Online

Step 1: Visit rollno.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Enter your CNIC or Form Number
Step 3: Select your program
Step 4: Click "Search"
Step 5: Download and print roll slip

How to Get Roll Slip via SMS

Type: ROLL [Space] CNIC Number
Send to: 8008
Example: ROLL 12345-6789012-3
Receive roll number and center info via SMS (formal slip still required from portal)

Roll Slip Release Dates 2026

Spring 2026 Exams: Roll slip available from August 1, 2026
Autumn 2026 Exams: Roll slip available from February 1, 2027

What If You Lose Your Roll Slip?

Don't panic. You can:
1. Download again from portal (unlimited times)
2. Get duplicate from regional office (Rs. 100 fee)
3. Show downloaded copy on phone (but printed is better)

Information on Your Roll Slip

  • Roll Number: Unique exam ID (memorize this)
  • Exam Center: Full address with room number
  • Date Sheet: All exam dates with subject codes
  • Reporting Time: Usually 30 minutes before exam
  • Instructions: Rules for exam hall

Exam Day Checklist

✅ Printed roll number slip (2 copies)
✅ Original CNIC or student ID card
✅ Blue pens (at least 2)
✅ Water bottle (transparent)
✅ Watch to track time
❌ NO mobile phone
❌ NO smart watch
❌ NO bag or books

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter exam hall without roll number slip?

No. Strictly not allowed. Guard will not let you enter without printed roll slip.

What if my photo is missing on roll slip?

Contact regional office immediately. Carry passport-size photos as backup.
