In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain everything you need to know about AIOU's grading system, the formula to calculate CGPA, practical examples, and tips to improve your CGPA.

📌 What is CGPA? CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of all your semester GPAs. It represents your overall academic performance throughout your program. CGPA ranges from 0.00 to 4.00.

📋 AIOU Grading System & Grade Points

AIOU follows the standard HEC-approved grading system. Each percentage score is converted into a letter grade and grade point.

⚠️ Important: You need a minimum of 40% marks in BOTH assignments and final exams to pass a course. If you fail a course, it shows as "F" with 0.00 grade points.

📐 The CGPA Formula

CGPA = (Sum of (Credit Hours × Grade Point)) ÷ (Total Credit Hours)

📊 Step-by-Step Guide to Calculate CGPA

Step 1: Know Your Course Credit Hours

Each course at AIOU has assigned credit hours. Common credit hours:

  • BA/BSc courses: Usually 3 credit hours per course
  • MA/MSc courses: Usually 3-4 credit hours per course
  • B.Ed/M.Ed courses: Usually 3 credit hours per course
  • Compulsory courses (Islamiat, Pakistan Studies): 2 credit hours

Step 2: Convert Your Percentage to Grade Point

Use the grading table above to convert your percentage score in each course to a grade point.

Step 3: Calculate Quality Points for Each Course

Quality Points = Credit Hours × Grade Point

Step 4: Sum All Quality Points

Add the quality points of all courses you have completed.

Step 5: Divide by Total Credit Hours

CGPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Hours

💡 Tips to Improve Your CGPA

  • ✅ Focus on high-credit courses: They affect your CGPA more than low-credit courses.
  • ✅ Score well in assignments: Assignments contribute 30-40% of your grade. Use our assignment guide!
  • ✅ Retake failed courses: If you fail a course, retake it as soon as possible.
  • ✅ Improve weak subjects: Identify courses where you scored low and work harder next time.
  • ✅ Maintain consistency: A steady performance across semesters is better than ups and downs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📌 Q1: What is a good CGPA in AIOU?
3.00 and above (First Division) is considered good. 3.50+ is excellent.
📌 Q2: Can I improve my CGPA after graduation?
No. Your CGPA is final after you complete your program.
📌 Q3: How are assignment marks included in CGPA?
Assignment marks are part of your overall percentage for each course.
📌 Q4: What CGPA is required for a master's degree admission?
For MS/M.Phil: Minimum 2.50 CGPA. For PhD: Minimum 3.00 CGPA.
