AIOU DBA Program 2026 – Diploma in Business Administration Guide

Complete guide to AIOU DBA (Diploma in Business Administration). 1-year management diploma for office jobs, banking, and business careers.

What is DBA Program?

DBA (Diploma in Business Administration) is a 1-year professional diploma that teaches business management, office administration, and entrepreneurship skills.

Why Choose DBA at AIOU?

  • Quick 1-year qualification for office jobs
  • Learn practical business skills
  • Low fee (Rs. 13,000 total)
  • Perfect for business owners and entrepreneurs
  • Can upgrade to BBA later

DBA Syllabus 2026 – What You Will Learn

Semester 1 Courses:
- Principles of Management
- Business Communication
- Financial Accounting Basics
- Office Management

Semester 2 Courses:
- Marketing Fundamentals
- Human Resource Management Basics
- Business Mathematics
- Entrepreneurship & Small Business

Eligibility for DBA Program

- FA/FSc/ICom pass from recognized board
- Minimum 40% marks in intermediate
- No age limit
- Basic English understanding required

DBA Fee Structure 2026

Admission Fee: Rs. 3,000 (one time)
Tuition Fee Semester 1: Rs. 4,000
Tuition Fee Semester 2: Rs. 4,000
Exam Fee Total: Rs. 2,000
Total Program Cost: Rs. 13,000

How to Apply for DBA

Step 1: Visit AIOU admission portal
Step 2: Search "Diploma in Business Administration"
Step 3: Fill online application form
Step 4: Upload FA/FSc/ICom certificate
Step 5: Pay admission fee
Step 6: Submit and save application number

Jobs After DBA Diploma

Entry Level Jobs (Salary Rs. 30,000-60,000):
- Office Administrator
- Customer Service Representative
- Sales Associate
- Banking Teller
- Admin Assistant

Business Opportunities:
- Start your own small business
- Work as freelancer virtual assistant ($10-25/hour)
- Become independent sales agent

Further Studies After DBA

Short Term (1-2 years):
- Diploma in Digital Marketing
- Certificate in E-commerce
- QuickBooks Certification

Long Term (2-4 years):
- BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) – 2 years after DBA
- BS Economics
- MBA (after BA/BSc)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DBA accepted for government jobs?

DBA is accepted for BPS-7 to BPS-11 posts that require diploma qualification. For BPS-14+, BA/BSc required.

Can I do DBA after Matric?

No. FA/FSc/ICom is minimum requirement for DBA. For Matric students, certificate courses are available.
