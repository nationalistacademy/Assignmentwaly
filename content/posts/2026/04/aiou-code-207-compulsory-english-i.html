❓ Question 1: Define Parts of Speech. Explain any five parts of speech with examples.

Answer:

🔹 Definition of Parts of Speech:

Parts of speech are the basic categories of words according to their function in a sentence. There are eight parts of speech in English: Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, and Interjection.

🔹 Five Parts of Speech with Examples:

1. Noun (اسم): A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.

  • Person: Ali, Sara, teacher, doctor
  • Place: Lahore, school, park, hospital
  • Thing: book, pen, table, car
  • Idea: love, freedom, happiness
  • Examples in sentences: Ali is my friend. I read a book.

2. Pronoun (ضمیر): A pronoun is a word that is used in place of a noun.

  • Examples: he, she, it, they, we, you, I, me, him, her, them
  • Examples in sentences: He is a good student. She likes tea. Give the book to me.

3. Verb (فعل): A verb is a word that shows an action or state of being.

  • Action verbs: run, eat, write, play, sleep
  • State of being verbs: is, am, are, was, were
  • Examples in sentences: She runs fast. They are happy. I write a letter.

4. Adjective (صفت): An adjective is a word that describes a noun or pronoun.

  • Examples: beautiful, tall, red, happy, big, small
  • Examples in sentences: She has a beautiful dress. He is a tall man. The red car is fast.

5. Adverb (قید): An adverb is a word that describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb. It tells how, when, where, or how often.

  • Examples: slowly, quickly, very, yesterday, there, always
  • Examples in sentences: She runs quickly. He is very intelligent. I met him yesterday.
❓ Question 2: What are tenses? Explain the Present Continuous Tense and Past Indefinite Tense with rules and examples.

Answer:

🔹 Definition of Tenses:

Tenses are the forms of verbs that show the time of an action or event. There are three main tenses: Present, Past, and Future. Each tense has four aspects: Indefinite (Simple), Continuous, Perfect, and Perfect Continuous.

🔹 Present Continuous Tense (حال جاری):

Definition: This tense is used to describe an action that is happening right now or is in progress at the moment of speaking.

Rules:

  • Positive Sentence: Subject + is/am/are + Verb (1st form + ing) + Object
  • Negative Sentence: Subject + is/am/are + not + Verb (1st form + ing) + Object
  • Interrogative Sentence: Is/Am/Are + Subject + Verb (1st form + ing) + Object?

Examples:

  • Positive: I am reading a book. She is cooking food. They are playing cricket.
  • Negative: I am not sleeping. She is not watching TV.
  • Interrogative: Are you coming to the party? Is he going to school?

🔹 Past Indefinite Tense (ماضی مطلق):

Definition: This tense is used to describe an action that was completed in the past at a definite time.

Rules:

  • Positive Sentence: Subject + Verb (2nd form) + Object
  • Negative Sentence: Subject + did + not + Verb (1st form) + Object
  • Interrogative Sentence: Did + Subject + Verb (1st form) + Object?

Examples:

  • Positive: I ate an apple. He went to Lahore. They played cricket.
  • Negative: I did not eat meat. She did not come to school.
  • Interrogative: Did you see the movie? Did he call you?

❓ Question 9: Write a short story with the moral "Union is Strength" or "A Friend in Need is a Friend Indeed". You may use the following outline: (An old farmer has three sons - they always quarrel - farmer worried - gives them sticks - asks to break - they break easily - gives them bundle of sticks - none can break - teaches lesson)

Answer:

The Old Farmer and His Three Sons

Moral: Union is Strength


Once upon a time, there lived an old farmer in a village. He had three sons. They were strong and healthy but always quarreled with each other. They never agreed on anything. This made the farmer very sad and worried.


One day, the farmer fell seriously ill. He thought, "If I die, my sons will never live together in peace. They will waste all my property in quarrels." He decided to teach them a lesson.


He called his three sons to his bedside. He gave each son a single stick and said, "Break this stick." The sons easily broke the single stick. Then the farmer gave them a bundle of sticks tied together. He said, "Now break this bundle." Each son tried to break the bundle but failed. None of them could break the bundle.


The farmer then said, "My dear sons, you saw that a single stick breaks easily, but a bundle of sticks is unbreakable. Similarly, if you stay united, no one can harm you. But if you quarrel and stay separate, your enemies will destroy you easily."


The sons understood the lesson. They promised to live together in peace and unity. They never quarreled again and lived happily ever after.


Moral: Union is Strength.
