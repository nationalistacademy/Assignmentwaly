AIOU M.Ed Program 2026 – Complete Guide for Senior Teachers

Complete guide to AIOU M.Ed (Master of Education) program for professional teachers. Eligibility, fee, admission process, and career advancement.

What is M.Ed Program?

M.Ed is a postgraduate professional degree for teachers who want to advance to senior positions like subject head, vice principal, or education officer.

Eligibility Criteria for M.Ed

- B.Ed from recognized university
- Minimum 45% marks in B.Ed
- Minimum 2 years teaching experience (preferred)
- Valid teaching license (if applicable)

M.Ed Fee Structure 2026

Program Duration: 1.5 years (3 semesters)
Admission Fee: Rs. 5,000
Tuition per semester: Rs. 7,000 × 3 = Rs. 21,000
Degree Fee: Rs. 2,000
Total Program Cost: Rs. 28,000

M.Ed Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Visit AIOU admission portal
Step 2: Select "M.Ed Program 2026"
Step 3: Fill application with B.Ed details
Step 4: Upload B.Ed degree and experience certificate
Step 5: Pay admission fee (Rs. 5,000)
Step 6: Submit application

M.Ed Program Structure

Semester 1: Educational Leadership, Curriculum Development
Semester 2: Educational Research Methods, Teacher Supervision
Semester 3: Thesis/Research Project, Education Policy Analysis

Career Opportunities After M.Ed

Senior Teacher: Rs. 60,000-100,000/month
Subject Head/Coordinator: Rs. 80,000-120,000/month
Vice Principal: Rs. 100,000-150,000/month
Education Officer: Rs. 80,000-150,000/month (government)
Teacher Trainer: Rs. 70,000-120,000/month
Educational Consultant: Rs. 100,000-200,000/month

Further Studies After M.Ed

✅ MPhil Education (2 years) – For lecturer positions in colleges
✅ PhD Education (3-5 years) – For professor and researcher
✅ Educational Leadership Certifications – For principal roles

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do M.Ed without B.Ed?

No. B.Ed is mandatory for M.Ed admission. You cannot skip B.Ed.

Is AIOU M.Ed degree valid for principal jobs?

Yes, M.Ed from AIOU is HEC recognized and accepted for principal and senior education officer positions.
