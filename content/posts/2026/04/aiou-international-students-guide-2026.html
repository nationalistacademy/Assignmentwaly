AIOU International Students Guide 2026 – Admission for Foreigners

Complete guide for international students wanting to study at AIOU. Admission process, fee in USD, visa requirements, and distance learning from abroad.

Who Can Apply as International Student?

International students are non-Pakistani citizens. This includes:
- Students from Afghanistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, etc.
- Students from Middle East (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain)
- Students from Africa, Europe, USA, Canada, Australia
- Pakistani citizens living abroad (use regular admission)

Programs Available for International Students

  • BA (2 years) – For foreign students
  • MA (2 years) – Various subjects
  • BS (4 years) – Computer Science, Economics, Business
  • B.Ed (1.5/2.5 years) – For teaching careers
  • MPhil (2 years) – Research degrees
  • Certificate and Diploma courses (6 months-1 year)

Eligibility for International Students

- Equivalent qualification from home country
- Attested documents from education board of home country
- English proficiency (if not from English-medium)
- Valid passport
- No criminal record certificate

Fee Structure for International Students (USD)

Admission Fee (one time): $100
BA Program (2 years): $500 per year
MA Program (2 years): $600 per year
BS Program (4 years): $500 per semester
B.Ed Program: $400 per semester
MPhil Program: $800 per semester
Courier of degree: $50 (international shipping)

International Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Visit international.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Download international application form
Step 3: Fill form with your details
Step 4: Attest documents from:
- Your home country's education board
- Pakistan embassy in your country
Step 5: Pay fee via bank transfer (USD)
Step 6: Email scanned documents to international@aiou.edu.pk
Step 7: Original documents courier to AIOU Islamabad

Visa Requirements for On-Campus Study

If you want to study in Pakistan (not distance learning):
- Student visa from Pakistan embassy in your country
- AIOU admission letter (original)
- Passport valid for 6+ months
- Bank statement ($5,000 minimum)
- Medical certificate
- Police clearance certificate

Distance Learning from Your Country

Most international students study via distance mode:
- Receive study materials online (PDF)
- Submit assignments online via email
- Take exams at Pakistan embassy in your country
- Degree couriered to your address

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AIOU degree valid in my country?

AIOU is HEC recognized. You will need to get degree equivalence from your country's education authority.

Can I pay fee in my local currency?

No. All international fees must be paid in USD via international bank transfer.
