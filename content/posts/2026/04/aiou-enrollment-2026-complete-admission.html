🎓 Programs Offered

AIOU offers programs across all levels, from Matric to PhD, in two admission phases:

  • Phase 1 (Deadline: Feb 15, 2026) – Matric (SSC), FA/FSc, M.Phil, PhD.
  • Phase 2 (Deadline: April 1, 2026) – ADE, B.Ed, BA (Associate Degree), BS, MA/MSc, Postgraduate Diplomas.

Popular programs: Matric, FA/FSc, I.Com, Associate Degree, B.Ed (1.5/2.5/4 years), BS (CS, Economics, English, Psychology), MA/MSc, M.Phil, PhD.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • Matric: Middle pass from recognized institution.
  • Intermediate (FA/FSc/I.Com): Matric with min 33%.
  • Bachelor's (BA/BSc/BS): FA/FSc/I.Com with at least 2nd division (45%).
  • Master's (MA/MSc): 14-year bachelor's degree with min 45%.
  • M.Phil/MS: 16-year education with min 50% + AIOU GAT (General) or NTS GAT 50%.
  • PhD: Relevant master's/M.Phil with CGPA 3.0+ and AIOU GAT (Subject).

💻 Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  • Step 1: Visit aiou.edu.pk → "Admissions" → "Admission OAS".
  • Step 2: Click "Application for New Admission". Create account with email and phone.
  • Step 3: Login → "Apply for a New Program". Choose program, semester, study mode.
  • Step 4: Fill personal, academic, and contact details carefully.
  • Step 5: Upload scanned CNIC, previous certificates, photo.
  • Step 6: Review and submit.
  • Step 7: Generate fee challan. Pay at HBL, NBP, UBL, Easypaisa, or JazzCash.
  • Step 8: After payment, status changes to "Paid". Save application number.

📄 Required Documents

  • ✅ Attested CNIC copy (student + father/guardian)
  • ✅ Recent passport-size photos (2-4)
  • ✅ Attested previous educational certificates
  • ✅ B-Form (if under 18)
  • ✅ Domicile (only for quota)
  • ✅ Disability certificate (if applicable)

Note: NOC not required. Domicile not mandatory for general admission.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📌 Q1: Can I apply for two programs simultaneously?

Yes, separate forms and fees for each.

📌 Q2: Is there an entry test for all programs?

No, only for M.Phil/MS and PhD.

📌 Q3: How to check admission status?

Login to OAS portal. Updates appear within 4-6 weeks after deadline.

📌 Q4: Can I get a scholarship?

Yes, need-based and merit-based scholarships available. Apply at regional office after enrollment.

📌 Q5: Is AIOU degree valid for government jobs?

Absolutely. AIOU is HEC-recognized and accepted for FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, etc.

📌 Q6: What is the passing percentage?

Minimum 40% in assignments AND final exams separately.

📚 AIOU Assignment Submission Guidelines (Spring 2026)

To avoid getting your assignments rejected by your tutor, you must carefully follow the submission rules set by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU):

  • Matric, FA, BA/B.Com: Must be written by hand on blank single-sided pages. Staple three copies of the official Assignment Pertal (پرتال) form and send them via Pakistan Post or TCS directly to your tutor's address before the deadline.
