AIOU Improvement Exam 2026 – Rules, Fees & Registration

Complete guide to AIOU improvement exam 2026. Who can apply, fee structure, registration process, and how to improve your grades.

What is Improvement Exam?

If you scored low marks (below 60%) in any subject, you can take improvement exam to get better grade. Your new score replaces the old one.

Who Can Apply for Improvement Exam?

  • Students who passed but want higher marks (C to B, B to A, etc.)
  • Students who failed and want to pass
  • Students absent from original exam
  • Any student with result below 60%

Improvement Exam Schedule 2026

For Spring 2026 Exams:
Registration: October 1-30, 2026
Exams: December 15-30, 2026
Result: February 2027

For Autumn 2026 Exams:
Registration: April 1-30, 2027
Exams: June 15-30, 2027
Result: August 2027

Improvement Exam Fee Structure

Per subject fee: Rs. 1,500
Maximum 4 subjects per attempt
Total maximum fee: Rs. 6,000
Processing fee: Rs. 500 (one time)

How to Register for Improvement Exam

Step 1: Visit improvement.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Enter your Roll Number
Step 3: Select subjects you want to improve
Step 4: Pay fee online or via challan
Step 5: Download roll slip before exam

Important Rules for Improvement Exam

  • You can attempt improvement exam maximum 2 times per subject
  • Higher score will be kept (even if you score lower second time)
  • Improvement result takes 60 days to release
  • Original degree will be updated with new grades

What Subjects Can You Improve?

✅ Any theory subject
✅ Practical subjects (with lab attendance proof)
✅ Compulsory subjects (English, Islamiat, Pak Studies)
❌ Projects and thesis (cannot be improved)
❌ Workshop attendance (cannot be improved)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I improve my assignment marks?

No. Assignment marks cannot be improved. Only exam marks can be improved.

Will my old result be deleted after improvement?

Old result stays in record but new higher score is counted for final degree.
