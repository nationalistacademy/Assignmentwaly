📝 7 Easy Steps to Upload (No Tech Skills Needed)

🔹 STEP 1 – Log in to Aaghi LMS

Go to https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk. Enter your enrolment number (e.g., 0000‑1234567) and password (default is CNIC or as given). Click “Login”. Forgot password? Use “Forgot Password” link.

🔹 STEP 2 – Find Your Course

From the dashboard, click “My Courses”. A list of your Spring 2026 courses appears. Click on the course title (e.g., “417 – Pakistan Studies”).

🔹 STEP 3 – Go to Assignments Section

Look for “Assignments” or “TMA” tab. You’ll see Assignment 1 and Assignment 2.

🔹 STEP 4 – Click “Add Submission”

Next to the assignment you want to submit, click the “Add Submission” button. A file upload area opens.

🔹 STEP 5 – Upload Your PDF

Click “Choose File” → select your PDF (max 10MB). Then click “Upload this file” or “Save Changes”.

🔹 STEP 6 – Verify Upload

After upload, click on the file link to open it. Make sure it displays correctly. If it’s garbled, re‑upload.

🔹 STEP 7 – Check Submission Status

Return to the assignments page. Status should change from “Not submitted” to “Submitted” or “Pending grading”. Take a screenshot for proof.

❌ 5 Common Upload Errors & Instant Fixes

  • “File too large” → Compress PDF using Smallpdf or ILovePDF (keep under 10MB).
  • “Invalid file type” → Convert Word/JPEG to PDF (use “Save as PDF” or free converter).
  • “Deadline passed” → Sorry, LMS is closed. Next time, order urgent solved assignment from us.
  • “Course not visible” → You may not be enrolled for Spring 2026. Check admission status.
  • “Login failed” → Reset password via “Forgot Password” or call AIOU helpline.
