Answer:

"He said, 'I am going to Lahore tomorrow. Will you come with me?' 'No,' I said, 'I have some work.'"

💡 Punctuation Rules Applied: Capital letter at the beginning, comma to separate speaker from quote, period at end of statement, question mark for question, quotation marks around direct speech.

❓ Question 2: Change the voice of the following sentences

Answer:

Active VoicePassive Voice
She writes a letter. – A letter is written by her. –
They are playing cricket. – Cricket is being played by them. –
The boy broke the window. – The window was broken by the boy. –
I will finish the work. – The work will be finished by me. –
Help the poor. – The poor should be helped. –

❓ Question 3: Change the following sentences into Indirect Speech

Answer:

Direct SpeechIndirect Speech
He said, "I am happy." – He said that he was happy. –
She said, "Will you come to the party?" – She asked if I would come to the party. –
The teacher said, "Work hard." – The teacher advised us to work hard. –
He said, "I have completed my homework." – He said that he had completed his homework. –
They said, "We were playing cricket." – They said that they had been playing cricket. –

❓ Question 5: Write an essay on "My Aim in Life"

Answer:

An aim is a goal or target that a person wants to achieve in life. Without a clear aim, life becomes like a ship without a rudder. My aim in life is to become a doctor.

I want to become a doctor because I want to serve humanity. In our country, many people suffer from diseases because they cannot afford proper medical treatment. I want to help such people by providing free medical care. Doctors are respected in society because they save lives and relieve pain.

To achieve my aim, I work very hard. I study science subjects like Biology, Chemistry, and Physics with full concentration. After matriculation, I will join F.Sc (Pre-Medical) and then appear in the Medical College Entrance Test (MDCAT). After getting admission in a medical college, I will study hard for five years to become a qualified doctor.

I know that becoming a doctor is not easy. It requires hard work, dedication, and patience. But I am determined to achieve my aim. With the blessings of Allah and the support of my parents and teachers, I will become a doctor one day.

❓ Question 7: Write a story with the moral "Slow and Steady Wins the Race"

Answer:

The Hare and the Tortoise

Once upon a time, a hare was very proud of his speed. He always laughed at the tortoise for moving slowly. One day, the tortoise challenged the hare to a race. The hare laughed loudly and accepted.

The race began. The hare ran very fast and soon disappeared. He looked back and saw the tortoise was far behind. He thought, "Let me take a nap under this tree." He fell asleep. The tortoise kept walking slowly but steadily. He did not stop. When the hare woke up, he saw the tortoise near the finishing line. He ran fast, but it was too late. The tortoise won the race.

Moral: Slow and Steady Wins the Race.
