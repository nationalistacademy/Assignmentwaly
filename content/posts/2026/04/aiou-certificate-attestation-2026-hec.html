AIOU Certificate Attestation 2026 – HEC, IBCC, Foreign Office Guide

Complete guide to AIOU certificate attestation for jobs and foreign education. HEC attestation, IBCC equivalence, and Foreign Office process.

What is Certificate Attestation?

Attestation is official verification that your degree is genuine. Required for government jobs, foreign jobs, and higher education abroad.

Types of Attestation for AIOU Degrees

1. HEC Attestation (For University Jobs & Foreign Studies)

HEC (Higher Education Commission) attestation is required for:
- Lecturer/Professor jobs in colleges/universities
- Master's/PhD admission in Pakistani universities
- Foreign university admission (part of process)

2. IBCC Equivalence (For O/A Level Students)

If you have O/A Level or foreign qualification, IBCC issues equivalence certificate for AIOU admission.

3. Foreign Office Attestation (For International Use)

MOFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attestation required for:
- Jobs in foreign countries (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, etc.)
- Visa applications
- Embassy legalization

4. Embassy Attestation (For Specific Country)

After MOFA, the destination country's embassy attests your degree.

HEC Attestation Process for AIOU Degree

Online Process (Recommended):
Step 1: Visit attestation.hec.gov.pk
Step 2: Create account with CNIC and email
Step 3: Fill online attestation application
Step 4: Upload scanned copies of degree and CNIC
Step 5: Pay fee online (Rs. 1,500 per degree)
Step 6: Courier original documents to HEC
Step 7: Receive attested degree in 10-15 days

Physical Process (Regional Office):
Step 1: Visit HEC regional office (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi)
Step 2: Submit original degree + 2 copies
Step 3: Pay fee at bank counter
Step 4: Collect attested degree after 7 days

IBCC Equivalence for O/A Level Students

If you want to take admission in AIOU with O/A Level:
Step 1: Visit ibcc.edu.pk
Step 2: Apply for equivalence certificate
Step 3: Submit O/A Level certificates and result sheets
Step 4: Pay fee (Rs. 2,500 for O Level, Rs. 3,500 for A Level)
Step 5: Receive equivalence in 15 days

MOFA (Foreign Office) Attestation

After HEC Attestation (Mandatory first):
Step 1: Visit Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Islamabad (or regional camp office)
Step 2: Submit HEC-attested degree
Step 3: Pay fee (Rs. 1,000 per degree)
Step 4: Receive MOFA attestation in 5-7 days

Embassy Attestation (For Specific Country)

After MOFA, visit embassy of your destination country:
- UAE Embassy: Rs. 5,000, 7 days
- Saudi Embassy: Rs. 4,000, 10 days
- Qatar Embassy: Rs. 6,000, 7 days
- UK/USA/Canada: Not required (use HEC + MOFA only)

Documents Required for Attestation

✅ Original AIOU degree
✅ 2 attested copies of degree
✅ CNIC copy (student)
✅ Passport copy (for foreign attestation)
✅ All semester result cards
✅ Fee payment receipt
✅ Job offer letter (for employment attestation)

Attestation Fee Summary 2026

  • HEC Attestation: Rs. 1,500 per degree
  • IBCC Equivalence: Rs. 2,500-3,500
  • MOFA Attestation: Rs. 1,000 per degree
  • Embassy Attestation: Rs. 4,000-10,000 (varies by country)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HEC attestation mandatory for all jobs?

No. Only for university lecturer jobs and foreign employment. Private sector jobs usually don't require attestation.

Can I get same-day attestation?

No. Minimum 5-7 days for HEC, 10-15 days for complete process. Plan ahead.
