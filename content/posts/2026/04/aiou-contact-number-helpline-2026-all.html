How to Register a Complaint

Method 1: AIOU Complaint Portal
Visit complaint.aiou.edu.pk → Fill form → Get tracking ID → Check status online

Method 2: Email Complaint
Send email to complaints@aiou.edu.pk with your roll number and issue details

Method 3: Physical Complaint
Visit regional office complaint cell → Submit written application → Get receipt

Complaint Resolution Time

  • Simple queries: 2-3 working days
  • Admission issues: 7-10 working days
  • Exam/Result issues: 15-20 working days
  • Degree issues: 30-45 working days

Best Time to Call AIOU

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM
Friday: 9:00 AM - 12:30 PM (then 2:30 PM - 4:00 PM)
Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday (less rush)
Avoid: Monday morning and Friday afternoon

Tips for Calling AIOU Helpline

✅ Have your roll number ready before calling
✅ Write down your question before dialing
✅ Call between 10 AM - 12 PM (less waiting)
✅ Take name of representative for follow-up
✅ If line busy, try after 30 minutes

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AIOU have WhatsApp support?

No official WhatsApp support. Use SMS 8008 or call helpline for queries.

What is AIOU SMS code for results?

Send "AIOU [Roll Number]" to 8583 for result. Send "ROLL [CNIC]" to 8008 for roll slip.
