📊 How to Check AIOU Result Online & Via SMS 2026

📅 Updated: April 12, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 7 min read 📚 Category: AIOU Resources ⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

Waiting for your AIOU exam results can be stressful. Whether you've appeared for BA, BSc, MA, MSc, B.Ed, or any other program, knowing how and where to check your result saves time and reduces anxiety.

In this complete guide, we'll walk you through every method to check your AIOU result — online via the official portal, SMS to 8008, and through regional offices. We'll also explain how to understand your result marksheet and what to do if you face any issues.

📌 Did You Know? AIOU usually announces results 60-90 days after the final exams. Spring semester results (exams in June) are typically announced in August-September, while Autumn semester results (exams in December) are announced in February-March.
🔍 4 Methods to Check AIOU Result
💻 Method 1: Check AIOU Result Online (Official Website)

The most reliable and detailed method is through AIOU's official result portal. You can view your full marksheet with subject-wise grades.

📝 Step-by-Step Process:
  • Visit the official AIOU result portal: https://www.aiou.edu.pk/Results
  • Select your Program Level (Matric, FA, BA, BSc, MA, MSc, B.Ed, M.Ed, etc.)
  • Select your Semester (Spring or Autumn) and Year
  • Enter your Roll Number (found on your roll number slip)
  • Enter the Captcha Code shown on screen
  • Click the "Submit" or "Get Result" button
  • Your result will appear on screen showing: Student Name, Father's Name, Course Codes, Theory Marks, Practical Marks, Total Marks, Grade, and Status (Pass/Fail)
  • You can download and print the result for future reference
🔗 Direct Result Portal Link: https://www.aiou.edu.pk/Results
✅ Pro Tip: Bookmark the result portal on your browser for quick access. Results are usually uploaded by 10:00 AM on announcement day.
📱 Method 2: Check AIOU Result via SMS (8008)

No internet? No problem! AIOU offers a fast SMS service to check results. This works on all mobile networks (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone).

Type: RESULT (space) Roll Number
Send to: 8008

Example: If your roll number is 123456789, type RESULT 123456789 and send to 8008.

You will receive an SMS reply with:

  • Student Name
  • Roll Number
  • Program Name
  • Total Obtained Marks / Total Marks
  • Percentage
  • Grade
  • Status (Pass/Fail)
⚠️ Important: Standard SMS charges apply (approx. Rs. 2-5 per SMS). Ensure you have sufficient balance before sending.
📚 Method 3: Check Result via AIOU Aaghi Portal (LMS)

The Aaghi Learning Management System (LMS) also displays your results once announced. This method also shows your assignment marks separately.

  • Visit the Aaghi Portal: https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk
  • Login with your Roll Number and Password
  • Navigate to "My Results" or "Examination" section
  • Select the relevant semester and course
  • Your result including assignment marks, practical marks, and theory marks will be displayed
📌 Note: The Aaghi Portal shows more detailed results than the public result portal, including assignment breakdowns. Highly recommended for students who want to see their complete performance.
🏢 Method 4: Check Result at AIOU Regional Office

If you prefer physical assistance or have trouble with online methods, you can visit your nearest AIOU regional office.

  • Visit your nearest AIOU Regional Office (list of major offices below)
  • Go to the "Examination" or "Student Services" counter
  • Provide your Roll Number and CNIC
  • The staff will print your result or provide a verification letter
  • Small fee may apply for printed copies (approx. Rs. 50-100)

❌ Common Issues & Solutions
🔴 Problem: "Result Not Found" Error

Solution: This usually means your result hasn't been uploaded yet. Wait 2-3 days and try again. If still not found, verify your roll number is correct (check your roll number slip). Contact your regional office if the problem persists.

🔴 Problem: SMS Not Working (8008)

Solution: Ensure correct format: RESULT [Roll Number] (space between RESULT and roll number). Check your balance. Try sending from a different mobile network. Use online method as alternative.

🔴 Problem: Forgot Roll Number

Solution: Check your roll number slip, admission form, or old result cards. You can also send ROLL [CNIC Number] to 8008 to retrieve your roll number. Alternatively, contact your regional office.

🔴 Problem: Result Shows "Fail" But I Think I Passed

Solution: Request a rechecking/re-evaluation of your papers. Submit an application to the Examination Department within 15 days of result announcement. Fee: approx. Rs. 500-1,000 per paper.

🔴 Problem: Result Portal Not Loading

Solution: High traffic on result day is common. Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late night). Use SMS method for quick results. Clear your browser cache or try a different browser.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: How long after exams does AIOU announce results?

Typically 60-90 days after the last exam. For semester systems, results come in 2-3 months. For annual systems, results take 3-4 months.

📌 Q2: Can I check my AIOU result without roll number?

No, the roll number is mandatory. If you've lost it, send ROLL [CNIC Number] to 8008 to retrieve your roll number first.

📌 Q3: Is the SMS result service free?

No, standard SMS charges apply (Rs. 2-5 per SMS depending on your network). Online checking is completely free.

📌 Q4: What do I do if I fail one subject?

You will need to appear for a supplementary exam for that subject. Apply for the supplementary exam when announced. You don't need to repeat the entire year/semester.

📌 Q5: Can I get my original marksheet online?

The online result is an unofficial copy. For official marksheets, you must apply to the Examination Department and pay the prescribed fee (Rs. 500-1,500 per copy).

📌 Q6: How are assignment marks included in the final result?

Assignment marks typically count for 30-40% of your final grade. Theory exam marks count for 60-70%. Both are combined to calculate your final result.

📌 Q7: What is the passing percentage in AIOU?

Minimum 40% marks are required to pass each subject. For some professional programs (like B.Ed, M.Ed), the passing percentage may be 50%.
