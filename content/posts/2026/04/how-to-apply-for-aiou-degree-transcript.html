📜 How to Apply for AIOU Degree Transcript 2026 – Complete Guide

📅 Updated: April 12, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 8 min read 📚 Category: AIOU Resources ⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

After successfully completing your program from Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU), the next crucial step is obtaining your official transcript (Detailed Marks Certificate / DMC). Whether you're applying for a job, seeking admission to a foreign university, or applying for a visa, your academic transcript is an essential document.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain everything you need to know about applying for an AIOU transcript — from application methods and required documents to fee structure, processing time, and tracking your application.

📌 What is a Transcript (DMC)? A transcript or Detailed Marks Certificate (DMC) is an official document that lists all courses you studied, your semester-wise marks, overall CGPA, and total percentage. It's different from your degree certificate (which only confirms completion).
📋 When Do You Need an AIOU Transcript?
  • 🎓 Higher Education Abroad: For admission to foreign universities (MS, PhD, etc.)
  • 💼 Job Applications: Government and private sector jobs require official transcripts
  • 🛂 Visa Applications: Study or work visa applications need academic records
  • 🏛️ HEC Attestation: Required for degree attestation from HEC
  • 📊 Professional Certifications: Many professional bodies require transcripts
  • 🔄 Credit Transfer: If transferring to another university
🔍 2 Methods to Apply for AIOU Transcript
💻 Method 1: Apply Online for AIOU Transcript (Recommended)

The easiest and fastest way to apply for your transcript is through AIOU's online transcript portal. You can apply from anywhere in the world.

📝 Step-by-Step Process:
  • Visit the official AIOU Transcript Portal: https://www.aiou.edu.pk/Transcript
  • Click on "Apply for Transcript / DMC"
  • Fill out the online application form with:
    • Full Name (as per matriculation certificate)
    • Father's Name
    • Roll Number
    • Program Name (BA, BSc, MA, MSc, B.Ed, etc.)
    • Year of Passing / Semester
    • CNIC Number
    • Mobile Number
    • Postal Address (for delivery)
    • Number of copies required
  • Upload scanned copies of required documents (see list below)
  • Pay the transcript fee online (via credit card, bank transfer, or Easypaisa/JazzCash)
  • Submit the application and save the application tracking number
  • Your transcript will be delivered to your postal address within 15-30 working days
🔗 Direct Transcript Portal: https://www.aiou.edu.pk/Transcript
✅ Pro Tip: After submitting, save the tracking number and screenshot of the application. You'll need it to check status or follow up.
📮 Method 2: Apply Offline at AIOU Regional Office

If you prefer physical submission or have trouble with online payment, you can apply at any AIOU regional office.

  • Visit your nearest AIOU Regional Office (addresses listed below)
  • Get the "Transcript Application Form" from the Examination/Student Services counter
  • Fill out the form completely (use block letters)
  • Attach attested copies of required documents
  • Pay the fee via bank challan or at the office cash counter
  • Submit the form and get a receipt/acknowledgment slip
  • Collect your transcript from the same office or request delivery by courier (additional fee)
⚠️ Note: Offline applications typically take longer (20-40 working days) than online applications. Online is strongly recommended for faster processing.
📋 Required Documents for Transcript Application
  • ✅ Copy of CNIC (Student's own CNIC - attested)
  • ✅ Copy of Roll Number Slip (any semester)
  • ✅ Copy of Matric Certificate (for name verification)
  • ✅ Passport-size photographs (2 copies) - for offline only
  • ✅ Previous DMC/Transcript (if applying for additional copies)
  • ✅ Fee Payment Receipt/Challan (proof of payment)
  • ✅ Application Form (filled and signed)

🌍 International Transcript Application (For Students Abroad)

If you're living outside Pakistan and need your AIOU transcript, follow these steps:

  • Online Application: Use the online transcript portal as described above. Select "International Delivery" option.
  • Fee Payment: Pay via international credit card or bank transfer (USD equivalent).
  • Courier Delivery: Transcripts are sent via DHL, TCS, or Pakistan Post International. Additional courier charges apply (approx. Rs. 3,000 - 5,000).
  • Processing Time: 25-40 working days (including international shipping).
  • Attestation: For use abroad, you may also need HEC and embassy attestation (separate process).
⚠️ Important for International Students: Always apply at least 3 months before your university application deadline to account for processing and shipping delays.

🔍 How to Track Your Transcript Application
  • Online Tracking: Visit the AIOU Transcript Portal and enter your Application Tracking Number (provided after submission).
  • SMS Tracking: Send TRACK [Application Number] to 8008 to get status update.
  • Email Inquiry: Send an email to transcripts@aiou.edu.pk with your tracking number and roll number.
  • Phone Inquiry: Call AIOU Helpline at 051-111-112-468 (Extension 4 for Examinations) and provide your tracking number.
❌ Common Problems & Solutions
🔴 Problem: Transcript Not Received After 30 Days

Solution: Check tracking status online. Call the Examination Department at 051-111-112-468 (Ext 4). If lost in courier, you may need to apply for a duplicate (with additional fee).

🔴 Problem: Name Mismatch on Transcript

Solution: Name mismatches between CNIC and academic records are common. Submit a correction application with:

  • Copy of CNIC
  • Copy of Matric certificate
  • Affidavit on stamp paper (Rs. 50-100)
  • Correction fee (approx. Rs. 500-1,000)

🔴 Problem: Online Portal Not Working

Solution: Try during off-peak hours (early morning). Clear browser cache. Use Google Chrome or Firefox. If still not working, apply offline at regional office.

🔴 Problem: Lost Original Transcript

Solution: Apply for a Duplicate Transcript. You'll need:

  • Police report (if lost/stolen)
  • Affidavit on stamp paper
  • Copy of CNIC
  • Duplicate fee (Rs. 3,000 - 4,000)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: How many copies of transcript should I order?

Order at least 3-5 copies when you apply. Additional copies cost less than ordering separately later. Keep 1 copy for yourself, and use others for different applications.

📌 Q2: Is transcript different from degree certificate?

Yes! Transcript/DMC shows all subjects with semester-wise marks and CGPA. Degree Certificate only confirms program completion. Most employers and universities need BOTH.

📌 Q3: Can I get my transcript attested by HEC?

Yes, but you need to apply to HEC separately. AIOU transcript must be original (not photocopy) for HEC attestation. HEC attestation fee is additional (approx. Rs. 2,000 - 5,000).

📌 Q4: How long is the transcript valid for?

Official transcripts have no expiration date. They are valid forever. However, some employers may request recent transcripts (issued within last 6 months) for verification purposes.

📌 Q5: Can I apply for a transcript before completing my degree?

Yes, you can apply for a provisional transcript for completed semesters. Final transcript is only issued after full program completion.

📌 Q6: What is the difference between normal and urgent processing?

Urgent processing prioritizes your application and reduces processing time from 30 days to 5-10 days. Fee is higher (approx. Rs. 3,000 - 5,000).

📌 Q7: Can someone else collect my transcript on my behalf?

Yes, but they need an authorization letter signed by you, plus a copy of your CNIC and their original CNIC for identification.

📌 Q8: I passed in 1995. Can I still get a transcript?

Yes! AIOU maintains records for all past students. However, processing may take longer (45-60 days) as old records need to be retrieved from archives.
