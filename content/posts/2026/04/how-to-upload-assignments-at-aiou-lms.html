📤 How to Upload Assignments at AIOU LMS Portal (New)

✍️ By Assignmentwaly 📅 Updated: April 13, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read 🎓 For AIOU Students

So AIOU finally upgraded their LMS portal. New look, new features, and honestly? A bit confusing at first. I've seen so many students struggling to find where to upload assignments, getting error messages, or accidentally submitting the wrong file.

After helping dozens of students navigate the new portal, I put together this step-by-step visual guide (no screenshots, but I'll describe exactly what you'll see). Follow these steps carefully, and you'll upload your assignment in under 5 minutes.

📌 Before You Start: Make sure your assignment is saved as a PDF file (not Word, not images). Name it properly: CourseCode_RollNumber_Name.pdf (example: 1429_123456789_Ali.pdf). This helps tutors identify your work easily.

🔐 Step 1: Login to the New Aaghi LMS Portal

1
Access the Portal

Open your browser and go to: https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk

You'll see the new login interface — cleaner design with a blue/white theme.

Login Credentials:

  • Username: Your Roll Number (e.g., 123456789)
  • Password: Your CNIC without dashes (e.g., 1234567890123) OR the password you set during first login
⚠️ Can't login? Click "Forgot Password" or call AIOU helpline at 051-111-112-468. Don't keep trying multiple times — your account might get locked.

🏠 Step 2: Navigate to Your Dashboard

2
Find Your Courses

After login, you'll land on the Dashboard. Look for a section called "My Courses" or "Enrolled Courses". It usually shows all the courses you're registered for in the current semester.

You'll see each course card with:

  • Course Code (e.g., 1429, 1430, 8608)
  • Course Title (e.g., Business Mathematics, Teaching Practice)
  • Course Teacher/Tutor Name

Click on the course for which you want to submit an assignment.

📂 Step 3: Find the Assignment Submission Section

3
Locate "Assignments" or "Activities"

Inside your course page, look for a menu or tab labeled:

  • "Assignments" (most common)
  • "Activities"
  • "Course Content" → then "Assignments"

In the new LMS, assignments are usually listed in a table format showing:

  • Assignment Number (Assignment 1, Assignment 2, etc.)
  • Due Date
  • Status (Not Submitted / Submitted / Graded)
  • "Add Submission" or "Upload" button
💡 Pro Tip: If you don't see the "Add Submission" button, the submission window might not be open yet, or the deadline has passed. Check the due date column.

📎 Step 4: Upload Your Assignment File

4
Choose and Upload File

Click the "Add Submission" button. A new window or section will appear.

What you'll see: A file picker area that says "Choose File" or "Drag and drop file here".

Accepted file formats: Usually PDF only. Some courses may accept DOC/DOCX, but PDF is safest.

Maximum file size: Usually 10MB to 20MB. If your file is larger, compress it using online tools like SmallPDF or ILovePDF.

✅ How to upload: Click "Choose File" → Select your assignment PDF from your computer/phone → The file name will appear → Click "Save Changes" or "Upload Assignment".

✅ Step 5: Confirm Submission

5
Check for Confirmation Message

After clicking upload, wait for the confirmation. You should see:

  • A green success message: "Submission successful" or "Assignment uploaded"
  • The status changes from "Not Submitted" to "Submitted"
  • You may see the date and time of your submission
⚠️ Important: If you DON'T see a confirmation message, your assignment was NOT uploaded. Refresh the page and try again. Don't assume it worked — always verify!

📸 Step 6: Take a Screenshot (Save Proof)

6
Document Your Submission

This is the most important step that students skip. Take a screenshot of the confirmation page showing:

  • Course name and code
  • Assignment number
  • Submission date and time
  • "Submitted" status

Save this screenshot on your phone or computer. If the portal ever has a glitch and your submission disappears, you have proof that you uploaded on time.

📌 Real story: One student last semester lost 15 marks because the portal didn't save her submission. She had no proof. Don't be that student. Screenshot EVERY submission.

❌ Common Errors & How to Fix Them

❌ Error: "File type not allowed"

Convert your file to PDF. Use Word → Save As → PDF, or use free online converters.

❌ Error: "File size exceeds limit"

Compress your PDF. Use ilovepdf.com/compress_pdf or smallpdf.com/compress-pdf. Aim for under 5MB.

❌ Error: "Submission deadline passed"

Contact your tutor immediately. Some tutors accept late submissions with penalty. Don't wait.

❌ Error: "Page not loading" or "Server error"

Try during off-peak hours (early morning 6-8 AM or late night 10 PM-12 AM). Clear browser cache or try a different browser (Chrome or Firefox works best).

❌ Error: "No assignment submission available"

The tutor may not have opened the submission link yet. Check your tutor's instructions or contact them directly.

💡 Pro Tips for Stress-Free Submission

  • ✅ Don't wait until the last day. Upload at least 3 days before the deadline. Servers get slow on the last day.
  • ✅ Upload one assignment at a time. Don't rush through multiple submissions — each needs confirmation.
  • ✅ Use a computer if possible. Mobile browsers sometimes have issues with the portal.
  • ✅ Keep your file name simple. Use: CourseCode_RollNumber_Name.pdf (no spaces, no special characters).
  • ✅ Check your submission after 24 hours. Log back in to verify the file is still there and opens correctly.
  • ✅ Save your tutor's contact info. If something goes wrong, you can reach out directly.

📱 Can You Upload from Mobile?

Yes, the new LMS portal is mobile-friendly. However, I recommend using a computer for the first few submissions until you're comfortable. If you must use mobile:

  • Use Chrome or Firefox browser (not the in-app browser of WhatsApp/Facebook)
  • Make sure your PDF is saved on your phone (not just in Google Drive)
  • Use a stable WiFi connection
  • Switch to "Desktop Site" view in browser settings for better experience

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can I submit an assignment after the deadline?

Some tutors accept late submissions with a marks penalty (usually 5-10% deduction per day). Contact your tutor immediately. Never assume you can submit late without permission.

Q2: Can I replace a submitted file?

Yes, if the deadline hasn't passed. Go back to the assignment, click "Edit Submission" or "Remove" and upload the corrected file. After the deadline, changes are usually not allowed.

Q3: How do I know my assignment was received?

Check the status column — it should say "Submitted". Also check your email (some portals send confirmation emails). Take a screenshot for proof.

Q4: What if the portal is down on the deadline day?

Take screenshots showing the error or downtime. Email your assignment to your tutor directly with the screenshots as proof. Then upload when the portal is back up.

Q5: Can I submit handwritten assignments on the LMS?

Yes! Scan your handwritten pages using a phone scanner app (CamScanner, Microsoft Lens, or Google Drive Scan). Save as PDF and upload. Make sure the scan is clear and readable.

Q6: I submitted but the status still shows "Not Submitted" — what to do?

Wait 10-15 minutes and refresh. If still not showing, upload again. If the problem continues, email your assignment to your tutor and contact the LMS helpdesk.
