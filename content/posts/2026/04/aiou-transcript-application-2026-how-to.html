AIOU Transcript Application 2026 – How to Get Official Transcript

Complete guide to AIOU transcript application for jobs and further studies. Fee, processing time, and online application process for official transcript.

What is Official Transcript?

Transcript is your complete academic record. It shows:
- All subjects studied semester-wise
- Marks obtained in each subject
- Total percentage and CGPA
- Overall grade and division

When Do You Need Transcript?

  • Job applications in government/private sector
  • Applying for Master's or PhD abroad
  • Visa application for study/work
  • Professional certifications (PMP, ACCA, etc.)
  • HEC attestation requirement

How to Apply for Transcript Online

Step 1: Visit transcript.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Click "Apply for Transcript"
Step 3: Enter your Roll Number and CNIC
Step 4: Select number of copies (1-5 copies)
Step 5: Pay fee online via credit card or bank challan
Step 6: Select delivery method (pickup or courier)
Step 7: Submit and save application ID

Transcript Fee Structure 2026

Per transcript copy: Rs. 1,500
Additional copies (same application): Rs. 500 each
Courier delivery (within Pakistan): Rs. 500
Courier delivery (international): Rs. 5,000
Processing fee: Rs. 300 (one time)

Transcript Processing Time

Normal processing: 30-45 working days
Urgent processing (extra Rs. 2,000): 10-15 working days
Delivery time: 3-5 days (Pakistan), 15-20 days (international)

Documents Required for Transcript

✅ Copy of final degree (if issued)
✅ All semester result cards
✅ CNIC copy
✅ Passport size photo (2 copies)
✅ Fee paid challan copy
✅ Application form (downloaded after online apply)

How to Check Transcript Status

Visit transcript portal → Click "Check Status" → Enter Application ID → View status:
- Pending: Application received
- Processing: Under review
- Ready: Transcript printed, ready for pickup/shipment
- Dispatched: Sent via courier (tracking number provided)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is transcript different from degree?

Yes. Degree is a single certificate. Transcript is detailed mark sheet for all semesters.

Can someone else collect my transcript?

Yes, with your signed authorization letter and copy of your CNIC.
