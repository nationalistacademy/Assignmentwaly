AIOU B.Ed Program 2026 – Complete Guide for Teaching Career

Complete guide to AIOU B.Ed (Bachelor of Education) program. Admission, fee structure, eligibility, and how to become a professional teacher in Pakistan.

What is B.Ed Program?

B.Ed is a professional teaching degree required for school teaching jobs in government and private schools in Pakistan.

B.Ed Programs Offered by AIOU

B.Ed (1.5 Years): For MA/MSc graduates (fast track)
B.Ed (2.5 Years): For BA/BSc graduates
B.Ed (4 Years): For FA/FSc graduates (integrated program)

Eligibility Criteria

B.Ed (1.5 Years):
- MA/MSc/M.Com with minimum 45% marks
- No third division in any exam

B.Ed (2.5 Years):
- BA/BSc/B.Com with minimum 45% marks
- Teaching experience preferred but not mandatory

B.Ed (4 Years):
- FA/FSc/ICS/ICom with minimum 45% marks
- Age: 17-25 years

B.Ed Fee Structure 2026

B.Ed (1.5 Years – 3 semesters):
Admission Fee: Rs. 3,500
Tuition per semester: Rs. 5,000 × 3 = Rs. 15,000
Total: Rs. 18,500

B.Ed (2.5 Years – 5 semesters):
Admission Fee: Rs. 3,500
Tuition per semester: Rs. 5,000 × 5 = Rs. 25,000
Total: Rs. 28,500

B.Ed (4 Years – 8 semesters):
Admission Fee: Rs. 3,500
Tuition per semester: Rs. 5,000 × 8 = Rs. 40,000
Total: Rs. 43,500

B.Ed Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Visit admission.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Select your B.Ed program (1.5/2.5/4 year)
Step 3: Fill application form
Step 4: Upload educational documents
Step 5: Pay admission fee
Step 6: Submit and wait for confirmation

B.Ed Program Structure

Core Courses:
- Foundations of Education
- Educational Psychology
- Teaching Methodologies
- Classroom Management
- Assessment and Evaluation

Practical Components:
- Teaching Practicum (40 hours)
- Lesson Planning
- School Internship (8 weeks)
- Action Research Project

Teaching Jobs After B.Ed

Government Schools: PST, EST, SST (Rs. 35,000-80,000/month)
Private Schools: Teacher (Rs. 25,000-60,000/month)
International Schools: Rs. 50,000-150,000/month
Home Tutoring: Rs. 15,000-50,000/month part-time

Required Exams for Teaching Jobs

NTS (National Testing Service): Required for most government teaching jobs
SPSC/PPSC/FPSC: For permanent teaching positions
Subject Specialist Test: For higher grades (SST, Lecturer)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is B.Ed mandatory for teaching jobs in Pakistan?

Yes, B.Ed is now mandatory for all school teaching positions in government and most private schools.

Can I do B.Ed online from AIOU?

AIOU B.Ed is distance learning with workshop attendance. Not fully online but flexible for working professionals.
