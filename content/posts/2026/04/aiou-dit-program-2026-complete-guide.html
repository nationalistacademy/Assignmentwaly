AIOU DIT Program 2026 – Complete Guide for IT Careers

Complete guide to AIOU DIT (Diploma in Information Technology) program. Admission process, fee, syllabus, and IT job opportunities after DIT.

What is DIT Program?

DIT (Diploma in Information Technology) is a 1-year professional diploma that teaches practical IT skills for office and computer jobs.

Why Choose DIT at AIOU?

  • Only 1 year duration (faster than BS CS)
  • Practical skills focus (not just theory)
  • Low fee (Rs. 13,000 total)
  • Job ready immediately after completion
  • Can upgrade to BS CS later (credit transfer)

DIT Syllabus 2026 – What You Will Learn

Semester 1 Courses:
- Introduction to Computers (Windows, MS Office)
- Typing & Document Processing (MS Word)
- Spreadsheet & Data Analysis (MS Excel)
- Internet & Email Basics

Semester 2 Courses:
- Graphic Design Basics (Canva, Photoshop)
- Web Design Fundamentals (HTML, CSS)
- Database Management (MS Access)
- IT Project (Practical assignment)

Eligibility for DIT Program

- Matric/FA/FSc/ICS pass from recognized board
- Minimum 33% marks in previous exam
- Basic computer knowledge preferred (not mandatory)
- No age limit

DIT Fee Structure 2026

Admission Fee: Rs. 3,000 (one time)
Tuition Fee Semester 1: Rs. 4,000
Tuition Fee Semester 2: Rs. 4,000
Exam Fee Total: Rs. 2,000
Total Program Cost: Rs. 13,000

How to Apply for DIT at AIOU

Step 1: Visit admission.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Search "Diploma in Information Technology"
Step 3: Fill online application
Step 4: Upload Matric certificate and CNIC
Step 5: Pay Rs. 3,000 admission fee
Step 6: Submit application

Jobs After DIT Diploma

Immediate Jobs (Salary Rs. 25,000-50,000):
- Computer Operator
- Data Entry Operator
- IT Support Assistant
- Office Assistant
- Junior Web Designer

Freelance Opportunities (Online Earnings):
- Data entry on Upwork/Fiverr ($5-15/hour)
- MS Word formatting ($10-30/page)
- Excel data analysis ($20-50/project)
- Canva graphic design ($10-50/design)

Further Studies After DIT

Short Term (1-2 years):
- Advance Diploma in Web Development
- Certificate in Python/JavaScript
- Graphic Design Specialization

Long Term (4 years):
- BS Computer Science (credit transfer – skip 1st year)
- BS Information Technology
- BBA (Business + IT)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DIT equal to ICS?

No. DIT is a 1-year diploma. ICS is a 2-year intermediate degree. DIT is more practical, ICS is theoretical.

Can I do DIT online from AIOU?

AIOU DIT is distance learning with study materials. Some workshops may require attendance.
