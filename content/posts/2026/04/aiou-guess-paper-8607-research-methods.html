🔮 Important Topics (High Probability – Spring 2026)

  • Research paradigms – Quantitative vs. Qualitative vs. Mixed Methods
  • Research design – Descriptive, experimental, correlational, case study
  • Sampling techniques – Probability (random, stratified) & non‑probability (convenience, purposive)
  • Data collection tools – Questionnaire, interview, observation, document analysis
  • Validity & reliability – Internal/external validity, test‑retest, inter‑rater reliability
  • Ethical issues in educational research – informed consent, confidentiality, plagiarism
  • Data analysis – Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode) and basic inferential (t‑test, chi‑square)
  • Writing a research proposal – Structure: introduction, literature review, methodology, timeline
