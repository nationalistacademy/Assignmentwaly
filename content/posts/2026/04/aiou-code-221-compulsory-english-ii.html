❓ Question 1: Add correct punctuation marks to the following passage: "he said i am going to lahore tomorrow will you come with me no i said i have some work"

Answer:

Corrected Passage with Punctuation:

"He said, 'I am going to Lahore tomorrow. Will you come with me?' 'No,' I said, 'I have some work.'"

💡 Punctuation Rules Applied:
  • Capital letter at the beginning of a sentence (He, I, Will, No).
  • Comma (,) to separate the speaker from the quote (He said, I said).
  • Period (.) at the end of a statement.
  • Question Mark (?) at the end of a question.
  • Quotation Marks (" " or ' ') around direct speech.
  • Period (.) at the very end of the passage.

❓ Question 3: Change the following sentences into Indirect Speech: (1) He said, "I am happy." (2) She said, "Will you come to the party?" (3) The teacher said, "Work hard." (4) He said, "I have completed my homework." (5) They said, "We were playing cricket."

Answer:

Direct SpeechIndirect Speech
He said, "I am happy." He said that he was happy.
She said, "Will you come to the party?" She asked if I would come to the party.
The teacher said, "Work hard." The teacher advised us to work hard.
He said, "I have completed my homework." He said that he had completed his homework.
They said, "We were playing cricket." They said that they had been playing cricket.
💡 Rules for Changing Direct to Indirect Speech:
  • Remove quotation marks and use "that" for statements.
  • Change pronouns: I → he/she, you → I/he/she/they (according to context).
  • Change tenses: Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect, etc.
  • Questions: Use "asked" + "if/whether" for yes/no questions; use "asked" + question word for WH questions.
  • Commands: Use "ordered/advised/requested" + to + verb.

❓ Question 5: Write an essay on any one of the following topics: (a) My Aim in Life (b) Importance of Discipline (c) A Visit to a Historical Place

Answer:

(a) My Aim in Life

An aim is a goal or target that a person wants to achieve in life. Without a clear aim, life becomes like a ship without a rudder. Everyone should have an aim in life to work hard and achieve success. My aim in life is to become a doctor.

I want to become a doctor because I want to serve humanity. In our country, many people suffer from diseases because they cannot afford proper medical treatment. I want to help such people by providing free medical care. Doctors are respected in society because they save lives and relieve pain. The profession of a doctor is noble and honorable.

To achieve my aim, I work very hard. I study science subjects like Biology, Chemistry, and Physics with full concentration. After matriculation, I will join F.Sc (Pre-Medical) and then appear in the Medical College Entrance Test (MDCAT). After getting admission in a medical college, I will study hard for five years to become a qualified doctor.

I know that becoming a doctor is not easy. It requires hard work, dedication, and patience. But I am determined to achieve my aim. With the blessings of Allah and the support of my parents and teachers, I will become a doctor one day. A life without an aim is like a bird without wings. I will keep striving until I achieve my goal.

(b) Importance of Discipline

Discipline means obedience to rules and regulations. It is the key to success in every field of life. Without discipline, life becomes chaotic and meaningless. Discipline is important for individuals, families, institutions, and nations.

Discipline is very important for students. A disciplined student wakes up early in the morning, studies regularly, respects teachers, and follows school rules. Such students always succeed in exams and in life. On the other hand, students who lack discipline waste their time and fail in life.

Discipline is also important in the army. Soldiers follow orders strictly and live a disciplined life. This discipline makes them strong and united. A disciplined army can protect the country from any threat.

In our daily life, discipline helps us manage our time well. When we follow a routine, we can complete our work on time. We get enough time for studies, work, rest, and entertainment. Discipline also teaches us self-control and patience.

In conclusion, discipline is the foundation of a successful and peaceful life. We should practice discipline from childhood. Parents and teachers should teach children the importance of discipline. A disciplined person is always respected and successful in life.

(c) A Visit to a Historical Place

Last year during my summer vacation, I got a chance to visit the historical city of Lahore with my family. Lahore is known as the "Heart of Pakistan" and has many historical places. The most famous historical place we visited was the Badshahi Mosque.

Badshahi Mosque was built by the Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb in 1673. It is one of the largest mosques in the world. The mosque is made of red sandstone and has beautiful white marble domes. The courtyard is so big that thousands of people can offer prayers there at the same time. The architecture of the mosque is amazing and shows the beauty of Mughal art.

After visiting the mosque, we went to the Lahore Fort. The fort is located right next to the Badshahi Mosque. It has many beautiful buildings inside like Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors), Naulakha Pavilion, and Alamgiri Gate. The Sheesh Mahal is decorated with thousands of small mirrors that shine like stars.

We also visited Minar-e-Pakistan, where the famous Pakistan Resolution was passed on March 23, 1940. The minar is 70 meters high and represents the struggle of Muslims for a separate homeland. Seeing the minar gave me a feeling of pride and patriotism.

This visit was a great learning experience for me. I learned a lot about the history of our country. I felt proud of our rich culture and heritage. I hope to visit more historical places in the future.

❓ Question 7: Write a short story with the moral "Slow and Steady Wins the Race". You may use the following outline: (Hare and tortoise - hare laughs at tortoise - they race - hare runs fast - sleeps under tree - tortoise continues - tortoise wins - moral)

Answer:

The Hare and the Tortoise

Moral: Slow and Steady Wins the Race


Once upon a time, there lived a hare and a tortoise in a jungle. The hare was very proud of his speed. He always laughed at the tortoise because the tortoise moved very slowly.


One day, the hare was making fun of the tortoise again. He said, "You are so slow! You take hours to cover a distance that I can cover in minutes. You are a useless creature!" The tortoise felt very sad but replied calmly, "You may be fast, but that does not mean you are the best. Let us have a race. We will see who wins."


The hare laughed loudly and accepted the challenge. All the animals of the jungle gathered to watch the race. The fox was the referee. He marked the starting line and the finishing line. The race began.


The hare ran very fast and soon disappeared from sight. The tortoise started walking slowly but steadily. After some time, the hare was far ahead. He looked back and saw that the tortoise was nowhere in sight. The hare thought, "The tortoise is so slow. It will take him hours to reach here. I have plenty of time. Let me take a nap under this tree."


The hare lay down under a shady tree and soon fell asleep. Meanwhile, the tortoise kept walking slowly but steadily. He did not stop. He did not give up. He kept moving towards the finishing line.


After some time, the hare woke up. He stretched his legs and looked around. To his surprise, he saw the tortoise near the finishing line. The hare ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. The tortoise crossed the finishing line first and won the race.


The hare was ashamed of his pride and laziness. He apologized to the tortoise. The tortoise forgave him and said, "Speed is not everything. Patience, persistence, and hard work are more important."


Moral: Slow and Steady Wins the Race.

❓ Question 8: Write a paragraph on any one of the following topics: (a) My Hobby (b) Importance of Trees (c) A Rainy Day

Answer:

(a) My Hobby

A hobby is an activity that we do for pleasure in our free time. Everyone has a hobby. My hobby is reading books. I love to read storybooks, novels, and informative books. Reading is a very useful hobby. It gives us knowledge and entertainment at the same time. When I read a book, I forget all my worries and enter a new world of imagination. Reading improves our vocabulary and language skills. It also makes us wiser and more thoughtful. My parents encourage me to read good books. They buy me books on my birthday. My favorite books are adventure stories and historical novels. I also like to read about science and technology. Reading is the best way to spend free time. I will continue this hobby throughout my life.

(b) Importance of Trees

Trees are very important for life on Earth. They provide us with oxygen, which is essential for breathing. Trees absorb carbon dioxide and other harmful gases, making the air clean and fresh. They give us shade on hot sunny days and protect us from the scorching heat. Trees provide us with fruits, wood, medicine, and many other useful things. They prevent soil erosion and keep the land fertile. Trees also bring rain and keep the climate moderate. Birds and animals live in trees. Without trees, life on Earth would be impossible. Unfortunately, people are cutting down trees for their selfish needs. This is causing global warming and climate change. We should plant more trees and take care of existing trees. One tree planted is a gift to the future.

(c) A Rainy Day

A rainy day is a day when it rains heavily. Rainy days are very pleasant and enjoyable. Last Monday, it was a rainy day. The sky was covered with dark clouds. The sun was not visible. Suddenly, strong winds started blowing. Then the rain started pouring down. The raindrops falling on the ground made a beautiful sound. The air became cool and fresh. The roads and streets became wet. Children came out to play in the rain. They made paper boats and floated them in the water. Some children danced in the rain. The plants and trees looked fresh and green after the rain. I sat by the window and enjoyed the view. I also drank a hot cup of tea and ate some snacks. Rainy days are a blessing of nature. They give relief from the summer heat and make the environment clean and beautiful.

⚡ Important Short Questions for Quick Revision

  • ✅ Q: What is a sentence? A: A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.
  • ✅ Q: What is a noun? A: A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.
  • ✅ Q: What is a verb? A: A verb is a word that shows an action or state of being.
  • ✅ Q: What is an adjective? A: An adjective describes a noun or pronoun.
  • ✅ Q: What is an adverb? A: An adverb describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb.
  • ✅ Q: What is the Past Participle of "go"? A: Gone.
