🎓 AIOU Degree Verification Process 2026 – Complete Guide

📅 Updated: April 12, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 9 min read 📚 Category: AIOU Resources ⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

After years of hard work and dedication, receiving your degree from Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU) is a proud moment. However, when applying for jobs, higher education abroad, or government positions, you'll often need to verify the authenticity of your AIOU degree. Employers and institutions want to confirm that your degree is genuine and recognized.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain every method to verify your AIOU degree — whether you're a student, an employer, or a foreign university. We'll cover online verification via HEC and AIOU portals, SMS verification, and the official verification process for international use.

📌 Why is Degree Verification Important? Degree verification protects against fake degrees, ensures credibility for employers, and is mandatory for higher education admissions, visa applications, and government job appointments.
✅ AIOU Degree Recognition Status

Before we dive into verification methods, it's important to know that AIOU degrees are fully recognized by:

  • 🏛️ Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan – All AIOU programs are HEC-approved
  • 🌍 International Universities – AIOU is recognized by UNESCO and many foreign institutions
  • 🏢 Government of Pakistan – AIOU degrees are valid for all government jobs (FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, etc.)
  • 📜 Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) – For engineering programs
  • ⚖️ Pakistan Bar Council – For law programs (LLB)
🔍 4 Methods to Verify AIOU Degree
🌐 Method 1: HEC Degree Verification System (Recommended)

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan maintains the official Degree Verification System (DVS) for all recognized universities including AIOU. This is the most trusted method for employers and institutions.

📝 Step-by-Step Process:
  • Visit HEC Degree Verification System: https://hec.gov.pk → Click on "Verification" → "Degree Verification System (DVS)"
  • Select "Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU)" from the dropdown menu
  • Enter the student's Roll Number and Year of Passing
  • Complete the captcha verification
  • Click "Search" to view degree details
  • The system will display: Student Name, Father's Name, Degree Title, CGPA/Marks, Year of Passing, and Verification Status
🔗 Direct HEC DVS Link: https://eportal.hec.gov.pk/degreere verification
✅ What You'll See: A green checkmark confirms the degree is genuine. You can download a verification report (PDF) for official use.
⚠️ Note: HEC's database includes degrees issued after 2010. For older degrees (before 2010), you may need to use AIOU's direct verification method.
🏫 Method 2: AIOU Degree Verification Portal

AIOU has its own online verification system for degrees issued by the university. This is useful for degrees not yet uploaded to HEC's database.

  • Visit AIOU Official Website: https://www.aiou.edu.pk
  • Navigate to "Student Services" → "Degree Verification"
  • Enter the Roll Number and CNIC of the degree holder
  • Select the Program/Semester from the dropdown
  • Click "Verify" to get instant results
📌 Pro Tip: If you don't have the CNIC number, you can use the Roll Number only. However, providing both gives more accurate results.
📱 Method 3: AIOU Degree Verification via SMS

For quick verification without internet access, AIOU offers an SMS-based verification service. This is perfect for employers who need instant confirmation.

Type: DEGREE (space) Roll Number
Send to: 8008

Example: If the roll number is 123456789, type DEGREE 123456789 and send to 8008.

You will receive an SMS reply with:

  • Student Name
  • Degree Title
  • Year of Passing
  • Verification Status (Genuine/Not Found)
⚠️ Important: Standard SMS charges apply. This service works on all major networks (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone).
🏢 Method 4: Physical Verification via AIOU Regional Office

For official purposes where a physical stamped document is required, you can request verification through AIOU's Controller of Examinations office.

  • Visit the AIOU Main Campus, Islamabad (Sector H-8) or your nearest Regional Office
  • Fill out the "Degree Verification Request Form" (available at the examination section)
  • Submit a copy of the degree and CNIC of the degree holder
  • Pay the verification fee (see fee structure below)
  • Collect the verification certificate (usually takes 3-5 working days)

🌍 International Degree Verification for AIOU Graduates

If you're applying for higher education abroad or seeking employment in another country, you'll need additional verification steps:

📜 Step 1: HEC Attestation

Visit the HEC Attestation Department in Islamabad or your regional HEC office. Required documents:

  • Original AIOU degree
  • Original transcripts/marksheets
  • Copy of CNIC
  • Passport-size photographs (2)
  • HEC attestation fee (approx. Rs. 2,000 - 5,000 per document)
🌐 Step 2: Ministry of Foreign Affairs Attestation

After HEC attestation, the degree must be attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) in Islamabad.

🏛️ Step 3: Embassy Attestation

Finally, the degree must be attested by the embassy of the country where you intend to study or work (e.g., UAE Embassy, Saudi Embassy, UK Embassy, etc.).

📌 Important: This entire process can take 2-4 weeks. Consider using professional attestation services if you're not in Islamabad.
❌ Common Issues & Solutions
🔴 Problem: Degree Not Found in HEC Database

Solution: This often happens for degrees issued before 2010 or recently issued degrees (last 6 months). Try AIOU's direct portal or SMS verification. If still not found, contact AIOU Examination Department at 051-111-112-468 (Extension 4).

🔴 Problem: Name Mismatch in Verification

Solution: Name mismatches are common due to spelling errors in CNIC vs. degree. Submit an application to AIOU's Examination Department with proof (CNIC copy, degree copy, and affidavit). Correction takes 2-3 weeks.

🔴 Problem: SMS Verification Not Working

Solution: Ensure you have sufficient balance. Try the correct format: DEGREE [Roll Number] (no spaces after DEGREE? Actually space between DEGREE and roll number). Send to 8008. If still not working, use online portals.

🔴 Problem: Degree Verification for Deceased Person

Solution: Provide the original degree, death certificate, and CNIC of the legal heir. Submit to AIOU Examination Department with a written application. Additional processing time applies.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: How long does AIOU degree verification take online?

Online verification via HEC or AIOU portal is instant. SMS verification takes 10-30 seconds. Physical verification takes 3-5 working days.

📌 Q2: Is AIOU degree verification free?

Online verification (HEC portal, AIOU portal, SMS) is free (excluding SMS charges). Physical verification certificates require a fee (Rs. 1,000 - 5,000).

📌 Q3: Can employers verify my AIOU degree without my consent?

Yes, employers can verify using your roll number (which is usually on your CV/degree). They don't need your CNIC for basic verification.

📌 Q4: What if I lost my AIOU degree certificate?

Apply for a duplicate degree from AIOU's Examination Department. You'll need a police report (if lost/stolen), affidavit, and payment of duplicate fee (approx. Rs. 3,000 - 5,000).

📌 Q5: Are AIOU online degrees verifiable?

Yes, AIOU's online/distance learning degrees are fully recognized and verifiable through all methods mentioned above.

📌 Q6: Can I verify an AIOU degree from outside Pakistan?

Yes! Use the HEC Degree Verification System or AIOU online portal from anywhere in the world. For attested copies, you may need to use courier services or authorized representatives.

📌 Q7: What is the difference between HEC verification and AIOU verification?

HEC verification is the national standard accepted by all government bodies. AIOU verification is university-specific and may be accepted for some purposes, but HEC verification is preferred for jobs and higher education.
