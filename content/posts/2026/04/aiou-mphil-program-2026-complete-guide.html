AIOU MPhil Program 2026 – Complete Guide for Lecturer Jobs

Complete guide to AIOU MPhil program. Admission process, eligibility, fee, and how MPhil leads to lecturer jobs in colleges and universities.

What is MPhil Program?

MPhil is a 2-year postgraduate research degree. It is the minimum qualification for lecturer positions in colleges and universities in Pakistan.

MPhil Programs Offered by AIOU

  • MPhil Education
  • MPhil Urdu
  • MPhil English
  • MPhil Islamic Studies
  • MPhil Pakistan Studies
  • MPhil Economics
  • MPhil Sociology
  • MPhil Psychology
  • MPhil History
  • MPhil Political Science

Eligibility Criteria for MPhil

- MA/MSc in relevant subject with minimum 55% marks
- Or BS (4-year) with minimum 2.5 CGPA
- GAT (General) score minimum 50%
- Research proposal required
- Interview by admission committee

MPhil Fee Structure 2026

Program Duration: 2 years (4 semesters)
Admission Fee: Rs. 10,000
Tuition per semester: Rs. 15,000 × 4 = Rs. 60,000
Thesis Evaluation Fee: Rs. 15,000
Degree Fee: Rs. 5,000
Total Program Cost: Rs. 90,000

MPhil Admission Process 2026

Step 1: Register for GAT General test at NTS
Step 2: Visit AIOU MPhil admission portal
Step 3: Fill application with MA/BS details
Step 4: Upload GAT score and research proposal
Step 5: Pay admission fee (Rs. 10,000)
Step 6: Appear for interview (if shortlisted)
Step 7: Check merit list online

MPhil Program Structure

Year 1 (Semester 1-2): Coursework (8 courses)
Year 2 (Semester 3-4): Research and Thesis Writing
End of Program: Thesis defense (viva voce)

Career Opportunities After MPhil

College Lecturer (BPS-17/18): Rs. 80,000-150,000/month
University Lecturer: Rs. 100,000-200,000/month
Research Associate: Rs. 60,000-100,000/month
Education Officer: Rs. 80,000-150,000/month
PhD Candidate: Scholarship opportunities

Required Exams for Lecturer Jobs

FPSC Lecturer Test: For federal government colleges
PPSC/SPSC Lecturer Test: For provincial government colleges
NTS Subject Test: For private universities
Interview: Required for all permanent positions

Further Studies After MPhil

✅ PhD (3-5 years) – For professor and HOD positions
✅ Post-Doctoral Research – For international careers
✅ Higher Education Leadership Diplomas

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I become lecturer with AIOU MPhil?

Yes, AIOU MPhil is HEC recognized and accepted for lecturer positions in government and private colleges.

What is minimum GAT score for MPhil?

Minimum 50% in NTS GAT General is required for MPhil admission in AIOU and all HEC recognized universities.
