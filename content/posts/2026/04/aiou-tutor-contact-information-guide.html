👨‍🏫 AIOU Tutor Contact Information Guide 2026

📅 Updated: April 12, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 8 min read 📚 Category: AIOU Resources ⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

One of the most common challenges faced by AIOU students is finding their tutor's contact information. Without proper communication with your assigned tutor, you may miss important updates, assignment feedback, or guidance that could improve your grades.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll show you every possible method to find your AIOU tutor's name, code, phone number, and address. We'll also share tips on how to communicate effectively with your tutor for better academic support.

📌 Why is Tutor Contact Important? Your AIOU tutor is responsible for marking your assignments, providing feedback, conducting workshops, and guiding you throughout the semester. Staying in touch can significantly improve your performance.
📋 Who is Your AIOU Tutor?

When you enroll in any AIOU program (Matric, FA, BA, BSc, MA, MSc, B.Ed, M.Ed, etc.), the university assigns a tutor for each course. This tutor is usually an experienced teacher or subject specialist in your region. Your tutor:

  • ✅ Marks your assignments and provides written feedback
  • ✅ Conducts tutorial workshops (if applicable)
  • ✅ Answers your academic queries
  • ✅ Helps you prepare for final exams
🔍 5 Ways to Find Your AIOU Tutor Contact Information
📱 Method 1: Check via SMS (Easiest & Fastest)

AIOU provides a dedicated SMS service to get tutor details instantly. This works on all mobile networks (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone).

Type: TUTOR (space) Your Roll Number
Send to: 8008

Example: If your roll number is 123456789, type TUTOR 123456789 and send to 8008.

You will receive an SMS with your tutor's name, tutor code, phone number, and sometimes address.

✅ SMS Reply Example:
"Your Tutor for Course Code 1429 is Mr. Ahmed Ali (Tutor Code: T-12345). Contact: 03XXXXXXXXX. Address: XYZ Colony, Lahore."
💻 Method 2: AIOU Aaghi Portal (LMS)

The AIOU Aaghi Portal (Learning Management System) is the official online platform where you can access tutor details, assignments, and study materials.

  • Visit: https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk
  • Login with your Roll Number and Password (default password usually your roll number or CNIC)
  • Navigate to "My Courses" or "Tutor Information" section
  • Click on your course to view tutor name, contact, and schedule
⚠️ Note: If you can't log in, try resetting your password via the "Forgot Password" option or contact AIOU's IT support at 051-111-112-468.
🏢 Method 3: Visit Your Regional Office

AIOU has regional offices in every major city of Pakistan. Visit the office with your roll number and CNIC, and the staff will provide your tutor's contact details.

Major AIOU Regional Offices:

  • Islamabad/Rawalpindi: Sector H-8, Islamabad
  • Lahore: 22-Empress Road, Lahore
  • Karachi: ST-1, Block 15, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi
  • Peshawar: 13-B, Khyber Road, University Town, Peshawar
  • Quetta: Sariab Road, Near Passport Office, Quetta
  • Multan: 3-KM Bosan Road, Multan
  • Faisalabad: Samundri Road, Faisalabad

Call AIOU Helpline: 051-111-112-468 to find your nearest regional office.

📧 Method 4: Check Your Admission Form or Prospectus

When you received your admission confirmation or study kit, it may include a "Tutor Allocation Letter". This document contains:

  • Tutor's name and code
  • Tutor's phone number
  • Tutor's mailing address (for submitting assignments)
  • Workshop schedule (if applicable)

Check the envelope or package you received from AIOU. If you've misplaced it, you can request a duplicate from your regional office.

📞 Method 5: Call AIOU Helpline or UAN

AIOU has a dedicated helpline to assist students with tutor-related queries.

📞 AIOU Helpline: 051-111-112-468
📱 UAN Number: 051-9057-000

When calling, have your Roll Number and Course Code ready. The operator will provide tutor details or redirect you to the relevant department.

📌 Pro Tip: Call during working hours (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM) for faster response. Avoid calling during peak times (12 PM - 2 PM).

💬 How to Communicate Effectively with Your AIOU Tutor
  • ✅ Be respectful and professional: Start with "Assalam-o-Alaikum" or "Dear Sir/Madam".
  • ✅ Introduce yourself properly: Mention your full name, roll number, course code, and semester.
  • ✅ Be specific about your query: Instead of "I need help with assignment", say "I need clarification on question number 3 of Assignment 1 for course code 1429".
  • ✅ Call during appropriate hours: Avoid calling early morning, late night, or on Fridays (prayer time). Best time: 10 AM - 12 PM or 3 PM - 5 PM.
  • ✅ Follow up politely: If you don't get a response in 2-3 days, send a gentle reminder.
  • ✅ Keep records: Save SMS, emails, or notes of your conversations with the tutor.
❌ Common Problems & Solutions
🔴 Problem: SMS to 8008 Not Working

Solution: Ensure you have sufficient balance (SMS charges apply). Try sending from a different mobile network. Alternatively, use the Aaghi Portal or call the helpline.

🔴 Problem: Tutor's Phone is Always Off or Unreachable

Solution: Send a WhatsApp message if the number is available. If still no response, contact your regional office and request an alternative contact method or a tutor change (in extreme cases).

🔴 Problem: Can't Log In to Aaghi Portal

Solution: Default password is usually your roll number or CNIC without dashes. If still not working, click "Forgot Password" or call AIOU IT support at 051-111-112-468 (extension 5).

🔴 Problem: No Tutor Assigned Yet

Solution: Tutor allocation can take 4-6 weeks after admission. If more than 2 months have passed, contact your regional office immediately.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: How long does it take for tutor allocation after admission?

Typically 4-6 weeks after the semester starts. You'll receive an SMS or letter when your tutor is assigned.

📌 Q2: Can I request a tutor change?

Yes, but only for valid reasons (e.g., tutor is unresponsive, relocated, or conflict of interest). Submit a written application to your regional office.

📌 Q3: What if my tutor doesn't respond to calls or messages?

Contact your regional office and explain the situation. They may provide an alternative contact or reassign you.

📌 Q4: Does AIOU provide online tutor support?

Yes, through the Aaghi Portal. You can send messages to your tutor within the LMS system.

📌 Q5: Can I contact my tutor on weekends?

It's better to avoid weekends. Most tutors prefer contact during weekdays (Monday to Friday), 9 AM to 6 PM.

📌 Q6: What information should I keep ready before contacting my tutor?

Your roll number, course code, assignment number, and specific question number. This helps the tutor assist you quickly.
