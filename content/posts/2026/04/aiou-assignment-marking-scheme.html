Do you know that assignments contribute 30-40% of your total marks in AIOU? That's right! Your final grade depends significantly on how well you perform in assignments. Yet, many students don't understand how tutors evaluate assignments and what they look for while marking.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll decode the AIOU assignment marking scheme — explaining exactly how marks are distributed, what tutors look for, and most importantly, how you can score maximum marks in every assignment.

📌 Did You Know? A student who scores 85% in assignments but only 50% in exams can still pass with a good overall grade. Assignments are your opportunity to secure marks before the final exam!

📝 How Many Assignments per Course?

The number of assignments varies by program level:

  • Matric / FA / BA / BSc: Usually 2 assignments per course
  • MA / MSc / M.Ed: Usually 2-4 assignments per course
  • B.Ed / M.Ed: Often 3-4 assignments including workshop reports
  • Certificate/Diploma Programs: Usually 1-2 assignments

🔍 Detailed Evaluation Criteria (What Tutors Look For)
📖 1. Content Accuracy & Relevance (35-40 marks)

This is the most important criterion. Your answers must be factually correct and directly address the question asked.

✅ What gives you high marks:

  • Answers that directly answer the question (no irrelevant information)
  • Correct use of definitions, theories, and concepts
  • Information taken from AIOU textbooks and recommended reference books
  • Up-to-date facts and figures (where applicable)

❌ What loses marks:

  • Off-topic or irrelevant answers
  • Outdated or incorrect information
  • Contradictory statements
💡 2. Depth of Knowledge & Analysis (20-25 marks)

Tutors don't want just definitions — they want to see that you understand the topic and can apply the knowledge.

✅ What gives you high marks:

  • Explaining concepts in your own words (not just copying from book)
  • Providing real-life examples to support your points
  • Comparing and contrasting different perspectives
  • Critical analysis rather than just description
  • Connecting theory to practical situations
📑 3. Structure & Organization (10-15 marks)

A well-organized answer is easier to read and understand. Tutors appreciate clear structure.

✅ Ideal Answer Structure:

  • Introduction: Define key terms, state what you'll cover
  • Main Body: 3-5 key points with explanations and examples (use headings)
  • Conclusion: Summarize main points, give final insight
  • References: List sources used
✍️ 4. Originality & Own Words (10 marks)

AIOU tutors check for plagiarism. Copying directly from textbooks or the internet leads to mark deduction.

⚠️ Warning: AIOU has started using plagiarism detection software. If your assignment is found to be copied, you may receive zero marks or be required to resubmit.

✅ How to show originality:

  • Read the topic, understand it, then write in YOUR OWN words
  • Paraphrase instead of quoting directly
  • Add your own examples and interpretations
  • If you must quote, use quotation marks and cite the source
📚 5. References & Citations (5-10 marks)

Using reference books and citing them properly shows you've done wider reading.

✅ What tutors want:

  • At least 2-3 references per assignment (for longer answers)
  • Proper citation format (APA for social sciences, MLA for humanities)
  • Bibliography at the end listing all sources
  • References from AIOU textbooks, reference books, or credible websites

📖 APA Citation Example:
Kothari, C. R. (2020). Research Methodology. New Delhi: New Age International.

🎨 6. Presentation & Neatness (5-10 marks)

First impressions matter! A neat, well-presented assignment creates a positive impression on the tutor.

✅ What gives you full marks:

  • Clean, legible handwriting (if handwritten) or clean typing (if typed)
  • Proper headings and subheadings
  • Consistent formatting (font, spacing, margins)
  • Page numbers on every page
  • No spelling or grammar errors
  • Binding or stapling properly

💯 How to Calculate Your Assignment Score

Example Calculation:

Suppose your course has 2 assignments of 100 marks each, and assignments contribute 40% to the final grade.

  • Assignment 1 score: 85/100
  • Assignment 2 score: 75/100
  • Average assignment score: (85 + 75) ÷ 2 = 80/100
  • Assignment contribution to final grade: 80 × 40% = 32 marks out of 40

These 32 marks are carried forward. You need at least 24 marks (40% of 60) in the final exam to pass the course.

🚫 Common Mistakes That Lose Marks
  • ❌ Not reading the question properly – Answering something different from what's asked
  • ❌ Copying directly from the textbook – No originality, high plagiarism risk
  • ❌ Poor handwriting – Tutor can't read = marks deducted
  • ❌ Missing page numbers or headings – Looks unprofessional
  • ❌ No references/bibliography – Looks like no research done
  • ❌ Submitting late – Late fees or marks deduction
  • ❌ Very short answers – Not enough depth
  • ❌ Spelling and grammar mistakes – Shows carelessness
  • ❌ No table of contents – Hard for tutor to navigate
  • ❌ Not answering all parts of a question – Partial marks only
✅ Tips to Score 80%+ in AIOU Assignments
  • ✅ Start early: Don't wait until the last week. Good assignments take time.
  • ✅ Read the question 3 times: Underline keywords like "explain", "compare", "analyze".
  • ✅ Make an outline: Plan your answer before writing.
  • ✅ Use the AIOU textbook as your primary source: It's what tutors expect.
  • ✅ Add 2-3 reference books: Shows wider reading (use the Top 10 Reference Books list).
  • ✅ Write in your own words: Paraphrase, don't copy.
  • ✅ Use headings and subheadings: Makes it easy to read.
  • ✅ Add diagrams/tables where relevant: Visual elements get attention.
  • ✅ Proofread before submission: Check spelling, grammar, and completeness.
  • ✅ Submit on time: Late submission = marks deduction.
  • ✅ Keep a copy: Always photocopy or scan before submitting.
📞 What to Do If You Disagree with Your Assignment Marks

If you believe your assignment was marked unfairly, you have options:

  • Step 1: Contact your tutor politely and ask for feedback on your marks.
  • Step 2: If not satisfied, submit a rechecking request to your regional office within 15 days of result announcement.
  • Step 3: Pay the rechecking fee (approx. Rs. 500-1,000 per assignment).
  • Step 4: A different tutor will re-evaluate your assignment.
  • Step 5: If marks increase, the new marks will apply. If not, original marks stand.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: What is the minimum passing marks in AIOU assignments?

40% (40 out of 100). Below 40 is a fail. You need to pass assignments AND final exams separately to pass the course.

📌 Q2: Do assignments affect my CGPA?

Yes! Assignment marks are included in your final grade calculation, which determines your CGPA. Good assignment scores boost your overall CGPA.

📌 Q3: Can I submit typed assignments instead of handwritten?

Yes, most AIOU programs accept typed assignments. Use Times New Roman or Arial, font size 12, with 1.5 line spacing. Typed assignments often get better presentation marks.

📌 Q4: How long does it take to get assignment results?

Typically 4-8 weeks after submission. Results are sent to your regional office and may be updated on the Aaghi Portal.

📌 Q5: Can I resubmit an assignment if I get low marks?

Generally, no. Assignments are one-time submissions. However, if you failed an assignment, you may be allowed to submit a revised assignment (check with your tutor).

📌 Q6: Do I get marks for attendance in workshops?

For some programs (B.Ed, M.Ed, MBA), workshop attendance is mandatory and may carry 5-10 marks in your assignment or practical component.

📌 Q7: How do I check my assignment marks?

Check via Aaghi Portal (LMS), ask your tutor, or visit your regional office. Some tutors return marked assignments via mail or in person.

📌 Q8: What if my assignment is lost in the mail?

If you have proof of submission (courier receipt, acknowledgment), contact your regional office. You may be allowed to submit a duplicate. Always keep a copy!
