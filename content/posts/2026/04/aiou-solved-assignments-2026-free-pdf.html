⚠️ Critical AIOU Submission Rules: Know Your Category

AIOU assignments are graded under two distinct methodologies. Failure to follow your program's specific protocol will lead to instant disqualification.

📝 Category A: Physical Submission (Handwritten Only)

Applicable Programs: Matric, Intermediate (FA, F.Sc, I.Com), and Bachelor (BA, B.Com / Associate Degree Programs).

  • Must be fully handwritten on clean, single-sided sheets.
  • You must attach three copies of the official "Assignment Form" (Pertal / پرتال) with each solved assignment.
  • These papers must be physically packed and sent via Pakistan Post (Registry) or TCS to your assigned tutor's mailing address.

💻 Category B: Online Upload (Digital Typing Only)

Applicable Programs: BS, B.Ed (1.5, 2.5, 4 Years), Associate Degree in Education (ADE), MA, MSc, M.Com, and MLIS.

  • Assignments must be digitally typed in MS Word and converted to PDF format before uploading.
  • Urdu assignments can be typed in InPage or MS Word (using Urdu keyboard layouts) and converted to PDF.
  • Files must be uploaded directly to the Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk) under your login credentials.
  • Strict Warning: Never scan handwritten pages and upload them as PDFs on LMS. AIOU filters will flag this, and tutors will assign zero marks.

✍️ How to Write AIOU Assignments to Secure 90+ Marks

Tutors scan hundreds of assignments in a limited time. To grab their attention and secure top grades, follow these practical, human-tested strategies:

  • Do Not Mindlessly Copy-Paste: Tutors easily recognize identical wording from standard keys. Read our solved assignment PDF, understand the core concept, and rewrite the answer in your own natural words.
  • Use Clear Structural Formatting: Break long answers into readable chunks. Use black markers for headings, sub-headings, and blue ink for writing details (for handwritten papers). Use bold headers for LMS typed files.
  • Incorporate Real-Life Examples: Adding a localized Pakistani case study, recent statistics, or a brief flowchart elevates the quality of your assignment instantly.
  • Strictly Follow the Word Limit: If the question asks for 500 words, keep your answers concise. Stretching the content unnecessarily leads to low grades.
⚠️ Pro Tip for Physical Mailing: Always send assignments using "Registered Post" via Pakistan Post or reputable courier services. Keep the physical booking receipt safe until your final semester results are announced on the CMS portal. This receipt is your official legal proof if your tutor misplaces your marks sheet.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (AIOU Student Guide)

📌 Are these solved AIOU assignments completely free?
Yes. All assignment drafts and PDFs on Assignmentwaly are 100% free. They are structured by subject teachers to act as ideal research guidelines and notes for students.
📌 Can I scan handwritten papers and upload them to the LMS portal?
Absolutely not. This is a very common mistake that BS and B.Ed students make. If you upload scanned images of handwritten sheets on Aaghi LMS, the system will flag the format, and your tutor has the authority to award you zero marks. You must type your answers digitally in MS Word or PDF.
📌 What should I do if my tutor list is not allotted yet?
If your semester tutor names or addresses are not displaying on the CMS portal, do not stop working! Write and complete your assignments beforehand. Keep checking your CMS account daily, or contact your local AIOU regional office if the deadline is approaching.
📌 How do I check if my LMS uploaded assignment is submitted?
When you upload your PDF on the Aaghi LMS, make sure to click "Submit Assignment" and ensure the status changes from "Draft (Not Submitted)" to "Submitted for Grading" in green color. If it remains a draft, the tutor won't be able to grade it.
📌 Can I use InPage Urdu for typing solved assignments?
Yes, you can type in InPage, export the files as PDF, and upload them to the LMS. Alternatively, you can use MS Word with a phonetics Urdu keyboard layout, which is much easier to export as a clean PDF document.
