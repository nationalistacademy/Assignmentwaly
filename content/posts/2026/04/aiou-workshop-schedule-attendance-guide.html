👨‍🏫 AIOU Workshop Schedule & Attendance Guide 2026

📅 Updated: April 12, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 9 min read 📚 Category: AIOU Resources ⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

Are you an AIOU student confused about workshop schedules, attendance requirements, and what happens if you miss a workshop? You're not alone. Workshops are a critical component of many AIOU programs, yet they remain one of the most misunderstood parts of the distance learning system.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll explain everything you need to know about AIOU workshops — from understanding what they are and why they're mandatory, to checking your schedule, attendance requirements, and what to do if you miss one.

📌 What is an AIOU Workshop? AIOU workshops are mandatory practical training sessions conducted by the university, mainly for BS, PGD, ADC, B.Ed, M.Ed, and teaching programs. In workshops, students attend physical or online sessions, perform practical activities, present lessons or assignments, and earn assessment marks. Attendance is compulsory[reference:0].
🎯 Which Programs Require Workshops?

Not all AIOU programs have workshops. Workshops are typically required for:

  • B.Ed (1.5 / 2.5 / 4-year programs) – Teaching practice workshops are mandatory[reference:1]
  • BS Programs – Including Accounting & Finance, Computer Science, etc.[reference:2]
  • M.Ed / MA Education – Practical training components[reference:3]
  • ADC (Associate Degree in Commerce) – Workshop participation required[reference:4]
  • Postgraduate Programs (M.Phil, PhD) – Research workshops may be required[reference:5]
  • Teaching Practice Courses – Such as 8608, 8609, etc.[reference:6]
⚠️ Note: Matric, FA, BA, and MA programs in non-teaching subjects may not have mandatory workshops. However, always check your course outline or contact your regional office to confirm.

💻 Types of Workshops: Online vs Face-to-Face
🌐 Online Workshops (Aaghi LMS / Microsoft Teams)

Most AIOU workshops are now conducted online through the Aaghi LMS portal. These are mandatory 12-hour interactive sessions (2 hours daily for 6 days) for programs like B.Ed, BS, and specialized courses[reference:9].

Requirements:

  • Microsoft Teams application installed on phone or computer[reference:10]
  • Active internet connection
  • Login through Aaghi LMS portal (not direct MS Teams link)[reference:11]
  • Regular participation during sessions
🏢 Face-to-Face (Physical) Workshops

AIOU face-to-face workshops are mandatory in-person training sessions for specific programs such as postgraduate, M.Phil, B.Ed, and teacher training courses (e.g., 1.5-year B.Ed). These workshops are required to fulfill teaching practice, project, or program requirements[reference:12].

Features:

  • Conducted at AIOU regional offices or designated study centers
  • Usually 5 hours daily for 5-7 days[reference:13]
  • Direct interaction with tutors and resource persons
  • Presentation and practical activities required
📋 Attendance Requirements & Policy

Attendance in workshops is strictly mandatory for eligible programs. Without attending workshops, the course remains incomplete[reference:14]. Here's what you need to know:

Program TypeMinimum Attendance RequiredConsequence of Absence
B.Ed WorkshopsMinimum 80% (at least 4 out of 6 days)[reference:15][reference:16]Mark deduction or fail requirement
BS Programs70-80% recommended[reference:17]May affect final grades
Teaching Practice Courses80% compulsoryCannot proceed with practicum
Online WorkshopsAttendance auto-tracked by systemNo attendance = no marks
⚠️ Important: According to AIOU's modern policy, if you are absent from a workshop, you will not be completely failed, but a significant percentage of marks will be deducted and you will have to complete them in the papers[reference:18]. Missing a workshop may result in being considered to have "forgotten the course"[reference:19].
🔍 How to Check Your Workshop Schedule

Follow these steps to find your personalized workshop schedule:

  • Via Aaghi LMS Portal: Visit aaghi.aiou.edu.pk → Login with your Student ID and Password → Navigate to "Courses" → Workshop schedule displayed in right sidebar[reference:20]
  • Via AIOU Official Website: Go to www.aiou.edu.pk/Workshop.asp → Enter your Student ID, Region, and Program[reference:21]
  • Via SMS: Send WORKSHOP [Roll Number] to 8008 (charges apply)
  • Via Postal Letter: AIOU regional offices dispatch written letters containing workshop timetables[reference:22]
  • Via Regional Office: Visit your nearest AIOU regional office with your roll number
📱 How to Attend Online Workshops (Step-by-Step)
✅ Step-by-Step Guide:
  1. Install Microsoft Teams – Download from official app store (phone) or Microsoft website (PC)[reference:23]
  2. Login to Aaghi LMS Portal – Visit aaghi.aiou.edu.pk with your credentials
  3. Access Workshop Link – Navigate to "Workshop" or "Courses" section and click the provided Teams link
  4. Important: Always join through LMS portal. Joining MS Teams directly will NOT mark your attendance[reference:24]
  5. Participate Actively – The system auto-tracks attendance. Your chat and class are recorded. AIOU Monitoring Office checks everything[reference:25]
  6. Stay Engaged – After every half hour, respond with "yes sir" or similar to show understanding[reference:26]
  7. Complete Quizzes – Some workshops have quizzes. You usually have 72 hours to attempt them[reference:27]
❌ What Happens If You Miss a Workshop?
  • ❌ Marks Deduction: A significant percentage of marks will be deducted[reference:28]
  • ❌ Incomplete Course: Without attending workshops, the course remains incomplete[reference:29]
  • ❌ Fail Consideration: Missing a workshop may result in being considered to have failed that component[reference:30]
  • ❌ Reappear Requirement: You may need to reappear for the workshop in the next semester[reference:31]
  • ❌ Final Grade Impact: Your overall grade may be reduced significantly[reference:32]

✅ Can You Make Up a Missed Workshop?

Yes, in most cases. Students have three chances to attend and pass the workshop assessment. The first chance is in the current semester, the second in the next semester, and the third in the following semester[reference:33]. If you miss a workshop, contact your regional office immediately to arrange for a makeup session.

💡 Tips for Workshop Success
  • ✅ Check schedule early: Don't wait until the last day. Verify your workshop dates at least 2 weeks in advance.
  • ✅ Set reminders: Use phone alarms for daily workshop timings.
  • ✅ Prepare materials: If it's a teaching practice workshop, prepare your lesson plans in advance.
  • ✅ Stable internet: Ensure reliable connection for online workshops.
  • ✅ Join on time: Late entry may not be counted as present.
  • ✅ Take notes: Workshop content may be tested in quizzes or final exams.
  • ✅ Interact with tutors: Ask questions and participate actively.
  • ✅ Save tutor contact: Get your tutor's phone number and email during the workshop for future reference[reference:34].
  • ✅ Keep proof: Screenshot attendance confirmations if possible.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
📌 Q1: Are AIOU workshops compulsory for all students?

No, only for specific programs like B.Ed, BS, M.Ed, and teaching practice courses. Matric, FA, BA, and MA programs in non-teaching subjects typically do not require workshops. Always check your course outline[reference:35].

📌 Q2: Can I attend the workshop from my phone?

Yes, online workshops can be attended via the Microsoft Teams app on your smartphone. However, ensure you join through the Aaghi LMS portal link, not directly through Teams[reference:36].

📌 Q3: What if I don't receive my workshop schedule?

Check the Aaghi LMS portal first. If still not available, contact your regional office or call AIOU helpline at 051-111-112-468. Also check your postal mail as AIOU sends physical letters[reference:37].

📌 Q4: Do workshops have exams or quizzes?

Yes, many workshops include quizzes. For example, the Autumn 2026 workshop quiz opens on June 13, 2026, and is available for 72 hours on the LMS portal[reference:38].

📌 Q5: Will I get TA/DA for attending physical workshops?

No, AIOU does not provide any TA/DA (travel allowance/daily allowance) to students attending workshops[reference:39]. However, hostel facilities are available at the main campus for minimal charges[reference:40].

📌 Q6: How is attendance tracked in online workshops?

AIOU uses an auto-generated system for attendance. Your chat and class are on recording mode, and the AIOU Monitoring Office checks everything. You must stay engaged throughout the session[reference:41].

📌 Q7: What is the duration of a typical workshop?

Online workshops: 2 hours daily for 6 days (total 12 hours)[reference:42]. Physical workshops: 5 hours daily for 5-7 days[reference:43].

📌 Q8: Can I miss one day of workshop?

At least 80% attendance is required (e.g., 4 out of 6 days). Missing one day may be acceptable if you maintain 80% overall. However, try to attend all days to avoid any issues[reference:44].
