AIOU Rechecking & Paper Revaluation 2026 – Complete Process

Complete guide to AIOU paper rechecking and revaluation. How to apply, fee structure, success rate, and what to do if you need higher marks.

What is Paper Revaluation?

If you believe your exam was marked incorrectly, you can apply for revaluation. A new examiner will re-check your entire paper.

Who Should Apply for Revaluation?

  • Failed by 5-10 marks (close to passing)
  • Expecting higher marks but got lower
  • Answer sheet not returned (possible marking error)
  • Need higher marks for scholarship or job

Revaluation Schedule 2026

Spring 2026 Exams:
Application deadline: November 15, 2026
Revaluation result: January 15, 2027

Autumn 2026 Exams:
Application deadline: May 15, 2027
Revaluation result: July 15, 2027

How to Apply for Revaluation

Step 1: Visit rechecking.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Enter your Roll Number
Step 3: Select subjects for revaluation
Step 4: Pay fee (Rs. 500 per subject)
Step 5: Submit application
Step 6: Wait 60 days for result

Revaluation Fee Structure

Per subject fee: Rs. 500
Maximum 5 subjects per application
Processing fee: Rs. 300 (one time)
Fee is non-refundable (even if marks don't change)

Revaluation Success Rate

Average marks increase: 5-15 marks
Success rate: 15-20% of applications
Failed to Pass conversion: 30%
Grade improvement (C to B, B to A): 10%

What Happens During Revaluation

Step 1: Your original answer sheet is retrieved
Step 2: New examiner (different from first) rechecks
Step 3: All questions re-evaluated
Step 4: New marks calculated
Step 5: If higher marks found, result is updated
Step 6: If lower marks found, original marks remain

What to Do If Revaluation Fails

Option 1: Apply for improvement exam (next semester)
Option 2: Request answer sheet copy (Rs. 1,000 fee)
Option 3: Submit formal complaint to examination controller
Option 4: Take supplementary exam (if available)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get my answer sheet photocopy?

Yes. Apply at regional office with Rs. 1,000 fee. You'll receive copy within 30 days.

Can marks decrease after revaluation?

No. AIOU policy ensures original marks remain if new marking is lower.
