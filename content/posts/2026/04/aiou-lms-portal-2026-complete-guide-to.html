AIOU LMS Portal 2026 – Complete Guide to Learning Management System

Complete guide to AIOU LMS (Learning Management System) portal. How to access online lectures, download digital books, submit assignments online.

What is AIOU LMS Portal?

AIOU LMS is an online learning platform where students can access digital study materials, watch video lectures, and submit assignments online.

LMS Portal URL

lms.aiou.edu.pk

How to Login to AIOU LMS

Step 1: Visit lms.aiou.edu.pk
Step 2: Enter your Roll Number as Username
Step 3: Enter default password (your CNIC number without dashes)
Step 4: Click "Login"
Step 5: Change password after first login

Features of AIOU LMS Portal

1. Digital Textbooks

Download PDF versions of all course textbooks. No need to wait for physical books.

2. Video Lectures

Watch recorded lectures by AIOU professors. Perfect for visual learners.

3. Online Assignments

Submit assignments digitally (for select programs). No printing or courier needed.

4. Discussion Forums

Ask questions to tutors and other students. Get answers within 24-48 hours.

5. Quiz & Tests

Take practice quizzes to prepare for exams. Instant results.

6. Announcements

Get official AIOU notifications about exams, results, and deadlines.

How to Download Books from LMS

Login to LMS → Click "My Courses" → Select your subject → Click "Course Materials" → Click "Download PDF" → Save to your device

How to Watch Video Lectures on LMS

Login to LMS → Click "Video Lectures" → Select subject → Select topic → Watch video (requires good internet)

How to Submit Assignments Online

Login to LMS → Click "Assignments" → Select assignment number → Upload file (PDF/Word) → Click "Submit" → Download receipt

LMS Mobile App (Android/iOS)

AIOU has mobile app for LMS:
- Android: Download from Google Play Store "AIOU LMS"
- iOS: Download from App Store "AIOU Learning"
Features: Watch lectures, read books, get notifications on phone

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LMS mandatory for AIOU students?

No, but highly recommended. Physical books and workshops are still primary. LMS is extra support.

Can I submit all assignments online?

Only for select programs (BS, MPhil, PhD). Check with your regional office for online submission availability.

📚 AIOU Assignment Submission Guidelines (Spring 2026)

To avoid getting your assignments rejected by your tutor, you must carefully follow the submission rules set by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU):

  • Matric, FA, BA/B.Com: Must be written by hand on blank single-sided pages. Staple three copies of the official Assignment Pertal (پرتال) form and send them via Pakistan Post or TCS directly to your tutor's address before the deadline.
  • BS, B.Ed, MA, MSc: Type digitally in MS Word, convert your file into PDF format, and upload it directly to the official Aaghi LMS Portal (aaghi.aiou.edu.pk).
