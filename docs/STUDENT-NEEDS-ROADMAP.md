# Student and visitor needs roadmap

Assignmentwaly should help a visitor complete the next legitimate student task with minimum friction. Prioritize high-frequency, time-sensitive questions and reliable official routes over broad service claims.

## Core visitor journeys

| Visitor | Main question | Useful destination or feature |
|---|---|---|
| Prospective student | Which programme can I apply for, and what is the current deadline? | Programme directory, eligibility explainer, official admission notice and checklist |
| Newly enrolled student | Is my admission confirmed, and where are my books? | Admission-status steps, book-dispatch guide and official tracking route |
| Current student | Where is my course code, tutor, workshop or assignment deadline? | Course-code finder, semester calendar, LMS/AAGHI guide and workshop checklist |
| Assignment learner | How do I understand the question and prepare my own response? | Question breakdown, rubric/checklist, concept explanations and student-draft feedback guidance |
| Exam candidate | What should I revise and how can I test myself? | Syllabus-linked revision notes, practice MCQs, mock tests and explanations |
| Graduating student | How do I get transcript, degree or verification? | Document checklist, official application route and status follow-up templates |
| International learner | Can I study remotely and what time zone/process applies? | Overseas learner guide, online access instructions and official contact links |

## Product roadmap

### P0 — Fix trust and findability
- Working About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer and correction/report paths.
- Searchable navigation by programme, course code, semester and student task.
- Clear labels for official AIOU information versus Assignmentwaly educational explanations.
- Remove unsubstantiated metrics and unsupported guarantees; disclose update dates and sources.

### P1 — Low-cost, high-utility tools
- Course-code and programme finder with filters and aliases.
- Assignment planning checklist and question-analysis worksheet that guides students to write their own answers.
- Urdu/English assignment formatter with RTL support, title page, headings, tables, word count and local DOCX/PDF export where technically feasible.
- CGPA/GPA calculator with transparent formula and AIOU policy caveat.
- Study planner and semester deadline checklist with downloadable calendar/checklist.

### P2 — Practice and progress
- MCQ practice sets with explanations, topic tags and local progress history.
- Mock-exam timer and self-assessment; no claims that practice scores predict official grades.
- Past-paper index with course code, programme, semester, source, rights/permission status and file integrity checks.
- Citation helper that formats user-supplied source details and reminds learners to verify bibliographic facts.

### P3 — Student support library
- Plain-language guides for LMS/AAGHI login, password recovery, tutor details, workshop attendance, book delivery, assignment submission, roll slips, results, transcripts and degree services.
- Downloadable application/request templates for administrative issues, clearly marked as examples to personalize.
- Error troubleshooting pages and official helpdesk escalation steps.
- Feedback/correction workflow to keep time-sensitive pages current.

## Product principles

- Mobile-first, lightweight and usable on low-bandwidth connections.
- Free-first; no paid API, hosting or service purchase without owner approval.
- Minimize personal data. Never ask students to publish CNIC, roll number, passwords or private tracking IDs into public comments or tools.
- Prefer local browser processing for drafts and calculators; explain storage behavior and shared-device risks.
- Accessibility: keyboard navigation, readable typography, labels, contrast, reduced motion and Urdu RTL support.
- Academic integrity: the student owns and writes assessed work. Tools explain, organize, check and format; they do not impersonate the student or generate ready-to-submit assessed answers.
- Measure success by task completion, accuracy, accessibility, corrections and repeat usefulness—not invented visitor counters.

## Validation before building

Use Search Console queries/pages, genuine feedback and official AIOU student notices to rank features. Interview a small, diverse group of current students only with consent. Do not infer demand from keyword volume alone or publish a tool until it has been tested on mobile and desktop.
