# Batch 04 — Static pages content, trust and compliance audit (41 pages)

**Audit date:** 2026-11-02. **Scope:** all 41 public Blogger static pages listed in `archive/blogger-export/manifest.json`. All 41 live pages were fetched in five batches and reviewed for page intent, factual claims, programme/course mapping, duplicate content, file links, academic integrity and trust/legal disclosures. No live Blogger changes.

## Executive findings

- Static pages have significant duplicated or mislabelled content: the `/p/sitemap_0924605372.html` URL is actually a BS assignment landing page; `/p/sitemap.html` is a second sitemap page; two guess-paper hubs overlap; programme hubs overlap with programme-specific pages.
- Several programme pages make broad claims such as “all semesters”, “all courses” and “complete list” while tables are incomplete, malformed, duplicated or contain mismatched codes.
- Operational pages contain stale or conflicting Spring/Autumn 2026 dates, generic fee figures, questionable LMS links and unsupported performance/traffic statistics.
- Legal/trust pages overstate compliance and confidentiality; About and Advertise pages contain unsupported team, user, satisfaction and traffic metrics.
- The Career page advertises completed assignment-writing work. Reposition it around ethical tutoring and student-owned work; do not recruit for ghostwriting or contract cheating.

## Recommended remediation sequence

1. **Fix high-risk factual pages first:** admissions, academic schedule, question papers, B.Ed deadlines, programme availability and LMS links.
2. **Resolve duplicates and wrong page intent:** sitemap URLs, BS hub, guess-paper hubs, original-books hub and overlapping Associate Degree/BA-B.Com pages. Use Search Console, canonical and backlink evidence before redirecting.
3. **Rebuild programme/course catalogues:** validate current programme names, code, semester, language, book edition and actual working official PDF link from the current prospectus and AIOU softbook/question-paper portals.
4. **Correct legal and trust disclosures:** privacy policy, terms, disclaimer, DMCA, contact, About and Advertise. State only what is actually implemented and evidenced; seek qualified legal review for jurisdiction-specific language.
5. **Enforce academic integrity:** pages may explain concepts, show examples, offer feedback and support practice. Students must author their own assessed submissions. Remove ghostwriting recruitment and marks guarantees.
6. **Then improve metadata and internal links:** titles, descriptions, headings, breadcrumbs, structured data, related links and crawlable navigation should follow the final page map—not precede factual corrections.

## Limits and safeguards

- The audit checks page-level claims and visible content, not every PDF byte, every linked download, every programme's full curriculum, or legal enforceability. Each downloadable file and course code must be verified before being labelled official/current.
- A page marked Merge Candidate is not approved for deletion or redirection. Confirm Search Console performance, canonical signals, inbound links and user value first.
- No Search Console data was available in the earlier connector attempt; this is not evidence of zero traffic.
- No live Blogger pages, theme, DNS, production deployment or PR merge were changed.
