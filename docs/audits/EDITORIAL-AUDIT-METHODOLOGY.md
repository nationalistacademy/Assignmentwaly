# Assignmentwaly 190-URL Editorial Audit

Snapshot: 2026-11-02. Scope: 149 Blogger posts and 41 static pages.

## Status
This audit begins with automated metadata triage. It is not yet a completed page-by-page editorial or factual review. Each URL must be opened and its content read before a final Keep, Improve, Merge Candidate, or Remove Candidate decision.

## Disposition rules
- Keep: retain useful, accurate, non-duplicative pages.
- Improve: preserve the URL where practical and refresh accuracy, usefulness, title, description, structure, trust and internal links.
- Merge Candidate: only after comparing full content, search intent, backlinks, Search Console queries and redirect/canonical implications.
- Remove Candidate: only after human review confirms obsolete, misleading, unlawful, empty or irrecoverably duplicative content. Low traffic alone is not a removal reason.

## Required checks
1. Confirm live URL and canonical.
2. Read full page content.
3. Verify dates, fees, eligibility, course codes, processes and institutional claims against current official AIOU sources.
4. Compare overlapping pages before consolidation.
5. Review copyright/permission for PDFs, textbook scans, images and answer materials.
6. Improve titles, descriptions and internal links based on the page's real intent.
7. Record a human decision, reviewer and evidence.
