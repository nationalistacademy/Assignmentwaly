# Batch 1 Editorial Review — High-risk AIOU Student Guidance

**Review date:** 2026-11-02  
**Scope:** 21 pages from the first archived post batch (posts-01.html), prioritized for workshops, books, admissions/student services, exam-centre changes, degree verification and assignment resources.  
**Evidence base:** Archived HTML snapshot plus current AIOU primary pages searched/opened on the review date. Direct live Blogger pages were not accessible through the browser fetcher in this run, so the review compares archived content with current official AIOU sources; live-page rendering/canonical must be checked before publication.

## Findings requiring urgent correction

### 1. Exam-centre change notice — CRITICAL
**URL:** https://assignmentwaly.blogspot.com/2026/10/aiou-important-notice-address-change-15-november-2026.html

The post tells all students to change their address before 15 November 2026 to affect their exam centre. This appears to confuse the ODL Autumn mailing window (ending 15 November) with the exam-centre-change process. AIOU's official instructions say:
- change of exam centre is not allowed in normal circumstances;
- hardship cases only, generally once during a programme;
- request through CMS Service Request;
- supporting documentary evidence is required;
- prescribed fee is Rs 1,000;
- published schedule for listed programmes: Spring by 29 August; Autumn by 15 February.

**Official evidence:** https://aiou.edu.pk/sites/default/files/2025-08/REVISED%20EXAM%20CENTRE%20CHANGE%20FORM%201%20%281%29%20%281%29.pdf  
**Action:** Remove the 15 November exam-centre deadline immediately in the proposed edit. Separate address correction from exam-centre change. Do not publish a new deadline without a dated official notice.

### 2. Online refund guide — CRITICAL
**URL:** https://assignmentwaly.blogspot.com/2026/05/aiou-online-refund-application-complete.html

The article claims a 60-day deadline and describes an online application/IBAN/SMS-tracking workflow. Current AIOU FAQ instead states:
- eligible students apply before the start of study period;
- ineligible students may apply within one year of fee deposit;
- estimated processing time is about 30 days;
- deduction is 10% for eligible applicants who do not wish to continue and apply before study period, and 15% for ineligible applicants applying within one year.

**Official evidence:** https://www.aiou.edu.pk/frequently-asked-questions-faqs  
**Action:** Rewrite against the official FAQ and current refund form. Remove the 60-day deadline and unsupported online workflow unless a current official procedure explicitly confirms them.
