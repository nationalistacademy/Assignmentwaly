# Batch 02 — Content-level editorial audit (archive positions 51–100)

**Audit date:** 2026-11-02. **Scope:** 50 posts restored in `archive/blogger-export/posts-02.html` from public Blogger RSS. This is a reconstructed snapshot, not a byte-for-byte original export. No live Blogger changes.

## Executive findings

1. A repeated unrelated “Solved Assignment Key” heading and generic Spring 2026 deadline table appears at the top of informational guides and subject pages. Remove it from every affected post.
2. Operational articles contain unverified SMS shortcodes, default login credentials, fees, delivery windows and semester deadlines. These can misdirect students; correct them before SEO work.
3. Course-specific guess-paper pages (positions 84–100) claim “your tutor” authorship, promise 80%+ marks, assert five-year research without evidence and display demo download links that explicitly say replace with real URLs. They are not publish-ready.
4. Programme guides make unsupported claims about diploma/DIT/DBA availability, eligibility, fees, duration and course structure. Use current official prospectus PDFs only.

## Official reference baseline

- [AIOU Autumn 2026 Prospectus](https://www.aiou.edu.pk/prospectus) — programme availability, eligibility, fees, duration and structure.
- [International & Overseas Students](https://www.aiou.edu.pk/directorate-international-collaboration-exchange-0) — current international admissions and prospectus.
- [Admission Open](https://www.aiou.edu.pk/admission-open) and [OAS Fresh Admission](https://www.aiou.edu.pk/oas-fresh-admission) — current admission notices and application routes.
- [AIOU FAQ](https://aiou.edu.pk/frequently-asked-questions-faqs) — AAGHI-issued credentials, password reset and programme-specific assignment submission routes.
- [AAGHI LMS](https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk/) — separate workshop links per day and portal routes for ODL, face-to-face and international students.
- [Guidance & Help](https://www.aiou.edu.pk/guidance-help) — admission confirmation, book tracking, CMS, tutor information, assignment schedule and workshop help.
- [STEP / Certificate Courses](https://www.aiou.edu.pk/step-certificate-courses) — current certificate course families.
- [AIOU Contact Us](https://aiou.edu.pk/contact-us) — official central contact and department email addresses.

These sources do not validate every claim in an archived article. Programme-specific PDFs, current notices and forms must be checked before republishing dates, fees, criteria or processes.
