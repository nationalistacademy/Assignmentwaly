**Checked:** 2026-11-03  
**Site:** https://assignmentwaly.blogspot.com/  
**Branch:** strategy/student-journey-pakistan-seo  
**Scope:** Nine live Blogger URLs: three workshop pages and two URLs each for course codes 258, 259 and 260.  
**Method:** Read-only public page fetches plus comparison against the 190-URL editorial register and official AIOU catalogue/AAGHI sources.

## Executive result

- All nine inspected Blogger URLs returned HTTP 200 in the read-only fetch.
- All three course-code pairs show substantial overlap in subject and exam-preparation intent, but Course 260's study-guide page has a potentially distinct intent from its guess-paper page.
- Workshop content is duplicated across three posts, with conflicting/unverified attendance and quiz claims.
- Several course pages contain false score guarantees and # download destinations. A # destination is not a working PDF download.
- Search Console page-level clicks/impressions, indexed/canonical selection, and backlink data were not available in the inspected repository evidence. The earlier audit says Search Console connector returned zero rows, which is not evidence of zero traffic.
- Therefore this report makes content-target recommendations only. It does not authorize redirects, deletions, canonical tags, or publishing.

## E. Evidence limitations — do not skip

1. **Search Console:** The prior audit notes that the connected Search Console retrieval returned zero rows. No URL-level clicks, impressions, queries, indexing status or selected canonical data was available for these nine pages in this review. Zero connector rows does not mean zero Google traffic.
2. **Backlinks:** No URL-level backlink export was available. Do not redirect any candidate based on content similarity alone.
3. **Canonical/robots:** The public content fetch confirmed HTTP 200 and titles, but this report does not certify Google's selected canonical, raw canonical tags, robots directives or indexability for each URL.
4. **Analytics:** No reliable page-level engagement or conversion evidence was available.
5. **Course answer accuracy:** The public course catalogue confirms course code/title/credits, not the correctness of every solved answer, experiment or past-paper claim.
