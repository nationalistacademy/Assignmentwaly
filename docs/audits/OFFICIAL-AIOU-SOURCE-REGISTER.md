## Calendar caution
The current general academic-calendar page and the legacy online calendar display schedules grouped by programme/mode. Dates can differ by programme type and semester. Therefore, an article must identify the relevant programme, mode (ODL/F2F), semester and dated official notice. Do not present a generic calendar range as a universal deadline.

## Editorial evidence record
For each factual claim, record:
- exact claim and affected paragraph;
- official source URL and page/notice title;
- date checked and notice/publication date where available;
- programme/mode/semester scope;
- whether the blog statement is confirmed, contradicted, unclear or expired;
- correction required and reviewer.
