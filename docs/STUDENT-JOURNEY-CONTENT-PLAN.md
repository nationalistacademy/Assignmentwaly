# Student-journey content optimization plan

**Project:** Assignmentwaly  
**Scope:** Editorial planning for the existing Blogger site; no live Blogger publication or URL changes.  
**Audience:** AIOU learners and prospective learners across Pakistan, including overseas learners where the process differs.

## Outcome

Help each visitor complete the next legitimate student task with accurate, original, easy-to-follow guidance. Optimize for usefulness and discoverability, not keyword repetition or guaranteed rankings.

## Journey map and content requirements

| Journey stage | Questions to answer | Priority content | Next useful action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explore and apply | Which programme fits me? Am I eligible? When can I apply? | Programme explainers, eligibility checklists, official admission notices, fee guidance | Open the relevant official AIOU admission route |
| Newly admitted | Is my admission confirmed? How do I find my CMS access and books? | Admission confirmation steps, portal access, book dispatch and tracking guide | Check the official portal or dispatch route |
| Start the semester | What is my course code, tutor, workshop schedule and calendar? | Course-code navigation, semester checklist, tutor and workshop guides | Open the relevant student portal or verified notice |
| Complete assignments | What is the question asking? How do I plan and cite my own response? | Question-analysis worksheet, concept explanations, assignment structure, citation and proofreading guidance | Use the companion learning/formatting tool and write independently |
| Prepare for exams | What should I revise? How do I practise? | Syllabus-linked revision guides, past-paper context, MCQ explanations, mock-test guidance | Practise and review weak topics |
| Resolve a problem | Why is login, submission, tracking or result access failing? | Error-specific troubleshooting, decision steps and official escalation route | Follow the official support channel |
| Graduate and verify | How do I request transcript, degree, correction or verification? | Document checklist, official process guide and follow-up template | Submit through the current official channel |
| Study from overseas | Which steps are online, what time zone applies and which documents are needed? | Overseas learner guide with process-specific differences | Confirm the current route with AIOU |

## Editorial brief for every priority URL

Record these fields before editing:

- Existing Blogger URL and page/post title.
- Journey stage, primary task and one primary search intent.
- Intended audience/programme/semester, where relevant.
- Current accuracy status and last checked date.
- Official primary source URL, notice date and the exact claim it supports.
- Missing steps, exceptions, screenshots or examples.
- Duplicate/overlap candidates and the canonical page to preserve.
- Useful internal links: previous step, next step and relevant companion resource.
- Trust, accessibility, mobile and academic-integrity checks.
- Proposed disposition: keep, improve, merge candidate, noindex/archive candidate or remove candidate.
- Reviewer, review date and change log.

## Page structure standard

1. Descriptive title that matches the visitor's task.
2. Short answer-first introduction explaining who the guide applies to.
3. Visible last-checked date for time-sensitive information.
4. Official source link near the relevant claim.
5. Numbered steps, prerequisites, exceptions and common errors.
6. Original explanation or example that adds value beyond the official notice.
7. A concise checklist or table when it reduces task effort.
8. Relevant next-step internal links.
9. Genuine FAQs only when they answer likely follow-up questions.
10. Correction/report route and accurate author/editor disclosure.

Use Urdu, English or a natural bilingual explanation when it improves comprehension. Define acronyms such as CMS, AAGHI and LMS on first use. Do not add word count solely for SEO.

## Academic-integrity boundary

Assignmentwaly may explain concepts, unpack questions, show learning examples, guide research and citations, and help learners review or format their own drafts. It must not offer impersonation, plagiarism, fabricated references, guaranteed marks or ready-to-submit assessed work presented as the student's own. The learner remains the author.

## Prioritized execution

### P0 — Trust and high-risk accuracy
- Review About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer and correction route against actual site settings.
- Verify current admission, fee, assignment, workshop, book and exam claims against dated official sources.
- Correct misleading service wording, unsupported statistics and guarantees.
- Confirm permission/rights for downloadable files and images.

### P1 — High-intent journey pages
- Admission and eligibility.
- Admission confirmation and book dispatch.
- CMS/AAGHI access and common login errors.
- Assignment submission and workshop attendance.
- Course-code and tutor discovery.
- Roll number slips, exam schedules and results.

### P2 — Learning and exam support
- Assignment question analysis and student-authored draft review guidance.
- Syllabus-based revision and practice explanations.
- Past-paper index with provenance and rights status.
- Companion links to relevant free tools on AIOU Coaching.

### P3 — Completion and specialist needs
- Transcript, degree, correction and verification.
- Overseas learner processes.
- Administrative request templates and escalation guides.

## Review and release gate

Do not mass-edit or delete the 190 archived URLs. Inspect each URL, preserve useful permalink equity, check Search Console data where available, and record the proposed change. New or substantially revised content must pass official-source verification, originality, rights, academic-integrity, mobile/accessibility and human-editor review before the owner publishes it.
