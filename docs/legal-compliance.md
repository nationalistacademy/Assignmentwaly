# Legal and professional compliance baseline

**Status:** Planning baseline — not a legal opinion or certification.  
**Jurisdiction focus:** Pakistan. Federal and provincial requirements may differ.

## 1. What the platform may publish
- General public legal education in plain language.
- Summaries of publicly available statutes, regulations, judgments, and official procedures, with links and review dates.
- General checklists that do not decide a person's legal rights or strategy.
- A directory or referral workflow for independently verified advocates, once verification and consent processes exist.

## 2. Regulated legal work
The Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973 and applicable rules regulate admission and practice by advocates. Section 22 addresses the right to practise. Accordingly:
- Do not describe unqualified staff, writers, AI systems, or the platform itself as lawyers or advocates.
- Do not provide case-specific legal opinions, draft legal strategy, represent clients, appear before courts, or hold out as providing reserved legal services unless the work is undertaken by an appropriately entitled advocate.
- Before listing an advocate, verify identity, current enrolment/standing, relevant bar council, practice scope, and consent to be listed. Re-check periodically.
- A referral is not an endorsement or guarantee. Disclose who provides the service, who contracts with the client, fees, scope, and complaint route.
- Do not share a person's documents or contact details with an advocate without clear, recorded consent.
- Check professional conduct, advertising, fee, confidentiality, conflict, and client-care rules before launching any legal marketplace or paid referral model.

## 3. Legal content quality controls
Every legal explainer should include:
- Country and province/territory to which it applies.
- The legal question and a distinction between law, procedure, and practical commentary.
- Primary source links where available (Pakistan Code, Gazette, Supreme Court/High Court, regulator, or relevant Bar Council).
- Enactment/amendment details and a clearly labelled last-reviewed date.
- A note that laws and procedures can change and readers should obtain advice for their own facts.
- A named reviewer and evidence of qualified legal review before presenting content as professionally reviewed.

Never invent a statute, section, case citation, deadline, fee, government process, or professional credential. If the current source cannot be verified, label the point unverified and do not present it as definitive.

## 4. Business and administrative support
Company, tax, employment, intellectual-property, immigration, property, and regulatory topics may involve different laws and licensed professionals. The platform may publish general educational checklists and facilitate referrals only after scope and qualifications are checked. Do not file, certify, sign, or submit documents on behalf of users without a lawful, authorized, and professionally reviewed workflow.

## 5. User protection
Before any paid or case-related service launches, prepare and review:
- Terms of service and clear service-provider identity.
- Privacy notice and data-retention/deletion process.
- Fee, cancellation, refund, and dispute process.
- Informed consent and conflict-check procedure.
- Secure document transfer, role-based access, audit logs, and incident response.
- Accessibility and complaint escalation route.

## 6. Launch gate
A legal service must remain **not available** until a Pakistan-qualified legal reviewer confirms applicable federal/provincial requirements, professional rules, business structure, advertising model, data handling, and service contracts. This document alone does not establish compliance.
