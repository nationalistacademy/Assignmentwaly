## Official-source verification (checked 2026-11-03)

1. AIOU AAGHI LMS homepage: https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk/  
   The live page states that online workshops should be joined through the AAGHI LMS workshop link and that each day has a separate workshop link. It instructs users to select the link corresponding to the day shown in their schedule (example: day 3 → WORKSHOP03).
2. AIOU Workshop Information: https://www.aiou.edu.pk/workshop-information  
   The page title is present, but the page content returned in this check did not expose a usable current schedule. Do not infer dates or timings from its title.
3. AIOU FAQ: https://www.aiou.edu.pk/frequently-asked-questions-faqs  
   The FAQ links students to an AIOU tutorial for attending workshops on AAGHI LMS / MS Teams. The page's instructions may change; re-check before publication.
4. AAGHI homepage currently presents different portal tiles for different semesters/programme modes. The live page showed Spring 2026 / Autumn 2025 / Spring 2025 ODL links and an Autumn 2026 Face-to-Face tile at the time checked. This is a dated observation, not a permanent portal rule. Students must select the tile currently shown for their own programme and semester.

## Complete proposed Blogger body HTML

```html
<article style="max-width:900px;margin:0 auto;padding:8px;line-height:1.75;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">
<p><strong>Important:</strong> This is an independent student-help guide, not an official AIOU notice. Workshop dates, timings, portal links and attendance requirements can vary by programme and semester. Always follow the schedule and instructions shown in your own AIOU student account.</p>

<h2>What is an AIOU workshop?</h2>
<p>A workshop is a programme-related learning activity. Depending on the programme and semester, AIOU may provide online or face-to-face workshop arrangements. Do not assume that every student follows the same schedule, platform or attendance rule.</p>

<h2>Step 1: Check your own workshop schedule</h2>
<ol>
<li>Open the official <a href="https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AAGHI LMS website</a> from the AIOU website or a trusted official link.</li>
<li>Choose the portal tile that matches your programme type and semester. Read the semester and programme label before opening it.</li>
<li>Sign in using the credentials issued by AIOU. If your programme uses a different official route, follow the instructions given by your department or student portal.</li>
<li>Find your course/workshop schedule and note the date, time, course and workshop day.</li>
<li>Recheck the schedule before the session because universities may publish updates or corrections.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Step 2: Open the correct daily workshop link</h2>
<p>The AAGHI homepage instructs students and resource persons to join online workshops through the AAGHI LMS workshop link. It also states that each day has a separate workshop link. For example, if your schedule says Day 3, select the WORKSHOP03 link. Do not randomly choose another day's link or use an old saved meeting link without checking the current schedule.</p>

<h2>Step 3: Prepare before joining</h2>
<ul>
<li>Check your internet connection, device battery, speaker and microphone.</li>
<li>Use a quiet place and join with enough time to resolve basic technical issues.</li>
<li>Keep your course code, schedule and relevant study material available.</li>
<li>Use your own authorised student account. Never share your password, OTP or recovery code.</li>
<li>Follow the instructor's instructions about microphone, camera, chat and participation.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Step 4: During the workshop</h2>
<ul>
<li>Join the session that matches your own date, course and workshop day.</li>
<li>Listen to the instructor and take notes on topics, activities and assessment guidance.</li>
<li>Ask relevant questions politely through the method allowed by the instructor.</li>
<li>Do not post your password, CNIC, private contact details or another student's information in the chat.</li>
<li>If you are disconnected, return through the official LMS route and the correct daily link.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How can I check whether my attendance was recorded?</h2>
<p>Use the attendance or participation information made available through your official LMS, course instructor or programme office. A successful click on a meeting link is not proof that attendance has been recorded. If the record is missing or incorrect, keep the date, course, workshop day and a short description of the issue, then contact the relevant official support channel. Do not send passwords or OTPs.</p>

<h2>Workshop link or login problem</h2>
<ul>
<li>Recheck that you selected the correct semester and programme portal.</li>
<li>Confirm the workshop day shown in your schedule and use its matching daily link.</li>
<li>Try the official password-recovery option if you cannot sign in.</li>
<li>Check AIOU's current <a href="https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk/support" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AAGHI support page</a> or the university's official <a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/guidance-help" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Guidance &amp; Help</a> page.</li>
<li>If the problem continues, contact the relevant AIOU support office with the minimum information needed to identify the issue. Never disclose your password or OTP.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Is the same workshop link used every day?</h3>
<p>No. The AAGHI homepage states that each day has a separate workshop link. Use the link matching the day shown in your schedule.</p>
<h3>Can I use an old meeting link?</h3>
<p>Do not rely on an old saved link. Open the current AAGHI portal and check the link for your programme, semester and workshop day.</p>
<h3>Does joining the meeting automatically confirm attendance?</h3>
<p>Do not assume so. Follow the instructor's attendance procedure and verify the record through the official system or programme office.</p>
<h3>Are all AIOU students required to attend online workshops?</h3>
<p>Workshop mode and requirements can differ. Check the current instructions for your own programme and semester; this guide does not set an attendance rule.</p>
<h3>Where can I find the official workshop tutorial?</h3>
<p>Use the workshop guidance linked from AIOU's <a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/frequently-asked-questions-faqs" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official FAQ page</a> and the current AAGHI portal.</p>

<h2>Official links</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AAGHI LMS</a></li>
<li><a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/workshop-information" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Workshop Information</a></li>
<li><a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/frequently-asked-questions-faqs" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Frequently Asked Questions</a></li>
<li><a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/guidance-help" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Guidance &amp; Help</a></li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Last checked:</strong> 3 November 2026. Portal tiles and instructions can change. Confirm the latest information on the official AIOU pages before acting.</p>
<p>Assignmentwaly is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AIOU. Students remain responsible for following official university instructions.</p>
</article>
```
