## 1. Purpose and release rule

Prioritise claims that could cause a student to:
- enter credentials on the wrong portal or follow an obsolete login route;
- pay an incorrect fee or send money to an unverified person;
- miss an assignment, admission, examination, workshop, or service deadline;
- rely on invented marks, attendance, eligibility, or assessment rules; or
- submit work that misrepresents authorship or breaches AIOU academic-integrity expectations.

**No exact fee, deadline, SMS shortcode, phone number, portal URL, eligibility rule, or attendance threshold should be published unless checked against a current official AIOU source for the relevant programme, semester, and student category.** If not verified, remove the exact claim and explain where the student can confirm it.

## 3. Approved replacement wording — copy-ready drafts

### 3.1 Login and learning portals

> **Use the official portal for your programme.** AIOU may provide different online services for admissions, student records, assignments, workshops and learning management. The correct route can depend on your programme and semester. Start from the official AIOU website or its Guidance & Help page and follow the link relevant to your programme. Do not share your password, OTP, or account recovery code with Assignmentwaly or any third party. If a link in an old article no longer works, use the current official AIOU navigation instead.

### 3.2 Assignment support and academic integrity

> Assignmentwaly provides learning support, not a substitute author for assessed work. Students should understand the question, prepare their own answers, and submit work that reflects their own learning. We can help explain concepts, plan an answer, improve grammar and clarity, check references, and format a document. We do not sell completed assignments, impersonate students, or promise marks. Always follow the current AIOU course instructions and academic-integrity requirements.

### 3.3 Fees and payments

> **Confirm the current fee before paying.** AIOU fees and service charges may vary by programme, semester, service type and applicable notification. Check the current official prospectus, fee schedule or service instructions on the AIOU website. Do not send money to a personal account or an unofficial agent based only on a blog post, social-media message or phone call. Keep the official payment receipt.

### 3.4 Deadlines and semester dates

> **Dates are semester-specific.** Admission, assignment, examination, workshop and service-request deadlines can differ by programme and semester and may be revised through official notices. Before taking action, check the latest notice and your student portal. This page is an independent guide; it is not an official AIOU deadline notification. We will display an exact date only when it is supported by a current official source and labelled with its programme and semester scope.

### 3.5 Workshops and attendance

> Workshop schedules, access links and attendance requirements are course- and programme-specific. Follow the schedule and instructions issued through the official AIOU workshop system or your department. Do not assume that a meeting link, attendance threshold or daily duration published for another course applies to your course.

### 3.6 Guess papers and practice material

> This is independent practice material prepared for revision. It is not an official AIOU question paper, does not predict the examination, and cannot guarantee marks or a passing result. Use it alongside your official course books, assignments, course guide and current AIOU instructions.

### 3.7 Results, rechecking and service requests

> Application routes, fees and deadlines depend on the current AIOU rules and the service requested. Check the official examination or student-service instructions before submitting a request. A rechecking or complaint request does not guarantee a change in marks or result.

## 5. Mandatory editorial checks before any page is approved

For every affected page, the editor must record:

1. Exact live Blogger URL and page title.
2. Programme, course code, semester and student category to which the information applies.
3. Official source URL, source publication/update date, and the exact section supporting the claim.
4. Whether the claim is current, historical, or unverified.
5. Whether a date/fee/portal route must be removed, qualified, or replaced.
6. Whether the page includes a payment request, personal contact, login form, or third-party link.
7. Whether academic support wording preserves student authorship.
8. Last fact-check date and reviewer initials.

**Stop-ship conditions:** no page containing an unverified login URL, SMS shortcode, payment destination, exact deadline, fee, guaranteed marks, universal attendance threshold, or assignment-completion service should be approved for publication.

- deleting, merging, renaming, canonicalising or redirecting any URL;
- changing Blogger posts, pages, theme, menus, widgets, ads or settings;

- submitting or merging a pull request;

- publishing a fee, date, portal, eligibility or assessment rule without current official evidence.

**Owner approval required:** approve each page-level correction separately before any live Blogger change.
