```html
<article style="max-width:900px;margin:0 auto;line-height:1.75;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;padding:8px;">
<p><strong>Independent study resource notice:</strong> Assignmentwaly is an independent educational website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU). This guide helps students organise their learning; it is not an official university notice or course catalogue.</p>

<h2>Use course-wise resources responsibly</h2>
<p>AIOU programmes, course codes, course materials and assessment instructions can vary by programme and semester. Before using any study note or practice question, compare the course code and title with your own current course material, prospectus or official student portal. This page is a general guide, not a complete list of every AIOU programme or subject.</p>

<h2>Academic integrity comes first</h2>
<p>Students must understand the subject, prepare their own answers and submit their own assessed work. Do not copy another person's assignment, submit purchased or third-party work as your own, or present a practice answer as an original submission. Assignmentwaly supports learning, concept explanation, study planning, proofreading and document-formatting guidance. It does not promise marks, grades, admission or a pass, and it does not provide a shortcut around the university's assessment rules.</p>
<ul>
<li>Read the question and identify exactly what it asks.</li>
<li>Study the relevant chapters and official course material before drafting.</li>
<li>Make your own outline and explain the concepts in your own words.</li>
<li>Check facts, calculations, citations and references.</li>
<li>Follow the assignment format, submission route and deadline stated for your own programme and semester.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Practice questions are for revision, not prediction</h2>
<p>Practice questions and revision prompts can help you test your understanding. They are not official examination papers unless AIOU itself publishes them, and they do not predict the next examination. No question-match percentage or marks outcome is guaranteed. Use practice material alongside your official books, course outline and instructor guidance.</p>

<h2>Where to verify official AIOU information</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/prospectus" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Prospectus</a> — check programme, course and semester information.</li>
<li><a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/assignments-qp" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Assignment Question Papers</a> — use the university's official assignment-question-paper resource.</li>
<li><a href="https://www.aiou.edu.pk/guidance-help" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Guidance &amp; Help</a> — find current student-service guidance.</li>
<li><a href="https://aaghi21.aiou.edu.pk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AAGHI LMS</a> — access the official LMS route where applicable to your programme.</li>
<li><a href="https://online.aiou.edu.pk/LIVE_SITE/examinations.asp" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AIOU Examination Information</a> — check official examination instructions.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Assignment submission: check your own programme</h2>
<p>Submission methods are not necessarily identical for every programme, course or semester. Some students may receive tutor-specific instructions, while other programmes may use the university's online systems. Do not rely on a generic rule copied from an older post. Sign in only through a portal linked from the official AIOU website and follow the instructions shown for your own course. Never share your password, OTP or recovery code with a third party.</p>

<h2>Downloads and course-specific material</h2>
<p>This page currently does not offer downloadable assignment-answer files. We will only add a download when the file is available, checked against the stated course and semester, and suitable for lawful sharing. A missing file will not be represented by a placeholder button. If you need a specific resource, verify your course code first and use the official AIOU material as the reference point.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Does Assignmentwaly provide official AIOU solved assignments?</h3>
<p>No. Assignmentwaly is independent and does not publish material as an official AIOU answer key. Students should prepare and submit their own assessed work.</p>

<h3>Do practice questions guarantee exam questions or marks?</h3>
<p>No. Practice questions are revision aids only. They do not predict the examination and no marks or pass result is guaranteed.</p>

<h3>Where can I confirm a deadline or submission method?</h3>
<p>Check the current official notice, prospectus or student portal for your programme and semester. Do not rely on an old date copied into an independent blog post.</p>

<h3>What should I do if a course code or link is incorrect?</h3>
<p>Use the <a href="https://assignmentwaly.blogspot.com/p/contact.html">Contact Assignmentwaly</a> page and include the public page URL and course code. Do not send passwords, OTPs, CNIC numbers or payment details.</p>

<h2>Editorial note</h2>
<p>Course availability, deadlines, portal procedures and assessment requirements can change. We will add specific course information only after checking an appropriate current source. Last reviewed: 3 November 2026.</p>

<p><a href="https://assignmentwaly.blogspot.com/">Back to Assignmentwaly homepage</a></p>
</article>
```
