## 5. Non-negotiable content corrections

### Workshop pages
- Remove universal 70%, 80%, 70–80% attendance requirements unless an applicable current programme/course notice confirms them.
- Remove claims that leaving after 30–60 minutes is acceptable.
- Remove universal 30-mark quiz and 72-hour quiz rules.
- Remove unverified workshop dates, fixed daily hours and universal tutor duties.
- State that AAGHI currently describes a separate workshop link for each day; students should select the day matching their own schedule.
- Do not treat a course-specific rule (for example, course 6304) as university-wide policy.

### Course pages
- Remove 80%+ / 90%+ marks guarantees, exam prediction language and fake tutor personas.
- Remove generic Spring 2026 assignment-submission instructions from unrelated course pages.
- Remove every fake download button and every link resolving to the page itself with a # fragment.
- Do not say a file is free/available unless the actual file has been opened, checked for course and semester, and its sharing rights are known.
- Do not call broad revision categories the official syllabus until the current course outline is reviewed.
- Biology/chemistry experimental procedures require content and safety review; do not encourage unsupervised experiments.
- Preserve academic integrity: explain concepts and provide practice, but students must author their own assessed submissions.

## 6. URL consolidation decision

**No redirects are approved at this stage.** The live review established content overlap and HTTP 200 responses, but not the information needed to safely consolidate URLs.

Before redirecting a candidate:
1. Obtain 16-month Search Console data for each URL (clicks, impressions, queries, indexed status and Google-selected canonical).
2. Obtain URL-level backlink/referring-domain evidence.
3. Compare internal links and external references.
4. Compare any unique answer content and files; transfer only verified, rights-cleared material.
5. Select the destination and preserve its permalink.
6. Obtain owner approval for each redirect separately.
7. Only then prepare a redirect map and verify the resulting URLs.

A previous connector result of zero Search Console rows is a data-access limitation, not proof that a page has no traffic.
