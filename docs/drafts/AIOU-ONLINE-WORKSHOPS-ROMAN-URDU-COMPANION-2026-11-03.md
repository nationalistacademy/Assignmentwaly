## Official-source boundary

AAGHI LMS homepage (checked 3 November 2026): https://aaghi.aiou.edu.pk/  
The page says online workshops should be joined through the AAGHI LMS workshop link and that each day has a separate link. It gives the example of choosing WORKSHOP03 for Day 3. Portal tiles can change by semester and programme. No universal attendance percentage, quiz marks, fixed workshop hours or penalty rule is asserted here.
