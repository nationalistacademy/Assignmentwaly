# Assignmentwaly SEO + GEO roadmap

**Goal:** Build a trusted, mobile-first AIOU learning resource for students across Pakistan. GEO here means Generative Engine Optimization (clear, source-backed answers that AI search systems can understand), supported by accurate Pakistan-specific context. Rankings, AI citations and AdSense approval cannot be guaranteed.

## 1. Audience and search intent

Organize content around real journeys, not keyword permutations:

- Prospective learners: programmes, eligibility, admission steps, official deadlines, fee notices and application status.
- Enrolled learners: LMS/AAGHI access, course codes, books, tutors, workshops, assignments, quizzes and semester calendars.
- Exam preparation: syllabus interpretation, revision plans, practice questions, past-paper context and explanations.
- Graduating/alumni learners: transcript, degree, verification, attestation and correction procedures.
- Overseas learners: time zones, online access, document requirements and official contact routes.

## 2. Technical SEO baseline

- One stable canonical URL per useful page; preserve existing Blogger permalinks unless a documented migration decision is approved.
- Unique, human-readable title and meta description for every indexable page. Avoid comma-separated keyword lists and repetitive year-stuffing.
- Correct index/noindex behavior, crawlable navigation, XML sitemap health, robots rules and HTTPS consistency.
- Descriptive headings in logical order; one clear page purpose; visible table of contents only on long guides.
- Breadcrumbs and contextual internal links between programme hubs, course-code resources and related student tasks.
- Mobile-first rendering, accessible contrast/focus states, semantic HTML, keyboard support, RTL handling for Urdu, compressed responsive images and lazy loading below the fold.
- Validate structured data with Google Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator. Use only types that match visible content (e.g., Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, Organization/WebSite). FAQ markup must reflect visible FAQs; never add fake reviews, ratings, authors or offers.
- Monitor Core Web Vitals and Search Console indexing/crawl reports. Sitemap presence is not proof of indexing.

## 3. Content SEO standard

Every important guide should include:

1. A concise answer-first summary with who the information applies to and the semester/programme.
2. An explicit “last checked” date and the exact official source/notice link for deadlines, fees, eligibility, schedules and policies.
3. Step-by-step instructions, screenshots/diagrams where genuinely helpful, exceptions and what to do when a step fails.
4. Clear separation between official facts, editorial explanation and user-contributed information.
5. Original examples and explanations; avoid copying AIOU material beyond what is permitted and cite the source.
6. Contextual internal links to the next useful student task, not generic keyword anchors.
7. A correction/report route and transparent author/editor information that reflects actual review work.

Do not publish generic 2,000-word filler to meet a word count. A shorter accurate answer is better than a long unverified page.

## 4. GEO / AI-search readiness

- Use consistent entity names: Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU), programme names, course codes, semester names and official portal names.
- Put direct answers near the top; define abbreviations and explain the scope/eligibility of each answer.
- Cite primary sources inline and include source dates. Link directly to the exact official notice or portal page, not merely the AIOU homepage.
- Use clear headings, short factual paragraphs, tables for comparable facts and visible FAQs only where they answer genuine follow-up questions.
- Keep authorship and editorial methodology transparent. Do not invent expert reviewers, institutional affiliation, credentials, testimonials or user statistics.
- Add Pakistan context only when it changes the answer (PKR, Pakistan Post, local time, regional office process); do not create city doorway pages.
- Make pages useful without AI: answer the query fully, explain uncertainty and offer a clear next action.
- Never promise inclusion in AI Overviews, ChatGPT answers, rankings or traffic.

## 5. AdSense and trust quality gates

- Remove unsupported counters, fake scarcity, guaranteed results and unverifiable “expert verified” claims.
- Ensure privacy, terms, disclaimer and contact pages match actual site configuration and data practices.
- Replace contract-cheating language with tutoring, concept explanation, feedback on student-authored drafts, proofreading, citation guidance and formatting.
- Do not sell or distribute unauthorized copyrighted books, notes or papers. Confirm rights/permissions for every download.
- Avoid thin tag archives, duplicate course-code pages, doorway pages and scaled pages with no original value.
- Keep ads clearly separate from navigation/download controls; never encourage ad clicks or obstruct content.

## 6. Measurement

Connect the verified Assignmentwaly Search Console property and use exact field definitions before analysis. Track query/page clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, indexing status, country/device, and page-level engagement where valid analytics are configured. Segment by intent cluster and compare 28/90-day periods. Zero-row connector pulls are a data-connection/field issue until validated, not proof of zero traffic.

## 7. Release gates

No live Blogger publication or theme changes from this repository plan. Proposed content/template changes require owner review, preview, mobile/accessibility checks, factual source checks, URL/redirect review and a rollback copy. AdSense review submission remains an owner decision.
