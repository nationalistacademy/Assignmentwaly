# Student Support, CMS Privacy & Fee Transparency Policy

**Status:** Repository policy draft for review. This document does not itself change or publish any live Blogger page.
**Applies to:** Assignmentwaly (independent educational-resource platform).
**Contact:** faiezhafeez@gmail.com

## 1. Independence and scope
Assignmentwaly provides independent educational information, study resources, practice material and general student guidance. It is not an AIOU office, regional campus, agent, representative or authorized service provider. AIOU alone controls its admissions, CMS, student records, official fees, credentials and administrative decisions.

## 2. Student control of CMS accounts
Students must retain exclusive control of their CMS accounts. Assignmentwaly staff and contributors must not request, collect, store, view, use or transmit a student's CMS password, OTP, recovery code, security answer, session token or other authentication secret.

We do not log in to CMS for students, change account details, retrieve credentials, submit Student Service Requests, or send emails to AIOU pretending to be or acting on behalf of a student. This includes requests concerning login credentials, admission records, enrollment, results, assignments, tutor details and other confidential account matters.

If a student needs account-specific help, explain the steps at a general level and direct the student to submit their own request through the CMS **Student Service Request** facility, contact AIOU personally from their own email address, or visit the relevant AIOU Regional Office. The AIOU contact address supplied in its correspondence is **ad.adms@aiou.edu.pk**; students should verify current contact details on AIOU's official website before sending sensitive information.

## 3. Minimum-data support
Only collect information necessary to answer a general educational query. Do not ask students to share CMS screenshots containing CNIC, phone numbers, addresses, roll numbers, passwords, OTPs or other personal data in public comments or ordinary chat. If a student sends sensitive information unintentionally, do not reproduce it in replies, posts, logs or public examples; advise them to use AIOU's official secure channel.

## 4. Fees and financial transparency
AIOU's official charges and any optional, independently provided Assignmentwaly service charges must be described separately. Never represent a private charge as an AIOU fee, imply that payment guarantees an AIOU action or outcome, or conceal the amount or nature of a service charge. Before an optional paid service begins, disclose its scope, total price, payment method, refund/cancellation terms and the fact that it is not an official AIOU charge. Do not collect a student's AIOU login credentials as a condition of any service.

## 5. Academic integrity
Provide explanations, study notes, original examples, revision plans and practice questions that help students learn. Do not promise marks, grades, pass results or privileged access. Do not offer to impersonate a student, complete assessed work for submission as the student's own, or submit assignments or academic requests on a student's behalf. Students remain responsible for their own learning, authorship and submissions.

## 6. Contact and escalation wording
For CMS access, confidential student records, admission status, fee vouchers, results or account-specific corrections, direct students to AIOU's own CMS Student Service Request facility, their own email correspondence with AIOU, or the relevant Regional Office. Assignmentwaly can explain publicly available instructions but cannot access or intervene in individual student records.

## 7. Staff and contributor workflow
Before responding to a student:
1. Identify whether the query is general educational guidance or an account-specific administrative matter.
2. For general guidance, use public and current sources; state uncertainty where official information is unavailable.
3. For CMS/account-specific matters, do not request credentials or send a message on the student's behalf. Give the student the official self-service route.
4. Separate official AIOU fees from optional private charges and disclose all private charges before work begins.
5. Do not retain unnecessary personal information. Escalate suspected credential exposure by advising the student to secure their account through AIOU's official process.

## 8. Public-facing notice
Suggested short notice for the website:

> Assignmentwaly is an independent educational-resource platform and is not affiliated with or authorized by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU). Students must keep control of their own CMS accounts. We never ask for passwords, OTPs or recovery codes, and we do not submit CMS requests or send confidential account emails on a student's behalf. For account-specific help, students should use their own CMS Student Service Request, contact AIOU personally, or visit their Regional Office. Any optional Assignmentwaly charges are separate from official AIOU fees and are disclosed in advance.

## 9. Review
Review this policy whenever AIOU publishes a material change to its official student-support channels or when Assignmentwaly changes its services. This is an operational policy, not legal advice.
