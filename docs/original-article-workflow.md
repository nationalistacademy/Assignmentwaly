# Original article + free companion workflow

## Editorial promise
Assignmentwaly publishes useful, independently written learning material. It does not publish content designed to disguise AI use, manipulate detector scores, or imitate another publisher. No one can guarantee that Google or an AI detector will classify content in a particular way. Quality is judged by accuracy, originality, usefulness, sourcing, clarity and human accountability.

## Before drafting
- Identify a real learner question and define the intended reader.
- Review official and primary sources first; record URLs, publication dates and access/review dates.
- Note what is unknown, changing, disputed or specific to a course, semester, province or jurisdiction.
- Design the free companion resource before the article: tool, worksheet, calculator, checklist, template or practice activity.
- Define acceptance tests for that resource and test it on mobile and desktop.

## Drafting requirements
- Explain the topic from first principles in original language.
- Include practical steps, worked examples and limitations that add value beyond a generic summary.
- Attribute distinctive ideas, quotations, data, images and external material.
- Cite primary sources for deadlines, policies, laws, fees, course details and official procedures.
- Include genuine author/reviewer information only, and a visible last-reviewed date for changeable information.
- Link to the companion resource near the beginning and again at the relevant step.
- Avoid repetitive filler, keyword stuffing, fake personal experience, invented case studies presented as real, and unsupported guarantees.

## Pre-publication gate
The article and its companion are one release unit. Do not publish the article until the companion works, links are checked, facts are verified, mobile/accessibility checks pass, and a human editor approves it. Keep a correction log for material changes.

## Suggested article package
1. Reader problem and intended outcome.
2. Original article draft.
3. Source register with evidence notes.
4. Companion tool/resource and test results.
5. SEO title, meta description and useful internal links.
6. Image/diagram attribution.
7. Fact-check report and human approval.
8. Last-reviewed date and correction contact.
