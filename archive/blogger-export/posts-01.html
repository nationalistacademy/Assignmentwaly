AIOU Online Workshops ke Ahem Usool (BS, B.Ed, BBA, ADP)

Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU) ke BS, B.Ed, BBA, BS 2.5 (MA), PGD aur ADP ke tamam semesters ke students mutawajjah hon! Workshops (Online Classes) ke chand usool achi tarah samajh lein.
  • Har kitab ki sirf 6 din online workshop hoti hai (rozana 2 ghante aur time din 1 baje se raat 9 ke darmiyan kabhi bhi).
  • Farz karein agar kisi student ki class 1 baje shuru hogi toh 3 baje automatic khatam aur agar 3 baje shuru hogi toh 5 baje automatic khatam ho jayegi.
  • Workshops ke taqreeban 10 se 15 groups (batches) hote hain, kuch students ki pehle groups mein hoti hain aur kuch ki baad mein.
  • Har student ka workshop ka schedule mukhtalif hota hai, yeh kabhi mat kahein ke falan student ki workshop ho rahi hain meri kyun nahi ho rahi, har student ne apna schedule check karna hota hai.
  • Har class ka link mukhtalif hota hai, har class LMS Portal par ja kar alag se join karni hoti hai.
  • Jab workshop join karte hain toh hazri automatic lag jati hai, attendance bolni nahi hoti.
  • Poore 2 ghante class join rakhna zaroori nahi, agar aap ke paas time nahi toh aadhe ghante ya 1 ghante baad class chor sakte hain.
  • Workshop parhai ke liye bohat zaroori hain is liye kabhi bhi workshop mat chorein.
  • Workshop ke doran teacher add nahi karte, yeh mukammal auto system hota hai aur teacher hazri bhi nahi lagate.
  • Workshop ke teachers aarzi (temporary) hote hain, inka aap ki assignments, quiz ya paper ke sath koi taluq nahi, na hi woh aap ki koi bhi cheez check karenge.
  • Jis hafte jis bhi subject ki workshop hogi usi hafte ke aakhir par Jumma, Hafta, Itwar ko usi subject ka 30 Number ka Online Quiz hoga.
⚠️ Ahem Note: Quiz mein shirkat na karne ki soorat mein aap ke 30 numbers zaya ho sakte hain, is liye workshop ke sath sath quiz ka schedule bhi lazmi check karein.

IBCC aur HEC se Documents Verification Karwane ka Mukammal Tareeqa

Aap ki taleemi asnad ki tasdeeq (Attestation) karwana ek ahem marhala hai, khaas tor par jab aap ne AIOU (ya kisi doosri university) mein admission liya ho. Is guide mein hum ne Board, IBCC, aur HEC se tasdeeq karwane ka mukammal, aasan aur qadam ba qadam tareeqa bataya hai.

Step 1: Educational Boards

Sab se pehle aap ko apne saare asal documents ek file mein ikatthe karne honge: Matric ki certificate aur transcript (DMC), Intermediate (FSc) ki certificate aur transcript, aur apna CNIC. Har document ki kam az kam do do saaf photocopies banwa lein.

Ahem Hidayat: Har document ko ghor se check karein ke aap ka naam, walid ka naam aur date of birth bilkul CNIC ke mutabiq hai ya nahi. Agar kisi document mein ek harf ka bhi farq ho, toh aage IBCC aur HEC mein aap ki application ruk jayegi. Ghalati hone ki soorat mein pehle board se usay theek karwayein.

Phir apne mutaaliqa board ki website kholiye aur teen cheezein check karein:

  1. Aap ka board digitalized ho chuka hai ya nahi (yani kya woh IBCC ke sath online juda hua hai).
  2. Verification ki taza fee kya hai.
  3. Fee kis bank mein jama hoti hai.

Ab bank ja kar fee jama karwayein aur deposit slip sambhal kar rakhein. Kuch boards ab online payment bhi lete hain.

Digitalized Board:
Agar aap ka board digitalized ho chuka hai, toh fee jama karne ke baad board aap ki verification khud-ba-khud IBCC ke portal par bhej dega. Aap ko koi envelope lene ya IBCC le jaane ki zaroorat nahi hogi. Bas kuch din baad IBCC portal par check karein ke verification aa gayi hai.
Non-Digitalized Board (Purana Tareeqa):
Agar board digitalized nahi hai, toh fee jama karne ke baad board ki window par apne documents (photocopies, bank slip, CNIC) jama karwayein. Asal documents jama nahi karwane, lekin sath rakhein. Staff aap ko ek ghante baad seal band envelope dega. Yeh envelope kholay baghair mukammal band halat mein IBCC le kar jana hai.

Tip: Board ke daftar subah jaldi pohanchein taake line mein zyada intezar na karna pare. (Note: Ab taqreeban tamam boards digitalized ho chuke hain).

Step 2: IBCC (Inter Board Committee of Chairmen)

Jab board se aap ki verification ho jaye, toh agla marhala IBCC ka hai. Ab IBCC ki application mukammal tor par online hoti hai:

  • Sab se pehle attest.ibcc.edu.pk par jayein aur apna account banayein. Apni email dein aur usay verify karein.
  • Apni maloomat bilkul CNIC ke mutabiq bharein, aur phir apne certificates ki tafseel darj karein (Board, Year, Roll No).
  • Yahan aap ko select karna hoga ke asal documents attest karwane hain ya photocopies bhi. Mashwara: Asal ke sath ek ek photocopy bhi attest karwa lein.

IBCC Fee:

  • Asal document ke liye: Rs. 1,200 per document
  • Photocopy ke liye: Rs. 600 per document
  • Fee aap 1Bill, Easypaisa, JazzCash ya UBL challan ke zariye jama karwa sakte hain.

Fee ki adayegi ke baad aap ko teen mein se ek tareeqa select karna hoga:

  1. Appointment: Portal se apne qareebi IBCC daftar ki date aur time select karein, appointment letter print karein aur asal documents ke sath daftar jayein. Aam tor par usi din attested documents wapas mil jate hain.
  2. Courier: Agar shehar door hai toh documents courier kar dein. Taqreeban 10 se 15 working days lagte hain (wapsi ki courier fee Rs. 300).
  3. Express Service: (Jaldi wale kaamon ke liye 😃) Yeh sirf Islamabad aur Lahore mein shaam ke waqt dastiyab hai. Taqreeban ek ghante mein kaam ho jata hai. Fee: Asal ke Rs. 3,000 aur copy ke Rs. 1,500.
IBCC ke attested documents par QR Code hota hai, jise koi bhi university ya embassy online free verify kar sakti hai. (O-Level ya A-Level walon ko pehle IBCC se equivalence banwana hoga).
Step 3: HEC (Higher Education Commission)

HEC ka tareeqa ab mukammal tor par badal chuka hai. 11 May 2026 se HEC ne naya e-Attestation system mutarif karwaya hai, jo mukammal tor par online aur paperless hai. Na daftar jana hai, na asal documents bhejne hain.

  1. eservices.hec.gov.pk par ja kar apna account banayein. (Account hamesha apni email aur number se banayein).
  2. Apni profile mukammal karein (bilkul CNIC ke mutabiq, kyunki HEC isay NADRA se milata hai).
  3. Apna Educational Background darj karein (Matric se aakhri degree tak).
  4. Woh documents select karein jin ki attestation chahiye (Degree, Transcript, Provisional).
  5. CNIC aur Degree/Transcript ki saaf aur rangeen scanned copies upload karein. (Mobile ki tasveer ke bajaye Scanner istemal karein).

HEC Fee:

  • Fee Rs. 3,000 per document hai (per page nahi). Yani Degree aur Transcript mila kar Rs. 6,000.
  • Portal aap ko ek Consumer Number dega, jis se aap 1-Link (1Bill), JazzCash ya Easypaisa se fee pay kar sakte hain.

Adayegi ke baad HEC aap ki maloomat NADRA aur aap ki university se verify karega. Is doran apna HEC account, SMS aur email baqaida check karte rahein, kyunki kabhi kabhi HEC koi izafi document maang leta hai.

Manzoori ke baad HEC aap ko SMS aur email ke zariye agah karega, aur aap apne account se e-Attestation Certificate download kar sakenge. Yeh certificate universities, companies aur embassies online verify kar sakti hain.

Waqt ka Andaza: Waqt zyada tar aap ki university par munhasir hai, kyunki HEC aap ki degree ki tasdeeq university se karwata hai. Agar application bohat der se ruki ho, toh apni university ke mutaaliqa daftar (Registrar ya Examination Office) se rabta karein.

📝 Step-by-Step Guide to Recover Your LMS ID

Neeche diye gaye asaan steps ko follow karein:

  • AIOU LMS Portal par jayen (lms.aiou.edu.pk).
  • "Forgot Password" ke option par click karen.
  • Apni Registration ID (Jo aapki admission slip par hoti hai) enter karein.
  • Apni Registered Email ya Contact Number select karein.
  • Aapke Email ya Mobile Number par ek OTP (One Time Password) aayega.

  • Mubarak ho! Aapko aapki LMS ID aur Naya Password mil jayega.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

🔹 Agar mujhe apni Registration ID yaad nahi hai to kya karun?
Aap apni AIOU admission fee ki challan slip, books ke parcel, ya kisi bhi official result card par apni Registration ID dekh sakte hain. Yeh 9 ya 10 digits ki alphanumeric code hoti hai (jaise 21PGA12345).
🔹 OTP kahan aayega?

1 Define classroom assessment. Critically discuss the key principles of effective classroom assessment. +
Answer: Classroom assessment is gathering, interpreting, and using information about student learning. Key principles: Validity (measures what it intends), Reliability (consistent results), Fairness (unbiased), Practicality (feasible). Example for validity: A math test shouldn't require high reading skills. Balancing these principles is the art of teaching.
2 Explain the role of assessment in teaching and learning process. +
Answer: Assessment serves 4 key functions: (1) Diagnosing – pre-tests identify prior knowledge; (2) Monitoring – formative quizzes guide instruction; (3) Improving instruction – results tell teachers what worked; (4) Evaluating achievement – summative tests assign grades. Assessment FOR learning is more powerful than assessment OF learning.
3 Define educational objectives. Compare original vs revised Bloom's Taxonomy. +
Answer: Educational objectives state what students should be able to do. Original Bloom's (1956): Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Synthesis, Evaluation. Revised (2001): Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create. Key changes: verbs instead of nouns, "Create" as highest order. Importance: ensures tests assess higher-order thinking, not just recall.
4 What is test reliability? Explain three types and factors affecting it. +
Answer: Reliability = consistency of test scores. Three types: Test-Retest (same test twice), Inter-Rater (different scorers agree), Internal Consistency (items measure same construct). Factors affecting reliability: test length (longer = more reliable), item difficulty (too easy/hard reduces spread), scoring objectivity, student conditions (fatigue).
5 Define validity and describe its types with measurement methods. +
Answer: Validity = test measures what it claims. Types: Content validity (expert review using TOS), Criterion-related (correlation with external measure), Construct validity (factor analysis). A test can be reliable but not valid. Example: A reliable watch always shows 7:00 – it's consistent but invalid for telling actual time.
6 Rules for writing high-quality MCQs with examples. +
Answer: Rules: (1) Clear stem presenting one problem; (2) Plausible distractors; (3) Avoid "all/none of above"; (4) Grammatically consistent options; (5) Randomize correct answer position; (6) 3-5 options. Example: Good MCQ – "What is the capital of France?" with distractors Berlin, Madrid, Rome. Poor – "France is a country and its capital is..." with unrelated options.
7 What is Table of Specification (TOS)? Explain its preparation and significance. +
Answer: TOS is a two-way chart mapping content vs cognitive levels. Steps: (1) List topics; (2) Count instructional hours; (3) Calculate percentages; (4) Distribute items; (5) Allocate across Bloom's levels. Significance: Ensures content validity – test accurately samples the domain. Prevents over-testing easy topics.
8 Differentiate objective vs subjective tests with advantages/disadvantages. +
Answer: Objective tests (MCQs, true/false) – machine-scorable, high reliability, but encourage recognition. Subjective tests (essays) – assess higher-order thinking, but time-consuming to score. Best practice: mix both. Objective for breadth, subjective for depth.
9 Explain NRT vs CRT – purposes, advantages, disadvantages. +
Answer: Norm-Referenced (NRT) compares students to peers (percentile ranks). Used for selection (e.g., university entrance). Criterion-Referenced (CRT) measures mastery of objectives (pass/fail). Example: Driver's license test = CRT (score 80% to pass). University entrance = NRT (top 10% get admission).
10 Differentiate aptitude vs achievement tests. +
Answer: Aptitude tests measure potential/future ability (SAT, IQ tests). Achievement tests measure past/present learning (final exam, unit test). Aptitude predicts performance; achievement documents learning.
11 Role of interview and observation as assessment techniques. +
Answer: Interviews (structured, semi-structured, unstructured) allow probing, clarify understanding, assess oral communication. Limitations: time-consuming, bias, anxiety. Observations (participant/non-participant, structured/unstructured) capture real behavior, minimal disruption. Limitations: observer bias, Hawthorne effect (behavior changes when watched).
12 Role of parent-teacher conferences and key considerations. +
Answer: Parent-teacher conferences share progress, discuss development, build partnerships. Key considerations: prepare evidence, start positive, be specific, listen actively, develop action plan, follow up. Effective conferences transform parents into active partners.

📝 سیکشن A: ایڈوانسڈ HTML اور ملٹی میڈیا

Q1. HTML ٹیبلز – ٹیگز، colspan اور rowspan

جواب: HTML ٹیبلز ڈیٹا کو قطاروں اور کالموں میں ظاہر کرتی ہیں۔ اہم ٹیگز:

  • <table> – کنٹینر
  • <tr> – ٹیبل رو
  • <td> – ڈیٹا سیل
  • <th> – ہیڈر سیل (بولڈ)

colspan اور rowspan: یہ سیلز کو ضم کرتے ہیں۔ colspan="2" سے دو کالم افقی طور پر merge ہوتے ہیں، rowspan="2" سے دو قطاریں عمودی طور پر۔ مثال:

<table border="1">
 <tr><th colspan="2">Merged Header</th></tr>
</table>

Q2. HTML فارمز – ضروری عناصر اور name attribute

جواب: فارم صارف سے ڈیٹا لینے کے لیے ہوتے ہیں۔ اہم ان پٹ ٹائپس: text, password, email, radio, checkbox, select, textarea, submit button۔ name attribute کی اہمیت: یہ کلید کا کام کرتا ہے۔ سرور کو ڈیٹا پہنچانے کے لیے name کے بغیر ویلیو نہیں پہنچ سکتی۔

<input type="text" name="student_name">
<input type="submit" value="بھیجیں">

Q3. ملٹی میڈیا انٹیگریشن – ویڈیو، آڈیو اور فال بیک

جواب: HTML5 نے <video> اور <audio> ٹیگز متعارف کرائے۔ فال بیک (fallback) وہ متن یا Flash ہوتا ہے جو براؤزر نہ چلانے پر دکھایا جائے۔ فارمیٹس میں MP4, WebM, Ogg شامل ہیں۔

<video controls>
 <source src="course.mp4" type="video/mp4">
 آپ کا براؤزر ویڈیو سپورٹ نہیں کرتا۔
</video>

🎨 سیکشن B: Cascading Style Sheets (CSS)

Q4. CSS کی بنیادی باتیں – Inline, Internal, External

جواب: CSS دستاویزات کی پیشکش (presentation) کنٹرول کرتی ہے۔

  • Inline CSS: style attribute کے اندر → خاص element کے لیے، بڑی ویب سائٹ کے لیے مناسب نہیں۔
  • Internal CSS: <style> ٹیگ میں head سیکشن میں → ایک پیج کے لیے بہتر۔
  • External CSS: علیحدہ .css فائل، <link> سے منسلک → بڑی ویب سائٹس کے لیے بہترین (دوبارہ استعمال، آسان مینٹیننس)۔

Q5. CSS باکس ماڈل – Content, Padding, Border, Margin

جواب: ہر عنصر باکس کی شکل میں ہوتا ہے:

  • Content – اصل مواد
  • Padding – content اور border کے درمیان (اندر سے فاصلہ)
  • Border – padding اور content کے گرد لکیر

🌍 1. Introduction to Environmental Science (9407)

Environmental Science is the interdisciplinary study of how natural systems work, how humans interact with the environment, and how we can solve environmental problems. It includes ecology, geology, chemistry, and social sciences.

  • Scope: Natural resource management, pollution control, conservation, environmental policies.
  • Importance: Understanding climate change, protecting biodiversity, sustainable development.

📚 2. Important Topics & Units (AIOU 9407 Syllabus)

  • Unit 1: Fundamentals of Environment – components (lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, biosphere)
  • Unit 2: Ecosystems – types, structure, functions, food chains, ecological pyramids

✍️ 3. Solved Important Questions (Most Repeated)

Q1: Define ecosystem. Explain its components and functions.
Answer: An ecosystem is a community of living organisms interacting with their non-living environment.
Components: Abiotic (sunlight, water, soil, temperature) and Biotic (producers, consumers, decomposers).
Functions: Energy flow, nutrient cycling, productivity, decomposition.

Q2: What are the major causes of water pollution? Suggest control measures.
Answer: Causes – industrial waste, sewage, agricultural runoff, oil spills. Control – wastewater treatment, bioremediation, strict laws, public awareness.

Q3: Write a note on biodiversity and its importance.
Answer: Biodiversity is the variety of life at genetic, species, and ecosystem levels. Importance: ecosystem services (clean air, water), food security, medicine, climate regulation, cultural value.

🌿 4. Ecosystem – Detailed Notes

Types of Ecosystems: Terrestrial (forest, grassland, desert) and Aquatic (freshwater, marine).

  • Food Chain: Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle
  • Food Web: Interconnected food chains showing multiple feeding relationships.
  • Ecological Pyramids: Pyramid of numbers, biomass, energy (always upright except some exceptions).

Energy flow: Sun → Producers (photosynthesis) → Primary consumers → Secondary consumers → Decomposers. Only 10% energy transfers to next trophic level (10% law).

💧 5. Pollution – Air, Water, Soil

Air Pollution

  • Causes: Vehicles, industries, burning fossil fuels, deforestation.
  • Major pollutants: CO, SO₂, NOx, particulate matter (PM2.5), ozone.
  • Effects: Respiratory diseases, acid rain, global warming, smog.
  • Control: Clean fuels, catalytic converters, afforestation, emission standards.

Water Pollution

  • Causes: Industrial waste, untreated sewage, agricultural runoff (fertilizers, pesticides).
  • Effects: Eutrophication, waterborne diseases (cholera, typhoid), aquatic life death.
  • Control: Sewage treatment plants, bioremediation, reduce plastic waste.

Soil Pollution

  • Causes: Excessive chemical fertilizers, pesticides, industrial waste, plastic.
  • Effects: Loss of soil fertility, groundwater contamination, health risks.
  • Control: Composting, organic farming, phytoremediation, proper waste disposal.

🐅 6. Biodiversity & Conservation

Types of Biodiversity: Genetic diversity, Species diversity, Ecosystem diversity.

Major threats (anthropogenic): Habitat loss (deforestation, urbanization), overexploitation (hunting, fishing), climate change, pollution, invasive alien species.

Conservation strategies:

  • In-situ conservation: National parks, wildlife sanctuaries, biosphere reserves.
  • Ex-situ conservation: Zoos, botanical gardens, seed banks, gene banks.
  • International efforts: CITES, CBD (Convention on Biological Diversity).

🌡️ 7. Climate Change & Global Warming

Greenhouse effect: Natural process where greenhouse gases (CO₂, methane, water vapor) trap heat in Earth's atmosphere. Without it, Earth would be too cold.

Global warming: Enhanced greenhouse effect due to human activities (burning fossil fuels, deforestation, agriculture).

Effects: Rising sea levels, extreme weather events, melting glaciers, loss of biodiversity, food insecurity.

Solutions: Renewable energy (solar, wind), afforestation, energy efficiency, international agreements (Paris Agreement, Kyoto Protocol).

📝 Part 2: Important Grammar Questions

1
What is a preposition? Give examples.

Answer: A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. It indicates position, direction, time, or manner.

Examples: in, on, at, under, over, between, among, during, before, after, with, without, for, since, etc.

Usage Examples:

  • The book is on the table.
  • She arrived after the meeting.
  • He walked toward the door.
2
Change the voice: "She will write a letter."
Answer: A letter will be written by her.
Rule: Active to Passive: Object + will be + past participle + by + subject.
3
Change the narration: "He said, 'I am happy.'"
Answer: He said that he was happy.
Rule: Direct to Indirect: Remove commas and quotation marks. Change pronouns and tense accordingly (present to past).
4
Identify the underlined word: "Pakistan is very hot in summer." (Adjective/Adverb/Noun)
Answer: Adjective
Explanation: "Hot" describes the noun "Pakistan", so it is an adjective.
5
Identify the sentence type: "If we wait here, we shall be late."
Answer: Conditional Sentence (First Conditional)
Explanation: First conditional sentences express real and possible situations. Structure: If + present simple, will + infinitive.
