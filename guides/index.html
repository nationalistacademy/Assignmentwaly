Assignment writing and formatting

Before writing, confirm the exact question paper, course code, semester and assignment number. Organise each response with a clear introduction, logically ordered explanation, relevant examples and a concise conclusion. Number answers exactly as instructed. Use readable headings and consistent formatting; include a title page only when required by the course or university instructions.

Do not assume one format applies to every programme. Your current assignment question paper, course tutor and official AIOU instructions take priority.

Academic integrity and original answers

Use course books and credible sources to understand the topic. Write explanations in your own words, quote only when necessary, and identify sources accurately. Do not fabricate citations, copy another student's work or submit generated material as if it were independently researched. Review the university's current academic integrity requirements.

Tutor-marked assignments and submission guidance

Check the official submission route, deadline, file requirements and tutor instructions for your programme and semester. Keep a copy of your final work and any submission confirmation. If the portal, tutor allocation or deadline information conflicts, seek clarification through official AIOU student support rather than relying on an old third-party post.
