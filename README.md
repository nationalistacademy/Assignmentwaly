# Taleemwaly — Assignmentwaly Education & Professional Support

A Pakistan-focused learning and professional-support platform. This repository is being improved in place; existing project files and history are preserved.

## Mission
Help learners find trustworthy educational resources, develop practical skills, understand public legal information, and connect with appropriately qualified professionals when a matter requires regulated advice.

## Service areas

| Area | Planned services | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Education & AIOU support | Study notes, course explainers, assignment-writing guidance, past-paper practice, quizzes, study planners | Learning support only; students remain authors of submitted work |
| Research & thesis skills | Topic exploration, proposal structure, literature-search guidance, citation help, research methods | No fabricated sources/data, ghostwriting, or guaranteed grades |
| Career development | CV feedback, cover-letter guidance, interview practice, job-search resources | No false credentials or guaranteed employment |
| Legal information | Plain-language explainers, public-law links, legal process awareness | General information, not a lawyer-client relationship or case-specific legal advice |
| Advocate referrals | Consultation requests and introductions to independently verified advocates | Advice, representation, and reserved legal work only by eligible advocates |
| Business support | General compliance checklists and referrals for specialist review | No claim of acting as a law firm, accountant, tax agent, or regulator |

## Legal and ethical commitments
- We do not claim that this project or its operators are a law firm or licensed advocates.
- Legal opinions, document advice, representation, and court work must be delivered only by an appropriately entitled advocate whose identity, enrolment, jurisdiction, and engagement are verified.
- Public legal content must cite authoritative sources, show a last-reviewed date, identify jurisdiction, and explain when professional advice is needed.
- Educational services support learning. We do not promise marks, degrees, admissions, or examination outcomes, and we do not produce deceptive work for submission as a student's own.
- We do not knowingly publish pirated books, paid notes, copied articles, fabricated reviews, fake credentials, or misleading testimonials.
- Personal information and legal documents must not be collected until a documented privacy notice, lawful purpose, access controls, retention schedule, and secure handling process are in place.
- No legal or regulatory compliance guarantee is made. Each service must be reviewed against applicable federal and provincial laws and professional rules before launch.

## Contributing
1. Identify the user problem and intended audience.
2. Check copyright, privacy, accessibility, security, and professional boundaries.
3. Use authoritative sources and record review dates for legal content.
4. Avoid collecting sensitive data unless the feature has approved safeguards.
5. Add tests and update documentation where applicable.
