How we publish

Useful before published.

An article is not ready just because the words are written. Its sources, practical companion and quality checks must be ready too.

01

Find a real problem

Research the learner's question and identify what is missing from existing guidance.

02

Build the free companion

Create an useful tool, worksheet, checklist or calculator before the article goes live.

03

Write from verified sources

Explain the topic in original language, cite primary sources and show what is uncertain.

04

Review, then approve

Check facts, usability, accessibility, originality and legal boundaries. A human approved publication.

Our promise
